PartCraft wuxuu furaa qaybo makaanik caadi ah oo diyaar ah: cabbirrada waxay ka yimaadaan miisaska caadiga, cabbir kasta oo miiska ku jirna waa habayn aad u beddeli karto.

## Fur mid

1. Guji {{ui:Qayb caadi ah}} kooxda Fayl.
2. {{ui:Qaybta}} dooro nooca, tusaale {{ui:Qanjaruuf madax lix-geesle (ISO 4017)}}.
3. {{ui:Baaxadda}} dooro baaxadda, tusaale `M10 × 40`.
4. Guji {{ui:Fur}}.

Qaybtu waxay furmaa qayb cusub oo aan la kaydin. Kaydi si aad u hayso ({{key:Mod+S}}).

| Qayb | Caadi | Halbeegyada |
|---|---|---|
| Qanjaruuf madax lix-geesle, boolt lix-geesle | ISO 4017, ISO 4014, ASME B18.2.1 (inch) | `d` baaxadda dhuumada, `s` ballaca dhinac ilaa dhinac, `k` joogga madaxa, `l` dherer |
| Qodxan lix-geesle | ISO 4032, ASME B18.2.2 (inch) | `d`, `s`, `height` |
| Daarad caadi ah | ISO 7089 | `d1` gudaha, `d2` dibada, `h` dhumuc |
| Pin isu-dhigan | ISO 2338 | `d`, `l` |
| Bearing kubbad-wareeg qoto dheer | ISO 15 | `d` godka, `D` dibada, `B` ballaca |
| Fure isu-dhigan | DIN 6885-1, qaabka A | `b` ballaca, `h` joogga, `l` dherer |

## Beddel cabbirka

Cabbir kasta waa habayn (eeg [Habaynada iyo miisaska naqshadaynta](topic:design-tables)): fur {{ui:Halbeegyada}} oo dooro mid kale, ama beddel halbeeg, sida `l` qanjaruuf dheer ah, kadibna ku kaydi habayn aad iska leedahay.

## Waxa la moodeelay

- Qanjaruufyo iyo boolt-yo: madaxa lix-geesle iyo cagaha caadiga ah. Qodxanka: lix-geesle leh god baaxadda dhuumada ee magaca. Dhuumooyinka lama moodeelo; ku dar mid {{ui:Dhuumo}} haddii aad u baahan tahay (eeg [Dhuumooyinka](topic:threads)).
- Pin-yada: silinder caadi ah; chamfer-yada dhamaadka waa la reebay.
- Bearing-yada: dibada keliya (giraan godka, dhexroorka dibadda iyo ballaca), taasoo ah wixii aad u baahan tahay si aad mid u dhigto.
- Furayaasha: fure dhamaadkiisu wareegsan yahay.

Agabka qaybta waa bir, sidaas darteed guddiga cufka wuxuu muujinayaa miisaanka. Gear-ka halkan ma jiraan weli: PartCraft ma lahan astaamo ilkaha, diisku ma aha gear. CadCraft wuxuu gear-ka ku sawiraa heshiiska sawirka, maktabadduna way u hayaan sawiro.
