Furayaasha uma shaqeeyaan intaad ku qorayso sanduuq ku jira panel, marka laga reebo {{key:Mod+S}} iyo {{key:Mod+O}}, kuwaas oo had iyo jeer shaqeeya. Furayaasha xarfaha ah waxay shaqeeyaan iyada oo aan furaha wax kale la isticmaalin.

Macluumaad guud oo ku saabsan furayaasha browser-ka, iyo kiiboodhadda aan lahayn furayaasha function: [Furayaasha, mouse-yada iyo trackpad-yada](common:keys).

## Meel walba

| Fur | Falka |
|---|---|
| {{key:Mod+S}} | Kaydi qaybta (assembly-ga: assembly-ga). Marka hore wuxuu xiraa sawirka furan |
| {{key:Mod+O}} | Fur fayl qayb ama assembly ah (wuxuu marka hore weydiin doonaa haddii isbeddello aan la kaydin ay jiraan) |
| {{key:Mod+Z}} | Ka noqo |
| {{key:Mod+Y}} ama {{key:Mod+Shift+Z}} | Samee mar kale |
| {{key:F1}} | Fur ama xir caawimaadan (sidoo kale {{ui:Help}} ee bar-ka app-ka). {{key:Esc}} ayaa xidha sidoo kale. Laptop-yada badan iyo kiiboodhadda Mac-ka, qabo Fn |
| {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} | Habka shaashadda: caadi, shaashad buuxa, shaashad buuxa oo jiifka laga qariyay. Control ayaa Mac-ka sidoo kale, ma aha ⌘ |
| {{key:Esc}} | Ka bax shaashad buuxa (marka aad shaashad buuxa ku jirto) |
| {{key:Mod+,}} | Dejinta, oo ku jirta tab-ka PartCraft (eeg [Dejinta PartCraft](topic:settings)) |

Sanduuqa lambarka ee panel-ka, {{key:Mod+Z}} waa ka noqoshada qoraalka browser-ka. Sanduuqa lambarka ee ku dul yaal cabbirka sawirka, wuxuu ka noqdaa PartCraft.

## Nuqul, dhaji iyo nuqul labanlaab

Waxay wax ka qabtaan waxa aad ku shaqeyso: geometry-ga la doortay sawir furan, astaanta la doortay taariikhda, ama qaybta la doortay assembly-ga. Faahfaahin ku jirta [Nuqul, dhaji iyo nuqul labanlaab](topic:copy-paste).

| Fur | Falka |
|---|---|
| {{key:Mod+C}} | Koobiyee wax la doortay |
| {{key:Mod+X}} | Jar: koobiyee, kadibna tirtir |
| {{key:Mod+V}} | Dhaji. Sawir ku jira nuqulku wuxuu raacaa mouse-ka: guji meesha uu tagayo |
| {{key:Mod+Shift+V}} | Dhaji meeshiisa: isla isku-xidhka (assembly-ga, isla meeshii) |
| {{key:Mod+D}} | Nuqul labanlaab: koobiyee iyo dhaji hal tallaabo, iyadoo clipboard-ka aan laga taaban |
| {{key:Mod+A}} | Dooro dhammaan (sawir ku jira) |
| {{key:Esc}} | Ma doorato wax (iyo jooji dhajinta ku raacda mouse-ka) |

Sanduuqa lambarka, furayaashan waxay koobiyeeyaan oo dhajiyaan qoraalka sida caadiga ah. Qoraalka la doortay ee panel-ka browser-ka ayaa koobiyeeya.

## Shortcuts-ka browser-ku qaadi karo

- {{key:Mod+Y}} Safari-ga ayaa laga yaabaa inuu furo taariikhda: isticmaal {{key:Mod+Shift+Z}} si aad mar kale u samayso.
- {{key:Mod+D}} waa furaha bookmark-ka browser-ka: PartCraft wuu qaadaa intii app-ku xulan yahay, wuuna iska daayaa markaad sanduuq ku qorayso.
- {{key:F11}} iyo {{key:Mod+Plus}} / {{key:Mod+Minus}} waa shaashad buuxa iyo weynaynta bogga ee browser-ka, ma aha kuwa PartCraft. Isticmaal {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} shaashad buuxa, wheel-ka moodeelka.
- Furayaasha function laptop-yada iyo Mac-yada badan waxay u baahan yihiin furaha Fn: riix Fn+{{key:F1}} caawimaad, ama isticmaal badhanka {{ui:Help}}.

Gacmaha taabashada iyo trackpad-ka: [Trackpad, taabasho iyo shaashado yaryar](topic:trackpad-and-touch).

## Meesha ay Guuri ku taal

Guuri waxay ku taal menu-ga {{ui:More}} ee kooxda Astaanno, sida {{ui:Move body}}, sanduuqa {{ui:Move}} halkaas oo aad ku qorto ΔX, ΔY iyo ΔZ, iyo sida jiifka {{ui:Drag to move}} ee qaybta.
