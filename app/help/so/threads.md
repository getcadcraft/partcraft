{{ui:Thread}} wuxuu dhigaa dhuumo wejiyada wareega: dibadda shaft-ka (dhuumo dibadda) ama gudaha bore-ka (dhuumo gudaha). PartCraft wuxuu ka ogaadaa wejiga.

Dhuumadu wuxuu ku bilaabmaa **cosmetic**: wejigu wuu sii yahay, aragtidu waxay dhuumada ku sawirtaa goobo dashed ah, sawir-xaashiyada ayaa xusa, sida "M10×1.5 - 6g, dhuumo 20 dheer". Calaamadi **Modelled** si aad u gooyso dhuumada helical ee runta ah qaybta, daabacaadda 3D ama moodel u baahan inuu si sax ah u muuqdo.

## Dhuumee shaft ama bore

1. Samee shaft ama bore cabbirka dhuumada: shaft Ø10 ah boolt M10 ah, ama bore Ø8.5 (tap drill) ah nut M10 ah.
2. Guji wejiga wareega, kadibna dooro {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Thread}}. Haddii wejig wareeg aan la dooranin, PartCraft wuxuu kaa weydiinayaa inaad wejiyada gujiso, kadibna {{ui:Done}}.
3. Panelka midig, hubi **Size**. **Fit to the face** wuxuu cabbirka ku doortaa isaga: shaft-ka, cabbirka diameter-keedu weyn yahay sida shaft-ka; bore-ka, cabbirka diameter-keeda yar uu ku habboon yahay.

Dhuumadu wuxuu ka bilaabmaa dhamaadka furan ee wejiga, meesha qaybtu aan ku sii socon jiifka tooska ah: dhamaadka xor ah ee shaft-ka, afka bore-ka. **Start at the other end** ayaa beddelaya taas.

## Dejinta

| Dejinta | Ikhtiyaarada | Caadiga |
|---|---|---|
| **Form** | ISO metric (60°), M1.6 ilaa M64 coarse; trapezoidal (Tr, 30°), Tr8×1.5 ilaa Tr60×9 | ISO metric |
| **Size** | Fit to the face, ama hal cabbir oo liiska ka mid ah | Fit to the face |
| **The whole face** | Ka saar si aad **Length (mm)** u bixiso, oo laga bilaabo bilowga | La calaamadiyay |
| **Start at the other end** | Dhuumadu wuxuu ka socdaa dhamaadka kale ee wejiga | Damiyey |
| **Hand** | Gacanta midig ama gacanta bidix | Gacanta midig |
| **Modelled** | Gooy dhuumada helical ee runta ah | Damiyey |
| **Clearance (mm)** | Wuu muuqdaa markii Modelled la calaamadiyo: intee in le'eg ayey dhuumada shaft-ku yar tahay, iyo intee in le'eg ayey dhuumada bore-ku u weyn tahay, dhinac kasta | 0.1 |

Cabbir aan ku habboonayn wejiga ayaa weli la dhisaa, la gooyey wejiga, panelkuna wuu sheegayaa, sida "Shaft Ø12 mm maaha Ø10 mm ee M10". Dhowr wejood ayaa wadaagi kara hal feature Thread; **Fit to the face** ku jirta, mid kasta cabbir u gaar ah ayuu helaa.

## Dhuumooyinka moodelka ah ee daabacaadda 3D

Dhuumo moodel ah wuxuu raacaa profile-ka aasaasiga ah ee caadiga ah: ISO 68-1 metric (dhabbo iyo xidid toosan, dhinacyada 60°), ISO 2901 trapezoidal (dhinacyada 30°). **Clearance** wuxuu dhaqaajiyaa profile-ka oo dhan gudaha (shaft) ama dibadda (bore) si qaybaha la daabacay ay isku qabsadaan:

- **0.1 ilaa 0.2 mm** wuxuu ku habboon yahay printer-yada badan. Marka hore daabac tijaabo bolt iyo nut ah.
- **0** waa profile-ka caadiga ah, qaybaha la shiinay ama la hubinayo fit-ka.

Bolt iyo nut oo leh cidhiidhi isku mid ah weligood ma taabtaan dhinacyadooda: farqiga u dhexeeya waa laba jeer cidhiidhiga, dhinac kasta.

:::tip
Pitch-yada yar si xun ayey u daabacmaan: M3 iyo ka yar, ama pitch ka hooseeya 0.5 mm, waa ku dhow waxa nozzle 0.4 mm ku dhajin karo. Qaybaha waaweyn moodel, yaryarna isticmaal heat-set insert ama godka dhuumaysan.
:::

Dhuumo moodel ah waa qayb ka mid ah solid-ka: STL, 3MF iyo STEP exports way qaadaan, Measure wuu cabbiraa. Waxay qaybta ka dhigaysaa mid miisaan badan oo dib loo dhiso: dhuumo M10 oo 20 dheer wuxuu qiyaastii yahay 150,000 triangles.

## Godka dhuumaysan

God **Tapped (ISO metric)** wuxuu leeyahay **Modelled** switch oo isku mid ah, iyo **Clearance** isku mid ah. Eeg [Godaha iyo counterbores-ka ee dhibcaha sawir-gacmeedka](topic:holes).

## Marka dhuumadu aan dhisin

- "Dhuumo wuxuu ku yimaadaa wejiga wareega (silinda): shaft ama bore." Wejiga la doortay waa toos ama si kale ayuu u qalloocan yahay.
- "Cabbir ISO ah oo aan ku habboonayn wejiga wareega ee Ø0.6 mm: dooro cabbir." Fit to the face ma helin midna 12 % dhexdeeda. Dooro **Size**, ama beddel wejiga.
- "Wejigii dhuumadu ku saabsanaa wuu maqan yahay (…). Mar kale dooro." Isbeddel hore ayaa wejiga ka saaray. Guji {{ui:Pick faces}} oo mar kale guji wejiga.

:::note
Dhuumooyinka inch-ka (UNC, UNF), dhuumooyinka tuubada, dhuumooyinka tapered, dhamaadka dhuumada (run-outs) iyo bilowga dhuumada ee chamfer-ka weli lama dhisin. Exact kernel wuxuu u muujiyaa dhuumo moodel ah sida cosmetic.
:::
