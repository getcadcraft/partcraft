Isticmaal {{ui:More}} ee toolbar-ka Features › {{ui:Magnet study of sketches…}} qayb leh sawirro. Jidh kasta oo xidhan oo ku jira sawirrada aad calaamaysay waa hal gobol oo moodeel fidsan; daraasaddu wuxuu xisaabiyaa goobta magnetic-ka B, xoogga iyo torque-ka jidhka aad doorato.

## Tallaabooyinka

1. Jidhadka u rawso sida shakloo xidhan oo sawirrada ku jira: yoke bir ah, magnet, coil. Jidhadka isku taabtaa waxay wadaagaan geeso, sidaas darteed ku dhig sawiro kala duwan; jidhadka kala fog, ama kuwa ku jira midba kan kale (daaqad ku jirta yoke), waxay wadaagi karaan mid. Sawirrada ku hay isla diyaarad.
2. Fur daraasadda oo calaamee sawirrada la isticmaali doono. Mid kasta u sheeg waxa uu yahay: {{ui:Air}}, {{ui:Iron}} (permeability, ama B–H curve oo lagu qoro lammaano B tesla iyo H A/m), {{ui:Permanent magnet}} (Br tesla, jihadiisa xagal ka bilaabata x axis-ka sawirka, iyo recoil permeability-kiisa) ama {{ui:Coil}} (xaddiga wadarta ah ee shidaalka profile-kiisa, turns × amperes; calaamaddu waxay sheegaysaa jihada uu socdo).
3. Calaamee {{ui:Force and torque on this body}} jidhadka aad rabto inay leeyihiin. Bixi qoto dheerida qaybta ee sawirka ka baxda.
4. Dooro mesh-ka iyo hawada ku wareegsan moodeelka, kadibna {{ui:Run}}.

## Waxa aad arki doontid

- Dhufashada goobta |B| ee moodeelka, buluug qoto dheer oo hoose ilaa casaan oo sare, profile-yadana lambar la siiyay.
- Goobta ugu xoogga badan iyo meesha ay ku taal, tirada triangle-yada, iyo jidh kasta aad calaamaysay: xoogga x iyo y iyo torque-ka bartamaheeda, iyadoo loo eegayo qoto dheerida aad bixisay.
- {{ui:Save results (CSV)}} wuxuu qoraa tirooyinka.

## Waxa wanaagsan in la yaqaan

- Waa moodeel fidsan oo joogto ah: wax kasta waxaa loo qiyaasay mitir qoto dheer, iyadoo aan lahayn eddy currents iyo hysteresis, magnetku waana khad recoil toosan (ma dumin karo).
- Moodeelku wuxuu ku jiraa sanduuq hawo ah oo geeskiisu yahay derbi. Isbarbar dhig labo cabbir oo sanduuqa hawada ah, iyo mesh-yada Normal iyo Fine: xoog laga qaatay goobta ku wareegsan jidh waxa uu ka wanaagsan yahay mesh-ka halkaas oo keliya.
- Bir oo leh permeability keliya marna ma buuxo. Goobtu markay 2 T gudubto, daraasaddu waxay dhigaysaa ⚠ liiska Problems: siiyo birta B–H curve. Tirooyinka safka cusub waa tusaalooyin (bir μr 2000, magnet 1.2 T), ma aha xogta warqadda shirkadda.
- Jidhku wuxuu u baahan yahay meel ku wareegsan si uu xoogga u qaato: haddii jidh kale aad ugu dhow yahay, daraasaddu way sheegaysaa.
- Qalab hagid ah, ma aha ansixin injineernimo.
