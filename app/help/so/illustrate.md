**Illustrate** waa workspace-ka dukumeentiyada loo baahan yahay naqshadda ku wareegsan: muuqaalo kala firdhisan oo leh baloonyo iyo liis qaybaha, sawirro xarriiq-fan ah oo buugaagta loogu talagalay, tilmaamo tallaabo-tallaabo ah, dhaqdhaqaaq, iyo fayl 3D oo macmiilku furi karo iyada oo aan CAD lahayn. Dooro {{ui:Illustrate}} gudaha beddelka {{ui:Workspace}} ee bilowga toolbar-ka.

**Buugga** waa fayl gaar ah (`<name>.partcraft-manual.json`). Wuxuu hayaa nuqul qaabka qayb kasta, geedka alaabta iyo sifooyinka qaybaha, iyo xidhiidh dib ugu celinaya meesha ay ka yimaadeen, si aad buugga uga shaqayn karto intii naqshaddu sii socoto. Marka naqshaddu isbedesho, [Cusboonaysii buugga naqshadda](topic:illustrate-update) ayaa isbeddelada soo geliya.

## Bilow mid

Guji {{ui:New}} gudaha kooxda {{ui:Manual}}, kadibna:

- {{ui:From the open part}}: qaybta furan ee Naqshadda.
- {{ui:From the open assembly}}: wuxuu soo bixiyaa halkii uu muuqan lahaa marka isku-dhis furan yahay; qaybahiisa, meelahooda, lambarrada qaybaha, agabka iyo muuqaalada kala firdhisan ayaa la soo qaadaa.
- {{ui:From an assembly file…}}: fayl `.partcraft-asm.json` oo la kaydiyay; PartCraft marka hore qaybahiisa ayuu dhisaa.
- {{ui:From another file…}}: glTF (.glb, .gltf), OBJ, STL ama 3MF. Geedka faylka iyo magacyada ayaa la hayaa, nuqullada hal qayb (isla qaabka oo laba jeer la dhigay) waxay noqdaan hal qayb oo tiro leh. Faylasha STL iyo OBJ ma sheegaan unitkooda, sidaas darteed waa la weydiin doonaa. Faylasha STL waxa lagu akhriyaa Z sare; glTF iyo 3MF waxay sheegaan kuwooda.

Inta buugga furan yahay, Illustrate wuxuu muujiyaa nuqulka buugga laftiisa ee qaybaha; qaybta ama isku-dhiska furan ee Naqshadda waa la qariyaa ilaa aad ku noqoto Naqshadda.

## Kaydi iyo fur

{{ui:Save}} gudaha kooxda {{ui:Manual}} (ama {{key:Mod+S}}) wuxuu kaydiyaa buugga; {{ui:Open}} wuxuu furaa mid. Isbeddel kasta oo Illustrate ah waa hal tallaabo oo taariikhda buugga laftiisa: {{ui:Undo}} iyo {{ui:Redo}} waxay saameeyaan buugga inta Illustrate uu furan yahay.

## Wali lama dhisin

- Faylasha STEP ma furmaan {{ui:From another file…}} wali. Fur STEP-ka Naqshadda (wuxuu u yimaadaa sida jidh adag; eeg [Fur fayl STEP](topic:import-step)), kadibna bilow buugga {{ui:From the open part}}, ama u kaydi glTF, 3MF, OBJ ama STL.
- ModelCraft iyo BimCraft weli ma geyn karaan moodeel toos ah Illustrate: u kaydi glTF (.glb) oo isticmaal {{ui:From another file…}}.
