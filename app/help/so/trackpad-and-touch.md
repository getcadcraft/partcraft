PartCraft waxaa loo sameeyay mouse leh wheel iyo badhanka dhexe, laakiin trackpad-ku si fiican ayuu u shaqeeyaa marka aad garanayso gestures-ka; shaashadda taabashada waxay u shaqeysaa aragtida qaybaha iyo sawirka fudud.

## Trackpad

:::mac
| Si aad u | Samee tan |
|---|---|
| Wareeji qaybta (3D) | Riix oo jiid hal farood (ama jiid saddex farood haddii aad awood u leedahay jiidista saddex-farood) |
| Pan (3D) | Hay {{key:Shift}} (ama ⌘) oo riix-jiid, ama riix-jiid labo farood la riixay (jiidista click-ka labaad) |
| Wareeji qaybta, swipe | Swipe labo farood; {{key:Shift}} ku, swipe-ku wuu pan-yaa. Waxay u baahan tahay **Scrolling** oo loo dejiyay "Work it out" (bilowga) ama "Trackpad" |
| Zoom (3D iyo sketch) | Pinch. Zoom wuxuu u socdaa xagga pointer-ka. Haddii **Scrolling** loo dejiyay "Mouse wheel", labo farood scroll waa zoom |
| Right-click | Riix labo farood, ama {{key:Ctrl}}+click |
| Pan sketch-ka | Swipe labo farood ("Work it out" ama "Trackpad"), ama riix-jiid labo farood la riixay. {{key:Shift}}+jiid kuma pan-yo sketch-ka (wuxuu sameeyaa daaqada xulashada) |
| Ku dar xulashada sketch-ka | {{key:Shift}}+click ama ⌘+click |

Shid secondary click ee **System Settings, Trackpad, Point & Click** haddii riix labo farood aanu wax samayn.
:::

:::windows linux
| Si aad u | Samee tan |
|---|---|
| Wareeji qaybta (3D) | Riix touchpad-ka (ama taabo oo hay) oo jiid |
| Pan (3D) | Hay {{key:Shift}} ama {{key:Ctrl}} oo jiid, ama jiid iyadoo dhinaca badhanka midig la riixay |
| Wareeji qaybta, swipe | Swipe labo farood; {{key:Shift}} ku, swipe-ku wuu pan-yaa. Waxay u baahan tahay **Scrolling** oo loo dejiyay "Work it out" (bilowga) ama "Trackpad" |
| Zoom (3D iyo sketch) | Pinch meesha touchpad-ku taageero. Zoom wuxuu u socdaa xagga pointer-ka. Haddii **Scrolling** loo dejiyay "Mouse wheel", labo farood scroll waa zoom |
| Right-click | Taabo labo farood, ama geeska hoose ee midig ee touchpad-ka |
| Pan sketch-ka | Swipe labo farood ("Work it out" ama "Trackpad"), ama jiid iyadoo dhinaca badhanka midig la riixay. {{key:Shift}}+jiid kuma pan-yo sketch-ka |
| Ku dar xulashada sketch-ka | {{key:Shift}}+click ama {{key:Ctrl}}+click |
:::

**Scrolling** waa ku jirta [dejinta PartCraft](topic:settings): "Work it out" (bilowga) wuxuu u qaadaa scrolling-ka dhinacyada ama kan fine-grained ah sida trackpad, "Trackpad" had iyo jeer wuu sidaas yahay, "Mouse wheel" weligiis ma aha. Pinch wuxuu zoom-garaynayaa saddexdaba.

Furayaasha fallaadhaha ayaa wareejiya aragtida 3D, {{key:Shift}} oo leh furayaasha fallaadhaha wuu pan-yaa, marka aad riixdo aragtida: waa wax wanaagsan trackpad-ka. {{key:Plus}} iyo {{key:Minus}} waa zoom-garaynayaan.

## Shaashadaha taabashada

PartCraft wuxuu akhriyaa taabashada, marka tablet-yada, laptop-yada taabashada leh iyo telefoonada way shaqeeyaan, xadadaan leh.

:::touch
**Furayaasha taabashada**

Marka aad taabato shaashadda, bar yar ayaa soo muuqda hoosta midig ee aragtida oo furayaasha ay farsamada yaraanayso. Wuu baxaa markaad isticmaasho mouse.

- {{ui:Esc}}: furaha Esc. Wuxuu joojiyaa wax aad bilowday ama wuu dib u qaadaa, siday furuhu sameeyaan.
- {{ui:Add}} (sketch-ka dhexdiisa, oo leh Select): markuu shidan yahay, taabo kasta wuxuu ku daraa shay xulashada, ama wuu ka saaraa, sida {{key:Shift}}+click. Mar kale taabo si aad ugu noqoto shay mid kasta hal mar.
- {{ui:Done}} (markaad xariiq silsilad, spline ama polygon ah sawirayso): wuu dhammeeyaa, sida right-click.

Gacanta oo aad hakad u haysay qiyaastii nus ilbiriqsi wuxuu samaynayaa isla sidaas taabo keli ah: qalab sawir leh wuxuu dhammeeyaa wixii aad sawirtay, Select-ka wuxuu ku daraa ama ka saaraa shayga ka hooseeya gacantaada xulashada. Dhibcaha iyo xariiqyada way fudud yihiin in gacanta lagu taabto: PartCraft wuxuu eegaa qiyaastii laba jeer ka fog taabashada marka loo eego riix.

**Aragtida 3D**

- Hal farood: wareeji qaybta.
- Labo farood: pinch si aad u zoom-garayso, isku-wada dhaqaaq si aad u pan-garayso.
- Taabo: dooro wejig, geesi ama diyaarad. Ma jiro hover highlight ka hor taabashada.

**Sketch-ka**

- Taabo qalab sawir leh si aad u dhigto dhibic kasta: taabo bilowga, kadibna taabo dhamaadka. Snapping wuxuu dhacaa meesha aad gacanta ka qaaddo.
- Labo farood pinch iyo pan. Farood labaad oo la dhigo waxay joojisaa jiidista farooda kowaad ee bilowday.
- Si aad u dhammeeyso silsiladda xariiqa, taabo {{ui:Done}}, hakad gacanta, ama taabo {{ui:Line}} mar kale (ama qalab kale) toolbar-ka.
- Select: taabo si aad u dooratid; jiid dhibic ama xariiq si aad u dhaqaajiso; jiid meel bannaan si aad u samayso daaqad. Taabo labaad wuxuu beddelaa xulashada inta aan {{ui:Add}} shidnayn (ama haddii gacanta hakad ku hayso shayga labaad).
- Si aad u beddesho cabbir, isticmaal **Wax ka beddel** ee u dhow panelka midig.

**Sawir-xaashiyada**

Taabo si aad u dhigto oo aad u doorato calaamadaha, oo jiid calaamad si aad u dhaqaajiso. Pinch wuu zoom-garaynayaa, labo farood wuu pan-garaynayaa, ama jiid hal farood warqadda bannaan. Farood labaad wuxuu joojiyaa calaamad aad jiidaysay, waana ay dib u noqotaa.

**Waxa weli u baahan keyboard**

- Qorista lambarada: keyboard-ka shaashadda ayaa soo muuqda sanduuqyada lambarka iyo meelaha cabbirka.
- Furayaasha gaagaaban ee qalabka: isticmaal toolbar-ka.
:::

## Shaashadaha yaryar iyo telefoonada

- 1280 pixel ama ballaadhka, toolbar-ku wuxuu ku jiraa hal safka.
- Ilaa 760 ilaa 1100 pixel, panelada dhinacyada way cidhiidhaan.
- Ka hooseeya 760 pixel (telefoon, ama daaqad cidhiidh), shaashaddu waa la kala saaraa: toolbar (swipe sideways si aad u hesho badhanno dheeraad ah), aragtida 3D, status bar, kadibna properties panel iyo history. Scroll bogga si aad u gaadho panelada.
- Menus-ka ({{ui:Export}}, {{ui:Views}}) waxay furmaan ballaadhka shaashadda oo dhan.

:::note
Iphone-ka ma leh mood buuxa oo browser-ka ah; {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} iyo badhanka mood-ka shaashadda waxay u beddelaan oo kaliya caadi iyo bararka la qariyey.
:::

Telefoonku waa fiican yahay si aad u furto qayb, aad u rogto oo aad u hubiso xaqiiqooyinkeeda. Moodeel dhab ah wuxuu u baahan yahay shaashad weyn iyo mouse ama trackpad.

## Browser-ada

Chrome, Edge, Firefox iyo Safari ee hadda jira waxay ku shaqeeyaan PartCraft Windows, macOS iyo Linux, iyo tablet-yada iyo telefoonada, ilaa WebGL iyo WebAssembly ay jiraan. Eeg [Browser-ada iyo kombiyuutarrada](common:browsers).
