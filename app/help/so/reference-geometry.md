Geometry-ga tixraaca waa shaqsashada aad astaamaha ku dhistid: **dusha** aad ku sawir-gacmeyso meel aan wejhi jirin, **dhidib** aad ku revolve ama ku pattern-tid, **bar** aad ku joojiso extrude. Ma leh walxo gaar ah. Dushaha aragga waxay u muuqdaan sida labajibaaqyo cadcad, dhidibyada sida xarriiqyo jeexjeexan, barta sida calaamado jeex ah; kulligood waxay ku biiraan taariikhda, waxayna raacaan waxa laga sameeyay markaad beddesho cabbirka hore.

Waxay ku jiraan menu-ga {{ui:More}} ee kooxda Features: {{ui:Plane}}, {{ui:Axis}}, {{ui:Point}}.

## Samee dusha

1. Guji wejiga siman, ama dusha aasaasiga ah taariikhda, si aad uga bilowdo (ikhtiyaari).
2. Dooro {{ui:More}}, kadibna {{ui:Plane}}. Waxay ku bilaabmaysaa sida dusha ka fogaatay 20 % cabbirka qaybta meesha aad doorashay (ama dusha sare).
3. Panelka midig dooro **Type** iyo tixraacyadeeda:

| Nooca | Tixraacyada | Lambarada |
|---|---|---|
| Ka fogaanta dusha ama wejiga | **Laga bilaabo dusha** | **Fogaanta (mm)**, togan haddii ay tahay dhinaca kale |
| Xagal ilaa geesta ama dhidibka | **Laga bilaabo dusha**, **Ku wareeg** (dhidib adduunka, astaam dhidib, ama geesta toosan ee la doortay) | **Xagal (°)** |
| Saddex bar ku dhex maro | **Barta 1**, **Barta 2**, **Barta 3** | — |
| Dhexe ka dhexeeya laba | **Dusha 1aad**, **Dusha 2aad** (isbarbar ahaan: badhka dhexe; isgoyska: dusha xagasha labadood u qaybiya) | — |
| Taabasho silinda | **Silinda** (wejiga silindarka), **Barta u dhow** (dhinaca) | — |
| Jajab geesta, barta | **Geesta**, **Barta** (dusha waxay mariyaa barta geesta ee u dhow, iyo barta sidoo kale) | — |
| Geesta iyo bar ku dhex maro | **Geesta toosan** (ama dhidib), **Barta ku dhex maro** | — |
| Jajab geesta badhkeeda dhexe | **Geesta** (badhka dhexe, jajab geesta halkaas), ama geesla la'aan **Barta 1** iyo **Barta 2** (dusha ka dhexeeya) | — |

Tixraac kasta wuxuu leeyahay badhanka **Dooro**: riix, kadibna guji aragga. Slot-ka dushu wuxuu qaadanayaa dusha aasaasiga ah, dusha tixraaca ama wejiga siman; slot-ka barta wuxuu qaadanayaa dhamaadka geesta (guji meel ku dhow dhamaadkaas), astaamaha barta ama meel wejiga ku taal; slot-ka geesta wuxuu qaadanayaa geesta. {{key:Esc}} wuu joojiyaa dooritaanka. **Rog jihada** wuxuu rogaa dusha (sawir-gacmeedka ku taal wuxuu u jeedaa dhinaca kale).

Nooca hadda, slot-ka u baahan geesta ama wejiga silindarka (Taabasho silinda iyo slot-yada Geesta) wuxuu leeyahay "weli lama dhisin": isticmaal barar ama dhidib halkeeda.

## Sawir-gacmeed dusha ku sam

Dooro dusha (taariikhda ama guji murabbaca aragga) oo dooro {{ui:Sketch}}, ama **Naqshad ku sam** panelkiisa. Sawir-gacmeedku wuxuu raacaa dusha: beddel cabbirka dusha iyo sawir-gacmeedka, iyo astaam kasta oo ku yaal, ayaa sidoo kale dhaqaaqa.

## Dhidibyo iyo barar

- {{ui:Axis}}: laba bar ku dhex maro, dhidibka silinda ama koonka, meesha laba dusha isgoyaan, geesta toosan ku dhex (ama dhidibka geesta wareega), ama jajab wejiga ku taal barta ku dhex maro.
- {{ui:Point}}: ku meel-yaal, bar sawir-gacmeed ama geesta, ama dhexe geesta wareega, iyo barar "ku dhex dhexaad" (between objects):

| Nooca barta | Tixraacyada |
|---|---|
| Dhexe geesta (ama ku dhex laba bar) | **Geesta** (badhka dhererka, sidoo kale qalloocyada iyo jeexyada), ama geesla la'aan **Barta 1** iyo **Barta 2** |
| Halka geesta ama dhidibku wejiga ama dusha kula kulmo | **Geesta** (toosan ama qalloocan) ama dhidib, iyo **Meets plane** (dusha ama wejiga siman) ama **Or a face** (wejiga siman ama silinda). Goobo dusha waa laba jeer lagu gudbaa, xarriiqna silinda waa laba jeer lagu gudbaa: **The other crossing** wuxuu qaadaa kii labaad |
| Halka saddex dusha ay kulmaan | **First**, **Second** iyo **Third plane** (dusha ama wejiga siman) |

Mid kasta wuxuu fashilmaa fariin fudud marka tixraacyadiisu waafaqin: xarriiq la dhigan dushaha, xarriiq ka maqan silinda, geesta aan gaarin dusha, saddex dusha oo aan ku kulmin hal bar, ama bar xarriiqa saaran oo ay tahay inuu dusha ku hagaajiyo.

Ku isticmaal shaqooyinka kale:

| Meesha | Sidee |
|---|---|
| Revolve | **Axis**: dooro astaamaha dhidibka liiska ka (waa inuu ku jiraa dusha sawir-gacmeedka) |
| Circular pattern | **Axis**: astaamaha dhidibka |
| Linear pattern | **Direction**: astaamaha dhidibka, ama dusha (caadiga) |
| Mirror | **Mirror plane**: dusha tixraaca |
| Extrude | **End**: Ilaa wejiga ama dusha, Ilaa geeska (astaamaha barta). Nooca hadda weli lama dhisin (waxay u baahan tahay kernel sax ah) |
| Draft | **Neutral plane**: dusha dhexe |

## Marka tixraac baabi'o

Dusha laga sameeyay wejiga aan jirin mar dambe wuxuu fashilmaa fariin magacaabaysa, sida "Wejiga uu saaran yahay ayaa ka maqan Extrude1." Weligeed lama wareejiyo wejiga kale. Sawir-gacmeedyada iyo astaamaha ku yaal waxay calaamadsan yihiin "aan la dhisin" ilaa aad tixraac cusub dooratid; qaybta intiisa kale way sii jirtaa sidii ay ahayd.

:::note
Nooca hadda qayb kasta wuxuu ku dhisaa kernel-ka mesh, kaas oo dusha, dhidibyada iyo baraarka ka sameeya dushaha aasaasiga ah, wejiyada siman ee Extrude ama Revolve sameeyey, barar sawir-gacmeed, kaliya-jiifyada iyo astaamaha tixraaca kale. Wax u baahan geesta, dhamaadka geesta ama wejiga qalloocan waxay u baahan yihiin kernel sax ah: weli lama dhisin nooca hadda ("It uses a face or edge of the solid, which only an exact kernel can find: not built yet in this version.").
:::
