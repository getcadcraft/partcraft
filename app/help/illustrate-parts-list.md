The {{ui:Parts list}} section lists the parts: one row per part with its quantity (copies of one part, and parts with the same part number and shape, are one row). {{ui:List}} chooses the parts shown in this view (not hidden, not ghosted) or every part.

## Balloons

{{ui:Balloon the shown parts}} gives each row a number (the next free one) and puts one balloon on the copy nearest you, on a ring round the model. Drag a balloon to move it. A number stays with its part: when parts are added later they get new numbers, and a number is never given to another part, even after its part is removed.

## In the view, and as CSV

Tick {{ui:Show the parts list in this view}} to place the table in the picture (drag it to a corner). Choose its {{ui:Columns}} (the part's own properties are offered too) and whether it lists the parts of this view or every part. {{ui:Save as CSV}} writes the list for a spreadsheet; text that starts like a formula is written so a spreadsheet does not run it.
