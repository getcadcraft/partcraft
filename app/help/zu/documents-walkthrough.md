## Okuzoyenza

UnguAna, usebenza ku-Riverside Clinic nomsebenzisani wakho uBen. Izingxenye nezihlanganisela zephrojekthi zihlala efolda eyodwa enifinyelela kuyo nobabili (idrayivu ehlanganyelwe, ifolda ehlanganisiwe, noma ifolda yefu). Ekugcineni, ifayela eliyodwa liye livela ekukopisheni kwalo kokuqala laya ekuguqulweni okukhululiwe **A**, base lishintshwa futhi njenge-**B.1**, ubone ukuthi ubani wenze yini, futhi ubone ukuthi uBen ubona yini uma ubamba leli fayela.

Konke kugcinwa ku-folda efihliwe ethi `.ccvault` eseduze kwamafayela akho: alukho iseva, akulayishwa lutho (ifolda yefu iyisiphambano, bheka isinyathelo sokugcina). Ukuze uthole wonke amacala nemithetho endaweni eyodwa, bheka isihloko *Documents: check out, revisions and release* lapho uhlelo lwakho lunaso.

## 1. Lungiselela ifolda yephrojekthi

1. Khetha {{ui:Amadokhumenti…}} eqenjini **Ifayela** ebhalweni lamathuluzi.
2. Khetha {{ui:Khetha ifolda yephrojekthi}} bese ukhetha ifolda equkethe izingxenye nezihlanganisela zephrojekthi. (Amafolda ephrojekthi asebenza ku-Studio nasebhulawuzeni angavula ifolda, njenge-Chrome ne-Edge. Kwamanye amabhulawuza iphaneli iyakutshela lokho.)
3. Khetha {{ui:Shintsha}} eduze kwe-**Wena:** bese uthayipha igama lakho, `Ana` (uma kubuza, negama lalo kompyutha). Iphaneli manje ithi *Wena: Ana on* nekhompyutha yakho. Igama lakho lifakwa kukho konke okwenzayo.
4. Khetha {{ui:Qala isitoreji kule folda}} bese uthayipha igama lephrojekthi, `Riverside clinic`. Iphaneli ithi *Isitoreji siqalisiwe ku-Riverside clinic.*

## 2. Lethe ifayela ngaphansi kolawulo

1. Vula **Amafayela angekho esitoreji** bese ukhetha {{ui:Engeza}} eduze kwe-`plates/Bracket.partcraft.json`.
2. Liyavela ngaphansi kwe-**Amafayela esitoreji** ngokuguqulwa **A.1**, nesimo **Umsebenzi uyaqhubeka**. A.1 iyikhophi layo lokuqala; isitoreji sigcina ikhophi lokuguqulwa ngakunye.

## 3. Phuma, shintsha, ngenisa

1. Khetha {{ui:Vula}} eduze kwe-`plates/Bracket.partcraft.json`. Ingxenye ivulwa ku-PartCraft futhi iphaneli iyavaleka ukuze ubone. Vula iphaneli futhi kusuka ku-{{ui:Amadokhumenti…}}.
2. Vula futhi iphaneli bese ukhetha {{ui:Phuma}}. Umugqa manje uthi **Iphumele kuwe**. Kudalwe ifayela lokukhiya ku-`.ccvault/locks`; akekho omunye ongaphuma ifayela le kuze kube yilapho ulingenisa.
3. Shintsha ingxenye: hlela ubude, engeza umgodi. Ukulondoloza kuyenqatshwa ngaphandle kokuthi ifayela liphumele kuwe, futhi kutshela ukuthi kungani; {{ui:Londoloza njenge}} isebenza njalo ngekhophi yakho.
4. Vula iphaneli, khetha {{ui:Ngenisa}} bese uthayipha okushintshile (`Moved the door`). Iphaneli ithi *ingenisiwe njenge-A.2*. Ifayela ku-folda yephrojekthi manje yingxenye oyivulile, nokukhiya kususiwe. Ukungenisa ngaphandle kokushintsha kugcina ukuguqulwa okufanayo.

Uma ushintsha umqondo, {{ui:Hlehlisa ukuphuma}} ibuyisela ifayela ngaphandle kokuguqulwa okusha.

## 4. Buka bese ukhulula

1. Khetha {{ui:Thumela ukuze ibukwe}}. Isimo sishintshela ku-**Ukuze ibukwe** futhi ifayela lifundeka kuphela.
2. Ngaphambi kokuvuma, khetha {{ui:Izilungiselelo zephrojekthi}} bese ubhala ukuthi ubani angavuma (igama elilodwa ngomugqa ngamunye), isibonelo `Ben` no `Carla`, bese uthikha umthetho wokuthi umvumi kufanele abe omunye umuntu hhayi umbhali. Khetha {{ui:Londoloza izilungiselelo}}. Manje isitoreji ngokwaso siyavimbela omunye.
3. U-Ben (noma u-Carla) khetha {{ui:Vuma}} bese uthayipha igama lomvumi. Isimo sishintshela ku-**Ikhuliwe** nokuguqulwa kube **A**, nomugqa othi *Igunyazwe ngu-* negama. Ifayela elikhuliwe lifundeka kuphela. {{ui:Buyisela}} bekuyibuyisela ku-**Umsebenzi uyaqhubeka**, nesizathu.

Igama lomvumi ngelombhalo kuphaneli; liwukurekhoda ukuthi ubani usayine, hhayi ubufakazi bokuthi ungubani.

## 5. Ukuguqulwa okusha

1. Kufayela elikhuliwe, khetha {{ui:Inguqulo entsha}}. Uyilondoloza ukuze uthole inhlamvu elandelayo.
2. Yishintshe, bese {{ui:Ngenisa}}. Ukuguqulwa kuba **B.1**, umsebenzi uyaqhubeka. Yikhulule ngendlela efanayo futhi iba **B**. {{ui:Hlehlisa ukuphuma}} ngaphambi kokungenisa kokuqala kusishiya ku-**A**.

Izinhlamvu zokuguqulwa zilandelana A, B … Z, AA, AB. Uma izinga lakho lishiya ezinye izinhlamvu, izilungiselelo zephrojekthi *Sebenzisa ukulandelana kwe-ASME Y14.35* zidlula I, O, Q, S, X kanye no-Z kokuguqulwa okulandelayo.

## 6. Bona okwenzekile

Khetha igama lefayela ukuvula imininingwane yalo:

- **{{ui:Umlando}}**: yonke imicimbi, eyisha kuqala, nokuthi ubani, iyiphi ikhompyutha nesikhathi: *Ingenisiwe A.2* ngu-Ana, *Ikhuliwe* ngu-Ben, njll.
- **{{ui:Ukuguqulwa}}**: ithebula lokuguqulwa: wonke amakhophi okuguqulwa agciniwe, nedethi yawo, ubani wawungenisa, inothi, nokuthi ubani wawuvuma. Isitoreji sigcina ikhophi ngayinye ku-`.ccvault/revs`.
- **{{ui:Kusetshenziswa}}**: izingxenye zehlanganisela.
- **{{ui:Kusetshenziswa ngu}}**: uhlu lwawo wonke amahlanganisela esitoreji aqukethe le ngxenye: uma ushintsha isibambi, uyazi ukuthi yiziphi izinhlanganisela ezifanele ukuhlolwa.

## Amahlanganisela: okusetshenziswayo nohlu lwezinto

Faka ihlanganisela (`.partcraft.json`, elondolozwe kusuka kumodi yeInhlanganisela) efolda bese uyengeza. Khetha igama layo: **Kusetshenziswa** lohlu izingxenye eziqukethe, nokuguqulwa nesimo sengxenye ngayinye; **Kusetshenziswa ngu** kwingxenye ohlu wonke amahlanganisela eliqukethe. {{ui:Uhlu lwezinto (CSV)}} longa uhlu lwazo zonke izingxenye, izinga ngezinga, nobuningi, izinguqulo nezimo, ukuze kusetshenziswe kuspredishithi. Shintsha isibambi, bese **Kusetshenziswa ngu** ikutshela amahlanganisela okufanele ubheke.

## 7. Okubona uBen uma ubamba ifayela

Manje phuma ifayela futhi, bese ucela uBen ukuthi avule ifolda efanayo yephrojekthi ekhompyutheni yakhe bese evula iphaneli. Esihlahleni sefayela ubona **Iphumelwe ngu-Ana kukhompyutha yakho kusukela** isikhathi, futhi akabonanga inkinobho ethi {{ui:Phuma}}. Uma ezama noma kunjalo isitoreji siyenqaba: ukukhiya kuyifayela uhlelo olungeke lilenze kabili, ngakho abantu ababili abakwazi ukubamba ifayela ngesikhathi esifanayo, nangokushesha okufanayo, uma ifolda isendisikhi ku-Studio. (Kusibhulawuza ifolda inikeza okungenzeka kuphela, futhi iphaneli iyakutshela lokho ngaphansi kwe-**Folder**.)

Uma u-Ana engekho futhi uBen edinga ifayela, {{ui:Phula ukuphuma}} liyakukhulula. Kufanele abhale isizathu; sifakwa elogini kanye nokuthi ukuphuma kwakungokukabani. Uma ephrojekthi inabalawuli abadweliwe, bona kuphela abangakwenza lokho. Ukuphuma okudlule umkhawulo wokuphelelwa yisikhathi (izinsuku ezingu-14 ngokuzenzakalela) kumakwa ngokuthi *isikhathi eside*. Ngemva kokuphula, ukungenisa kuka-Ana okwephuzile kuyenqatshwa, ngakho nina ningakwazi ukubhalela phezu komunye.

## 8. Amafolda efu

Ifolda yefu iyifolda yephrojekthi egcinwe kusitoreji sefu seqembu lakho, ngakho iqembu alidingi idrayivu ehlanganyelwe. Ku-Studio, ungene ku-akhawunti yakho ye-CadCraft, iphaneli ibonisa {{ui:Vula ifolda yefu}} eduze kwe-{{ui:Khetha ifolda yephrojekthi}}. Yikhethe bese ukhetha ifolda, ohlwini oluthi isikhala sokusebenza / ifolda. Umnikazi wesikhala sokusebenza udala ifolda kuqala, futhi kufanele isitoreji sefu seqembu sisebenze. Konke okungenhla kusebenza ngendlela efanayo; iseva yenza ukukhiya, okuqinile phakathi kwabantu. Uma ungakangeni, iphaneli iyakutshela ukuthi ungene. Ethebhini lesiphequluli elijwayelekile inkinobho yefu ayitholakali; sebenzisa i-Studio.

## Zama

Yenza izinyathelo ezingu-8 ngenkopi yefayela langempela. Ezinyathelweni zabantu ababili udinga omunye umuntu, noma amawindi amabili ngamagama amabili. Uma uzama nge-folda eyangempela ehlanganyelwe, ikhasi lokuhlola lomphakathi liqukethe ukuhlola okubuza lokhu ngqo, futhi lamukela imiphumela.
