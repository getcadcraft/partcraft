I-**izakhiwo setholeransi** iyangeza izilinganiso ezilandelana kuchungechunge olududuza izingxenye eziningi ukuze ibone ukuthi umphumela, ngokuvamile igebe, ungahluka kangakanani uma yonke ingxenye yenziwa noma ngabe ikuphi phakathi kwetholeransi yayo.

## Khetha uchungechunge

1. Vula i-assembly bese ukhetha {{ui:Isakhiwo setholeransi}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Inhlanganisela}}. Ikhadi livula phezu kombono.
2. Khetha uchungechunge lukhonjwa **ngaphansi kwa**: X, Y noma Z ye-assembly, noma {{ui:Okujwayelekile kobuso bokuqala}}.
3. Cindezela {{ui:Khetha uchungechunge}} bese uchofoza izinhlangothi uchungechunge eludlula kuzo, **ngokulandelana**: qala ohlangothini olulodwa lwegebe, udlule ezingxenyeni, bese uphela ohlangothini olunye legebe.
4. Cindezela {{ui:Kuqediwe}} noma {{key:Enter}}. I-{{key:Backspace}} ibuyisela ukuchofoza kokugcina; {{key:Esc}} iyakhansela.

Ukuchofoza kabili endaweni yengxenye eyodwa kwenza isilinganiso sayo (ibanga layo elihamba kuchungechunge). Ukuchofoza esinye isiqephu kwenza **ukuhlangana** phakathi kwezingxenye, lapho zihlangana khona; alunawo umkhawulo wetholeransi ngaphandle kokuthi uyibhale. Izixhumanisi ziyangeza noma zikhiphe ngendlela uchungechunge elihamba ngayo, futhi zivala ilupu: igebe elikhishwe yimodeli yileli elibonisiwe.

Ungakwazi futhi ukuthi {{ui:Engeza ixhumanisi}} ngesandla bese ubhala inominal yalo, ithole lalo nokuthi lenezela (+) noma lukhipha (−).

## Nikeza ixhumanisi ngalinye ithole lalo

Ixhumanisi ngalinye linokuphambuka **okuphezulu** nokuphambuka **okuphansi** (isibonelo +0.1 no−0.05). **Thola kusuka ku-** izigcwalisa ngokusuka engxenyeni:

- **Ithole elijwayelekile**: inkambu ye-Tolerance yebhulokhi yesihloko sengxenye (bona [Umdwebo ukuya ku-PDF](topic:drawing-pdf)), uma ifunda ±0.1, +0.2/-0.1 noma ISO 2768-f, -m, -c noma -v (izilinganiso zomugqa ze-ISO 2768-1, ngosayizi womugqa). Ithole eliyisisekelo lengxenye lisetshenziswa ngokushesha lapho uchungechunge likhethwa.
- **GD&T**: noma yiyiphi incazelo yefreyimu yokulawula ku-drawing yengxenye. Izindawo zesikhundla, zephrofayli nezokuhamba zinika ± ingxenye yethole. Ithole lesimo noma lokuma ngokuvamile lisendaweni yetholeransi yosayizi esevele ikhona: liyengeze kuphela lapho uchungechunge lufinyelela ubuso olulawulwayo. Ngo-Ⓜ noma Ⓛ indawo ithathwa ngosayizi wayo oshiwo; ithole lebhonasi alibalwa.
- **Okubhaliwe**: noma yiziphi izinombolo oziphrinta.

## Funda imiphumela

- **Igebe, inominal** nokuthi, lapho amathole engalingani, **Igebe, mean** — +0.2/−0 no+0/−0.1 zishintsha phakathi kohla.
- **Isimo esibi kakhulu**: yonke itholetansi kumkhawulo wayo ngesikhathi esisodwa. Igebe alikwazi ukuphuma kulokhu.
- **RSS** (root sum square): ithole ngalinye lithathwa njengezinga elingu-3 lokuhlukahluka kokusatshalaliswa okuvamile, bese liyengezwa njengengcuba yesamba sezikwele zawo. Imihlangano eminingi ingaphakathi kakhulu esimeni esibi kakhulu; lesi yisilinganiso esijwayelekile zingxenye ezenziwa ngobuningi.
- Nikeza **Igebe okungenani** nokuthi **Igebe okungenani kakhulu** ukubona ukuthi isimo esibi kakhulu sihlala ngaphakathi yini, nokuthi ama-assembly angaki ngesigidi ngo-RSS alindele ukuphuma.
- Ikholomu ethi **#** ihlela izixhumanisi ngokwesabelo sazo se-RSS: qinisa okokuqala ukuze wenze umehluko omkhulu.

{{ui:Londoloza i-CSV}} ibhala izixhumanisi, izabelo zazo nemiphumela. Izakhiwo setholeransi zigcinwa kufayela le-assembly, futhi ushintsho ngalunye luyisinyathelo esisodwa kwi-{{key:Mod+Z}}.

:::note
Isakhiwo setholeransi siyindlela eyodwa. Asilandeli ama-engeli noma ukuguquka kuchungechunge, futhi alifundi amatholeosayizi kumadrowing; nikeza lawo njengamathole abhaliwe noma ngebhulokhi yesihloko.
:::
