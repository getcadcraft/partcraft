Isibalo esifana ne **Extrude1 depth = thick + extra** siyasebenza ezindleleni zombili.

## Phambili: shintsha ipharamitha

Ethebhini elithi **{{ui:Imingcele}}**, shintsha `thick` kusuka ku-20 kuya ku-30. Ukujula kwe-Extrude1 kuba 30 + extra, futhi ingxenye iyakhiwa kabusha.

## Emuva: shintsha inombolo eshayelwayo

1. Chofoza "Extrude1" emlandweni. Ibhokisi layo **Ukujula (mm)** libonisa **ƒ**: isibalo siyayishayela.
2. Thayipha usayizi ofunayo, isibonelo `40`, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

I-PartCraft ixazulula ipharamitha ngemuva kwesibalo, ngezinyathelo eziningi njengoba kudingeka (`depth = hub_h`, `hub_h = disc_t + 50`: ukuthayipha ubude kushintsha `disc_t`). Ibha yesimo ithi yini eshintshile, isibonelo ukuthi extra ishintshe isuka ku-5 iya ku-15.

Uma ipharamitha ezimbili noma ngaphezulu zishayela inombolo, ibhokisi libuza **Iyiphi ipharamitha kufanele ishintshe?** futhi libale lezo. Chofoza eyodwa; ezinye zigcina amanani azo. {{ui:Khansela}} ayishintshi lutho.

Okufanayo kusebenza kusikethi: chofoza kabili izilinganiso ezishayelwayo, thayipha inani, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Kusebenza nasekholomini **Value** ethebhileni lamapharamitha, kupharamitha engumbhalo wefomula ngokwayo.

## Lapho kungenzeki

- Inombolo okufanele ibe yinombolo ephelele (inani lephethini) noma ihlale ngaphakathi kobubanzi (i-engeli kuya ku-360°) inqatshwa ngaphambi kokuthi kushintshe lutho.
- Isibalo sezinombolo ezingashintshi kuphela (`=40`) alinayo ipharamitha yokushintsha: inombolo oyithayipha ithatha indawo yaso.
- Uma kungekho nani lepharamitha elinikeza usayizi owuthayiphile, ibha yesimo iyakutshela futhi akushintshi lutho.
- Isikethi esingenakuxazululwa ngenani elisha sigcina amanani asendulo, futhi ibha yesimo iyasiqamba igama.

Konke okushintshiwe, kunoma iyiphi indlela, kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sika {{key:Mod+Z}} no {{key:Mod+Y}}.
