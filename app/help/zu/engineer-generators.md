**Isikhiqizi** yisikripthi esilinganisa ingxenye ngezidingo zakho bese siyakha. Isikhiqizi somshintshi wokushisa sithatha ukushisa okudingeka ukuthi kudluliswe, izigeleza ezimbili nezinga lokushisa zazo, ingcindezi kanye nempahla; sibala ukuthi isigaxa kufanele sibe kanjani nokuthi udonga lwaso lukhulu kangakanani, siyakha, futhi sikutshela izinombolo asizisebenzisile nokuthi ngayinye yavela kuphi. Shintsha okufakwayo, ingxenye iyalandela.

Umphumela uyinhlaka ejwayelekile engxenyeni: ukhona ohlwini lwezici, uyaphrintwa, uyathunyelwa. Isikhiqizi ngokwaso sigcinwa engxenyeni, ngakho ifayela livulwa kunoma iyiphi ikhompyutha ngaphandle kokuthi isikhiqizi sifakiwe, futhi **ukuvula ingxenye akuwubizi ukuthi kusebenze isikripthi**.

## Yenza umzimba

1. Khetha indawo yokusebenzela ye-{{ui:Injiniyela}} kushintshi wendawo yokusebenza.
2. Khetha isikhiqizi ohlwini (esihamba ne-PartCraft, bese noma yiziphi ozivulile kufayela) bese uchofoza {{ui:Okusha}}.
3. Isikripthi siyasebenza. Lapho siqeda umzimba usebonakala embonweni, nephaneli ibonisa okufakwayo kwawo, **ishidi ledatha** kanye **nezinhlolo** zawo.

Ungaphinda uwengeze kusuka ku-Idizayini: {{ui:Izici}} › {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} › {{ui:Umzimba owenziwe}}.

## Shintsha

Shintsha noma yikuphi okufakwayo bese umzimba wenziwa futhi emizuzwini nje emva kokuthi uyeke ukubhala. Iphaneli ithi yiliphi kwamathathu aliqiniso:

- **Usuqediwe**: umzimba uhambisana nesikripthi nokufakwayo kwaso.
- **Okufakwayo kushintshile**: umzimba engxenyeni ushintsho lwangaphambili; uyakhiwa futhi, noma cindezela {{ui:Qalisa}}.
- **Akukaqalwa** noma **Ukuqalisa kwehlulekile**: kubonisa iphutha lesikripthi nomugqa waso.

{{ui:Misa}} iyeka ukuqalisa okuthatha isikhathi eside. Undo ibuyela emuva ngesinyathelo ngasinye: ukuqalisa kuyisinyathelo esisodwa, nokufakwayo kuyisinyathelo esisodwa.

## Ishidi ledatha

Yonke inani isikhiqizi silisebenzile lidwetshwe nayo **iyunithi** yalo kanye **nokuthi livela kuphi** (incwadi yezibalo, umgomo, ishidi ledatha). Akukho okufihliwe. Ishidi liyingxenye yefayela, ngakho liyafana kwikhompyutha elandelayo. [Data out](topic:data-out) ichaza ukuthi lilondolozwa kanjani njenge-JSON noma PDF.

## Izinhlolo

Isikhiqizi singahlola ingxenye yaso ngokumelene nenqubo yokuphrinta: izindonga nezikhala ezincane kunomkhawulo wenqubo, izingcezu ezikhululekile, izikhala ezigcina impuphu noma i-resin, imigodi emincane kakhulu ukuthi ingadlulwa, nezindawo eziphakanyekayo ezidinga ukusekelwa. Okutholiwe kusephaneli nasohlwini lwe-{{ui:Izinkinga}} (inkinobho ene-⚠), futhi kugcinwa ngengxenye.

Izibalo zenqubo ziyizinombolo ezizungezile ezinikezwa izincwadi zokuklama ezijwayelekile ngenqubo jikelele (FDM, SLA, SLS, umbhede womsizi wensimbi). **Akuyona inkomba yomshini wakho**: zishintshe ngezibalo zesitolo sakho ngaphambi kokuthembela ekuhloleni. Ukuhlola kusebenza ngegridi ye-voxel, ngakho kunembile cishe ku-voxel elilodwa: udonga lubikwa kuphela uma lulincane kunomkhawulo ngaphezu kwe-voxel elilodwa.

## Okuhilelwe

| Isikhiqizi | Esikhiqizayo |
|---|---|
| Gyroid block | Ibhlokhi egcwele i-gyroid, i-Schwarz P noma ishidi le-diamond: i-lattice elula kunazo zonke |
| Gyroid heat exchanger | Isigaxa somshintshi wokushisa wamanzi amabili, silinganiswe ngesidingo sokushisa ngendlela ye-effectiveness-NTU, udonga lwaso ngengcindezi; ingahlola ukwehla kwengcindezi ngocwaningo lokugeleza kwe-cell eyodwa |
| Distribution manifold | Ihedha namagatsha, ipayipi ngalinye lilinganiswe ngokugeleza olusithwalayo |
| Graded lattice beam | I-lattice enodonga oluthinta lapho ukuhlaziywa kwetopology kukhombisa ukuthi impahla iyadingeka |

Konke lokhu kuyizilinganiso zokuqala ezisekelwe ezibalweni zencwadi. [Ilabhulari yezobunjiniyela](topic:engineering-library) ichaza izibalo nemikhawulo yazo. Qalisa Simulation, Thermal noma Flow kumphumela ngaphambi kokuthembela kuwo.

## Izilawuli zokuthumela phandle

Izikhiqizi ze-Injiniyela nezishidi zedatha zinesaziso sezilawuli zokuthumela phandle nezijeziso. Lifunde ku-[Izilawuli zokuthumela phandle ze-Injiniyela](topic:engineer-export-notice); nalo lisezansi kwawo wonke amashidi edatha.

## Akukakwakhiwa

- Izihlahla, izixhumanisi nezifakeli ku-core yomshintshi wokushisa: ngumgogodla kuphela.
- Amajika ezinga lokushisa lokuqina kwezimpahla: izimpahla zisezingeni lokushisa legumbi.
- Ukulahleka kwe-tee nokwabelana kokugeleza phakathi kwamaphothi kumanifold.
- Ukungeza izikhiqizi kusuka kuhlu lwezengezo ngokuchofoza kanye; ukuvula ifayela kwengeza eyodwa kulesi siphequluli.
- Izikhiqizi zezinye izinhlelo.
