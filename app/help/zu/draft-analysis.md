Izingxenye ezenziwe ngesikhunta, ngedayi noma ngokuthulula ngesihlabathi zidinga **uhlaka**: izindonga ezithambekile kancane ukuze ingxenye iphume kalula. **Uhlaka lokuhlaziya** luyakholisa iziphethu ngazinye ngokuthi zithambekela kangakanani ngokuqondene nesiqondo esivula ngaso isikhunta (**ukuhudula**).

## Ukuyivula

1. Ku-workspace ye-Idizayini, vula {{ui:Imibono}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Ukuhlaziya kohlaka}}.
2. Setha **Ukuhudula**: isiqondo esivula ngaso isikhunta, ecaleni le-X, Y noma Z ngakunoma iyiphi indlela, noma **Okuyisikwele nohlangothi olukhethiwe**: cindezela {{ui:Khetha uhlangothi}} bese uchofoza uhlangothi olusheshile lwengxenye.
3. Setha **Uhlaka oluncane**, ngamadigri: ukuthambeka okuncane inqubo yakho edinga (imvamisa u-1° kuya ku-3°).

Ingxenye iyakholiswa:

| Umbala | Okusho |
|---|---|
| Luhlaza: **Uhlaka olulungile** | Luthambekele ohlangothini lokuhudula ngokungenani okuncane: lukhululeka kulolu hhafu lwesikhunta |
| Mpunga: **Kudinga uhlaka** | Ngaphakathi kokuncane kunoma yiluphi uhlangothi, njengodonga oluqondile: engeza uhlaka lapha |
| Bomvu: **Uhlaka olungalungile** | Luthambekele ngaphandle kokuhudula okungenani okuncane: lukhululeka kwelinye uhhafu, noma lusikeka ngaphansi uma ingxenye kufanele iphume kuhhafu elilodwa |

Isabelo sendawo yengxenye yalo kilasi sikhonjiswa eduze kwalo. **Shintsha isiqondo sokuhudula** libuka kusuka ohlangothini olunye lwesikhunta: okuluhlaza nokubomvu kuyashintshana.

Iziphethu ezigobile zihlolwa ngephuzu ngephuzu, ngakho isilinda esilele phansi kwesiqondo sokuhudula sibonisa ibhendi edinga uhlaka ezinhlangothini zayo.

Imibala ilandela ingxenye njengoba uyishintsha. {{key:Esc}} noma {{ui:Vala}} ivala ukuhlaziya; akukho okulondolozwa engxenyeni.

:::tip
Ukuze wengeze uhlaka, sebenzisa isici {{ui:Draft}} ezindaweni ezimpunga (bheka [Sweep, loft and draft](topic:sweep-loft-draft)), bese uhlola futhi.
:::
