Lezi yizinto ezishintshe ku-PartCraft 0.7, uma kuqhathaniswa ne-0.6. Izingxenye ziza kusuka emafayeleni amaningi, futhi indawo entsha yenza imihlahlandlela ngokusuka kuklama lwakho.

## Okusha

### Imihlahlandlela ngokusuka kuklama lwakho

- **Indawo yokusebenza ye-Illustrate.** Khetha {{ui:Illustrate}} kumshintshi we-{{ui:Indawo yokusebenza}}, bese {{ui:Okusha}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Umhlahlandlela}}: umhlahlandlela uqala ngengxenye evulekile noma ngenhlanganisela evulekile, ngenhlanganisela egciniwe, noma ngefayela le-glTF, OBJ, STL noma 3MF (isihlahla sawo namagama kugcinwa; amakhophi engxenye eyodwa aba yingxenye eyodwa enenani). Umhlahlandlela uyifayela layo (`.partcraft-manual.json`) enekhophi yezingxenye, ngakho imibhalo iqhubeka kuyilapho uklama luqhubeka. Bona [Qala umhlahlandlela ku-Illustrate](topic:illustrate).
- **Imibono nokubukeka.** Fihla, yenza zibonakale kancane, hlukanisa noma shintsha imibala yezingxenye, dlala umbono ohlukaniswayo, sika isigaba noma ibhokisi emodelini, engeza isikhulisi, bese ugcina konke njengombono. Bona [Imibono, ukubukeka, izigaba nesikhulisi](topic:illustrate-views).
- **Amabhamuni nohlu lwezingxenye.** Ukuchofoza kanye kwenza amabhamuni zonke izingxenye umbono oziboniso; izinombolo zihlala nezingxenye zazo futhi azisoze ziphindwe. Uhlu lungahlala embonweni futhi lugcinwe njenge-CSV. Bona [Uhlu lwezingxenye namabhamuni](topic:illustrate-parts-list).
- **Izincazelo ezihlala zinamathiselwe:** amalebula nezincazelo ezigcwalisa inombolo yengxenye, igama, impahla noma inani, izimpini, izibalo, amakhona neradiyasi ezivela ezingxenyeni, ezimo nezithombe. Zihamba nengxenye yazo lapho ihlukaniswa. Bona [Izincazelo ezihlala zinamathiselwe](topic:illustrate-annotations).
- **Imigqa yobuciko, imiyalelo nefayela le-3D.** Thumela noma yimuphi umbono njengemigqa yobuciko ye-vector (SVG noma PDF) enemigqa efihliwe namasekhishi anemigqa, noma njenge-PNG; shintsha imibono ibe imiyalelo ngezinyathelo ibe yi-PDF noma ikhasi elilodwa le-HTML; noma gcina ifayela elilodwa le-HTML elivula ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kunoma yisiphequluli esiyiso, nemibono, uhlu lwezingxenye nokunyakaza. Bona [Imidwebo yezobuchwepheshe](topic:illustrate-line-art), [Imiyalelo](topic:illustrate-instructions) kanye [Ifayela le-3D elisebenzisanayo](topic:illustrate-interactive).
- **Ukunyakaza nokubuyekeza.** Faka izikhombisi zokunyakaza ezingxenyeni nasekhamera, qopha umbono ohlukaniswayo sinyathelo ngesinyathelo, bese ulondoloza ividiyo. Lapho uklama lushintsha, {{ui:Buyekeza kusuka kwidizayini…}} ibonisa okunyakazile, okushintshile, okufikile noma okuhambile ngaphambi kokuthi kushintshe lutho, futhi igcina imibono, izincazelo, amabhamuni nezikhombisi zihlala nezingxenye zazo. Bona [Yenza umhlahlandlela ube nokunyakaza](topic:illustrate-animation) kanye [Buyekeza umhlahlandlela kusuka kwidizayini](topic:illustrate-update).

### Ukuvula amanye amafayela

- **Amafayela e-STEP avulwa njengezidumbu eziqinile.** {{ui:Vula}} ifunda i-AP203, i-AP214 ne-AP242 STEP ngaphandle kwe-exact kernel. Izindawo eziqondile ziqhubeka ziqondile, izindawo ezigobile ziba izindawo eziqondile eziningi ezincane, futhi inhlanganisela ingena njengesidumbu esisodwa nengxenye ngayinye lapho ifayela lilibeka khona. Dweba ezindaweni zalo eziqondile, usike kuzo futhi wengeze kuzo. Bona [Vula ifayela le-STEP bese wakha kulo](topic:import-step).
- **I-mesh ye-STL, OBJ ne-3MF iba izidumbu eziqinile.** Vula noma uyidonse ifayela: i-PartCraft ihlanganisa amakhona aphindiwe, ivala izikhala ezincane, igcwalisa izimbobo futhi iguqule izinxantathu ezibheke ngendlela engalungile, bese ikutshela ukuthi yenzeni nokuthi i-mesh manje iyavaleka yini. Khetha izinyunithi nokuthi yiyiphi indlela eyaphezulu, bese isidumbu sifika njenge-**Imported1**, nezindawo zaso eziqondile njengezindawo zokudweba. I-mesh engeke ivale itshela ukuthi kusele izimbobo ezingaki futhi ayingezi lutho. Bona [Vula i-STL, OBJ noma i-3MF njengesidumbu esiqinile](topic:open-meshes).
- **Amafayela e-.3dm avulwa njengesidumbu esisodwa.** Ama-mesh, ama-polysurface, ukukhishwa kanye nokubekwa kwamabhulokhi efayela le-`.3dm` ifika njengesidumbu esisodwa esiqinile, kusuka kuma-mesh agcinwe kufayela noma ahlelwe izinxantathu yi-PartCraft, ngokulungiswa okufanayo nokuhlolwa kwe-"iyavaleka?" njenge-mesh. Amajika, umbhalo, izinto ze-SubD, izendlalelo nemibala kukhishwa, nencwadi iyasho lokho. Bona [Vula imodeli ye-.3dm njengesidumbu esiqinile](topic:open-3dm-files).

### Izingxenye nezinhlanganisela

- **Uhlaka oluvela ezigabeni zensimbi.** Dweba uhlaka ngemigqa, esketshini eyodwa noma kwezimbalwa kuhlaka lwe-3D, bese {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} › {{ui:Ingxenye yesakhiwo}}: isigaba etafuleni le-AISC noma le-EN 10365 sihamba kuwo wonke umugqa. Amakhona ayasikwa ngokuhlangana noma ngokuthinta, izingxenye ezihlangana ngokwe-T noma ezintathu ekhoneni zisikwa ukuze zilingane, imiphetho ekhululekile ingathola amakepisi namapuleti okuqinisa amakhona, nohlu lokusika lunika ubude nama-engeli wokuphela kwesiqeqeshana ngasinye ({{ui:Londoloza uhlu lokusika (CSV)}}). Bona [Uhlaka oluvela ezigabeni zensimbi](topic:structural-members).
- **Amaluphu avaliwe kuzinhlanganisela.** Izingxenye ezihlanganiswe ngendandatho (uxhumo lwamabha amane, ihemu nesilayidi, uhlaka oluqiniswe) manje ziyavaleka: donsa noma uthayphe inani kulupu bese konke kulandela, futhi ukuhlolwa kokunyakaza kuhamba ngaleluphu ngendlela efanayo. Ukuxhumana okusenokunyakaza kuba luhlaza okwesibhakabhaka, nelupu elingeke livale liba bomvu futhi lishiye ukuthi likude kangakanani. Bona [Amaluphu avaliwe: uxhumo](topic:build-assembly#closed-loops-linkages).
- **Izingxenye ezinamakhulu ezakhiwo.** I-PartCraft igcina isidumbu esasinaso phakathi komlando, ngakho ushintsho ngasemuva komlando lwakha kabusha kuphela izakhiwo ezilandela, futhi okusikiwe nokuphakanyiwe okungathintani kwenziwa kanyekanye. Ivivinywe ngengxenye enezakhiwo ezingu-750. Iwindi lezimingcele ligcina ukuqhubeka namakhulu wezibalo, futhi ukuhlehlisa kugcina izinyathelo ezingu-200 ngaphandle uma zisebenzisa inkumbulo eningi (akusoze kube ngaphansi kwezingu-20). Bona [Izingxenye ezinamakhulu ezakhiwo](topic:edit-history#parts-with-hundreds-of-features).
- **Iphekhi yamavarianti kusuka ezilungiselelweni zakho.** {{ui:Iphekhi yamavarianti…}} ngaphansi kwe-**Ukulungiselelwa** ku-thebhu ye-{{ui:Imingcele}} yakha zonke izilungiselelo, noma ezo ozithikile, ku-zip eyodwa: i-STEP, i-IFC, ishidi lokudweba le-DWG kanye nesibonelo ngasinye, ithebula lezimingcele zazo (CSV ne-JSON), nengxenye njengento eyodwa yomtapo nezivarianti zayo. Ukulungiselelwa okungakwazi ukwakhiwa kuyadwetshwa futhi kukhishwe. Umugqa womyalo wenza okufanayo nge-`partcraft variants`. Bona [Yenza iphekhi yamavarianti kusuka ezilungiselelweni zakho](topic:variant-pack).

### Ukwakha

- **Izisebenzi zamapost ongazihlela.** Amapost afana ne-Fanuc, GRBL ne-LinuxCNC ku-Manufacture manje angamafayela amafupi kufomethi yePartCraft yakhe, futhi abhala kanye kanye okwawabhala ngaphambili. {{ui:Hlela iposi…}} ivula ikhophi yakho yomunye (futhi {{ui:Iposi elisha…}} ne-{{ui:Ngenisa ifayela le-post…}}), ikhombisa ukuthi yiliphi umugqa olungalungile, futhi ibonise i-G-code ayibhalayo njengoba uthayipha. Amapost agcinwa engxenyeni nanjengamafayela e-`.cpost`. Bona [Izisebenzi zamapost ongazihlela](topic:post-processors).
- **Ukuqeda i-3D: Parallel ne-Scallop.** Qeda ubuso bonke bemodeli ngebhola, ngesihlabi se-bull-nose noma esiyisicaba, ngemigqa eyi-Parallel nanoma yiliphi ikhona, noma ngokudlula okuhlukaniswe ngebanga elithile ukuze umgogodla phakathi kwawo ube nokuphakama okucelwayo (akusebenzi nesihlabi esiyisicaba). Bona [Ukuqeda i-3D: parallel ne-scallop](topic:finish-3d).
- **Umtapo wamathuluzi.** Gcina amathuluzi kulesi sisheshi sengxenye ngayinye, bese uwathumela noma uwangenise njengefayela le-JSON ukuze uwadlulisele kwenye ikhompyutha. Bona [Umtapo wamathuluzi](topic:tool-library).
- **Isitoko ngokuqhathaniswa nemodeli ngemibala.** Uma **Bonisa isitoko esilingisiwe** sivuliwe, isitoko siluhlaza emodelini, sibomvu lapho ithuluzi lisike khona, nesiphuzi kuya kwegrey lapho isitoko sisele khona, nencwadi esendaweni yephaneli. Bona [Lingisa](topic:manufacture#simulate).
- **Okuseleyo okugcinwe phakathi kwama-nest.** Ngemva kwe-nest, okusele kwephuleti ngalinye kungaba yinsalela enenombolo nempahla yayo nobukhulu bayo. I-nest elandelayo yempahla nobukhulu obufanayo isebenzisa izinsalela kuqala, bese kugcwala amaphuleti; uhlu lwesitoko lugcinwa kufayela nanjengeCSV, futhi i-DXF iphawula insalela ngayinye. Bona [Gcina futhi usebenzise okuseleyo](topic:nest-offcuts).
- **Izinhlelo ze-laser.** {{ui:Thumela}} › {{ui:Uhlelo lwelaser…}} ithatha izinkombandlela kufayela le-DXF futhi isethe ukuthi isendlalelo ngasinye senzani (sika, cwecwa, bhala, gcwalisa), ibonise izinyathelo zekhanda bese igcina i-G-code yemodi yelaser ye-GRBL. Ku-Chrome ne-Edge ingaphinda ihlaka umsebenzi ngelaser ivaliwe, bese iwuthumela nge-USB. Izilungiselelo zigcinwa kufayela. Bona [Thumela umdwebo ku-laser](topic:laser-cutting).
- **Ukuthumela kwe-USD.** {{ui:Thumela}} › {{ui:Iphakheji ye-USD (.usdz)}} ibhala ingxenye nempahla yayo, ivolumu nobunzima, noma inhlanganisela enengxenye ngayinye kanye kanye namakhophi wonke abekiwe, ngamamitha nge-Z ephezulu. Bona [Thumela ingxenye noma inhlanganisela njenge-USD](topic:usd).

### Abasizi

- **Amathuluzi womsizi wakho we-AI.** Uma kuvuliwe ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:PartCraft}} › {{ui:Abasizi}}, umsizi oxhunyiwe nge-CadCraft Studio angafunda izakhiwo, izimingcele nezakhiwo zobunzima, asethe ipharamitha bese ekha kabusha (isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa), aqhube isikripthi se-CraftScript, Lua noma Python njengendlela indawo yokusebenza ye-{{ui:Isikripthi}} eyenza ngayo, athumele i-STL, 3MF, i-STEP ebhikiwe noma i-USD, agcine, futhi athumele umhlahlandlela njenge-PDF, SVG, imiyalelo noma ifayela le-3D elisebenzisanayo. Iseva lefayela nomugqa womyalo we-`cadcraft` kwenza okufanayo ezingxenyeni nasemihlahlandlelweni egciniwe. I-PartCraft ayinayo i-AI. Bona [Sebenzisa i-PartCraft nomsizi we-AI](topic:use-with-ai-assistants).

## Okushintshiwe

- **Ingxenye enesidumbu esivela kufayela igcinwa njengenguqulo yengxenye 5.** Ingxenye enesidumbu se-STEP, STL, OBJ, 3MF noma `.3dm` igcina izinxantathu ezilungisiwe ngaphakathi kwefayela le-`.partcraft.json`, ngakho awudingi ifayela lokuqala futhi. I-PartCraft 0.6 yenqaba inguqulo 5 ngomyalezo wayo "inguqulo entsha" esikhundleni sokuyivula ngendlela engalungile. Ezinye izingxenye zigcinwa njengakuqala. Bona [Amafayela e-PartCraft](topic:file-formats#the-partcraft-files).
- Amapost obhala wena agcinwa engxenyeni. I-PartCraft endala evula ingxenye enjalo iyawuphonsa amapost lapho iyigcina futhi.

## Kuzo zonke izinhlelo

- **Izinyunithi.** {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Okujwayelekile}} › {{ui:Izinyunithi}} inolunye uhlu ngalunye lwe-ubude, indawo, ivolumu, i-engeli nokuthambeka, ngosayizi abanikezwa izincazelo zeyunithi (i-US survey foot iyiyunithi yayo). Vala iyunithi ongayisebenzisi nakancane, futhi ayiphumi ohlwini lweyunithi; faka inkanyezi kwenye, umsebenzi omusha uqala kuyo. Ifayela lihlala ligcina iyunithi yalo. Amabhokisi e-PartCraft awalandeli izinhlu noma inkanyezi kusaqala: izingxenye sezisabonisa ngamamilimitha. Bona [Izinyunithi, izinani ezithayiwe nezinhlu zeyunithi](common:unit-lists).
- **Isakhiwo esihlangene nezipaneli ezihlungazelwayo.** {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Okujwayelekile}} › {{ui:Ubukhulu besakhiwo}} › {{ui:Okuhlangene}} ingena okwengeziwe esikrinini (izikrini ezithinta nemishini ebanjwayo zigcina izinkinobho zazo ezinkulu). Iphaneli ngayinye eseceleni inebha yesihloko emincane: yidonse ukuze uyimise noma uyiphakamise, yilahle ebhareni yephaneli yenye ukuze yabelane ngefreyimu njengamathebhu, uyibophe ukuze ishelele ngasemphethweni, noma uyifihle bese uyibuyisela emphethweni. Bona [Lungisa izipaneli](common:arrange-panels#make-the-layout-denser).
- **Amadayalogi ayanyakaza.** Donsa idayalogi ngebha yesihloko uyikhiphe endleleni yomdwebo; ibuyela lapho uyishiye khona. Chofoza kabili ebhareni yesihloko ukuze uyibuyisele ephakathi. Bona [Hambisa idayalogi](common:arrange-panels#move-a-dialog).
- **Iresiphi zabasizi be-AI.** Izicelo eziyishumi ezisebenzayo zomsizi wakho (hlela umdwebo, dweba isethi, hlola imidwebo, buza imodeli imibuzo, bala ukubalwa kwamanani, zama ivarianti yengxenye…), ngayinye nethuluzi layo elibizwayo nezimvume ezidingekayo, futhi ngayinye ihlolwa ngezivivinyo zethu. Izinhlelo azinayo i-AI. Bona [Iresiphi zabasizi be-AI](common:assistants-recipes).
- **Izilimi.** **Português (Portugal)** itsha kuthi {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Okujwayelekile}} › {{ui:Ulimi}}. Yona, **Português (Brasil)**, **Español (México)** kanye **Bahasa Indonesia** manje sekunawo wonke amagama ezinhlelo, njengemihlahlandlela engakabhekwa yisisebenzi somdabu. Izihloko zosizo zisesiNgisi, kanti eyodwa isesiSpanishi.
- **I-CadCraft Studio** ithi kungani ukuhlolwa kwesibuyekezo kwehlulekile (akukho ifayela lesistimu yakho okwamanje, akukho inethiwekhi, isiginesha esingalungile) esikhundleni sokuthi "Kwenziwe" nje, futhi iyeka ukubuyisa ikhadi le-Okusha elivaliwe uze ube ukukhishwa okusha.

## Akukakwenziwa kule nguqulo

- **0.7 ayisayiniwe ngekhodi.** Ukusayina, nephakheji ye-Microsoft Store, kudlulele ku-0.8 ngoba ukuqinisekiswa kwesitifiketi akukafiki. Hlola i-SHA-256 eduze kwefayela ngalinye ku-getcadcraft.com/download. Ku-Windows, uma i-SmartScreen imisa isifakeli, khetha {{ui:Ulwazi oluthe xaxa}}, bese {{ui:Qhubeka noma kunjalo}}. Ku-Mac, okokuqala, chofoza ngakwesokudla ku-CadCraft Studio bese ukhetha {{ui:Vula}}. I-CadCraft Studio ayikakabi esitolo sezinhlelo, futhi kakukho ukwakhiwa kwe-iPad noma i-iPhone okwamanje.
- Izinhlu ze-engeli nokuthambeka ku-{{ui:Izinyunithi}} ziyagcinwa, kodwa akukho bhokisi elizifunda okwamanje.
- Izidumbu ezivela kumafayela e-STEP, e-mesh nama-`.3dm` azinakho umlando, nezindawo zazo ezigobile ziyizindawo ezincane eziqondile. Imibala, amagama ezingxenye nesihlahla senhlanganisela akufundwa ku-STEP; amajika, izinto ze-SubD, umbhalo, izendlalelo nemibala akufundwa ku-`.3dm`. Umfundi we-`.3dm` ubhekwe emafayeleni abhalwe ithuluzi lefomethi ngokwalo, hhayi okwamanje emafayeleni agcinwe uhlelo lokumodela ngokwalo.
- I-Illustrate ayikavuli amafayela e-STEP ku-{{ui:Kusuka kwenye ifayela…}} okwamanje (vula i-STEP kwi-Idizayini bese uqala {{ui:Kusuka engxenyeni evulekile}}), i-ModelCraft ne-BimCraft azikwazi ukudlulisa imodeli ngqo kuyo, nezithombe ezisezingeni le-path tracer azinikezwa. Ukuthunyelwa kwevidiyo nefayela elisebenzisanayo azikahlolwa ngamafoni nezithebuleti zangempela okwamanje.
- Izinhlelo ze-laser zithatha izinkombandlela kumafayela e-DXF kuphela: amaphethini aqondile ezingxenye zethala adinga i-exact kernel. I-CadCraft Studio ayinayo i-Web Serial okwamanje, ngakho gcina i-G-code bese uyithumela ngohlelo lwe-laser yakho. I-G-code ye-laser namapost amathathu akhelwe ngaphakathi awakaqinisekiswa emshinini wangempela: hlola zonke izinhlelo kusilingisi somlawuli wakho kuqala.
- Ukuqeda i-3D kuyi-Parallel ne-Scallop kuphela: akukho ukuhlanza i-3D, i-waterline, i-pencil noma ukukhishwa okusele, akukho ukujikeleza. Umtapo wamathuluzi ugcinwa kulesi sisheshi, awuhlanganiswa.
- Ikhasi lomlungiseleli, lapho izivakashi zikhetha ivarianti kuwebhusayithi zilande, livulelwe abasebenzi kuphela kuze kuvulwe isiswishi sewebhusayithi. Amachweba (izindawo zokuxhuma) akasethwa kusuka ku-PartCraft.
- Izingxenye zesakhiwo zilandela imigqa eqondile kuphela futhi ziyisidumbu esisodwa, azinawo amajeqe okushisela.
- Izitayela zemenyu yebhande ze-PartCraft zisadweba zonke izinkinobho ngosayizi ogcwele.
- Imagnethi: amamagnethi asingiwe aseqa ku-2-D kuphela; izinjini, insimbi egcwele kanye nomeshi wezingxenye ezigobile azikakhiwa.

Konke okunye kusencwadini [Okungakakhiwa](topic:not-built-yet).
