Ukuhlolwa kwemibili kwenziwa ezingxenyeni njengoba zibekiwe: **Ukugqekeza** kubheka izinto ezigqumanayo manje, futhi **ukuhlola kokunyakaza** kuhambisa uxhumanisi owodwa ngaso sonke ububanzi bawo kubheke ukugqumana ngendlela.

## Hlola ukuphazamiseka

1. Vula noma yakha inhlanganisela (bona [Yakha inhlanganisela](topic:build-assembly)).
2. Khetha {{ui:Ukugqekeza}}.

Ibha yesimo ibika umphumela:

- "Akukho ukuphazamiseka phakathi kwezingxenye ezi-3 (… ms)." Akukho okugqumanayo.
- "Ukuphazamiseka: Flange1 kanye Flange2: Ukugqumana, 2.5 mm³." Ngayinye yamapheya, uhlobo lomphumela nevolumu abelana ngayo.

Ibhokisi le-**Inhlanganisela** yesobunxele likhombisa **Ukuphazamiseka**: "Akukho", "imiphumela engu-2" (eyiboniswayo), noma "Akuhlolwanga". Uma kungekho okukhethiwe, ipheneli lesokudla lidwebela wonke umphumela ngaphansi kwe-**Ukuphazamiseka**.

Umphumela uyasulwa noma nini lapho inhlanganisela ishintsha. Phinda {{ui:Ukugqekeza}} ngemva kokuhambisa uxhumanisi. Zonke amapheya ezingxenye zihlolwa nezinye ngayinye, ngakho izinhlanganisela ezinkulu zithatha isikhathi eside.

### Izinhlobo zemiphumela

| Uhlobo | Kusho |
|---|---|
| **Ukugqumana** | Izingxenye zabelana ngevolumu: ukugqekeza kwangempela. |
| **Ukungalingani kokujula kocingo** | Ivolumu eyabelwanwayo isemcingweni wengxenye eyodwa kodwa ingaphezu kwowesinye: ibhawodi elifakwe ocingweni lujulile kunomgodi walo, noma inqamu engenacingo emgodini onocingo. |
| **Ukulingana kocingo (imicingo yokuhlobisa)** | Ivolumu eyabelwanwayo isemcingweni wezingxenye zombili: ibhawodi emgodini onocingo. Imicingo yokuhlobisa idwetshwa ngosayizi ophelele wocingo, ngakho ihlala igqumana kancane. |
| **Ubuso obuthinta** | Akukho volumu eyabelanwayo, kodwa ubuso buthintana (phakathi kuka-0.001 mm). |

Imicingo emodelwe (bona [Imicingo kumashafthi nasemigodini, edwetshiwe noma emodelwe](topic:threads)) iyijiyomethri yangempela: lokho abelana ngakho kungu-**Ukugqumana**.

### Fihla lokho ongadingi ukukubona

Ngaphansi kwe-**Ukuphazamiseka**, **Bonisa** inebhokisi ngalinye lohlobo: susa ukuthikwa kwe-**Ukulingana kocingo** kanye **Ubuso obuthinta** ukuze ubone kuphela ukugqekeza okubalulekile. **Fihla lolu bhangqa** eduze komphumela ufihla lelo bhangqa lezingxenye noma yiluphi uhlobo lwalo. Imiphumela efihliwe iyabalwa ("imiphumela engu-2 ifihlwe yilezi zihlungi"), futhi **Bonisa amapheya afihliwe futhi** uwabuyisela. Ibha yesimo iphinde ibale imiphumela efihliwe.

Izihlungi zilondolozwa nenhlanganisela, futhi noshintsho ngalunye lungesinyathelo esisodwa se-{{key:Mod+Z}}. **Zula uxhumanisi ukuze uthole ukugqekeza** (ngezansi) nazo ziyazisebenzisa. Inhlanganisela enezihlungi ilondolozwa njengenguqulo engu-4, eyenqaba ukuvulwa yizinguqulo zangaphambili ze-PartCraft kunokulahlekelwa yizihlungi.

## Bheka ukunyakaza

1. Khetha uxhumanisi onyakazayo (noma yiluphi ngaphandle kwe-eqinile) ohlwini oluseceleni lwesobunxele.
2. Donsa isilayidi ngaphansi komunye wamanani awo, njenge-**Ikona (°)** noma **Ukuhlehliswa (mm)**. Ingxenye iyanyakaza ngesikhathi udonsa.
3. Yeqa. Indawo entsha iyagcinwa futhi ingahlehliswa nge-{{key:Mod+Z}}.

Ungabhala futhi ikona noma ukuhlehliswa ebhokisini bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Uxhumanisi emjikelezweni ovaliwe (uxhumo) uhambisa umjikelezo wonke: inani odonsayo liyagcinwa nezixhumanisi zomjikelezo ezinye ziyalandela (bona [Imijikelezo evaliwe](topic:build-assembly#closed-loops-linkages)).

## Zula uxhumanisi ukuze uthole ukugqekeza

1. Khetha uxhumanisi onyakazayo.
2. Ngoxhumanisi onamanani amaningi (esilindrikhali, endizeni, ebhola, isikhonkwane emgodini), khetha okuzulwayo ku-**Hlola izinyathelo**.
3. Khetha {{ui:Hlola ukunyakaza ukuthola ukungqubuzana}}.

I-PartCraft ihambisa leli nani ezindaweni ezingu-25 ngezinyathelo ezingu-24 ezilinganayo, amanye ahlala njengoba enjalo: ikona kusukela ku−180° kuya ku-180° (ukuthambeka kusukela ku−90° kuya ku-90°), ubude kusukela ekukhishweni kuya ekungeni usayizi wengxenye enkulu kunazo. Ibika:

- "Akukho kugqekeza noma kuphi ku-Ikona (°) kusuka ku−180 kuya ku-180 (izindawo ezingu-25 zihloliwe)."
- "Izindawo ezi-6 kwezingu-25 ziyagqekeza, okokuqala ku-Ikona (°) 45 (12.4 mm³)." Indawo yokuqala egqekezayo kanye nokugqumana lapho.

Inkinobho ibe mpunga ngoxhumanisi oqinile, obungahambi. Ukuzula kuhlola izindawo kuphela, hhayi indlela phakathi kwazo: ingxenye encane ingadlula kwenye phakathi kwezinyathelo ezimbili.

:::note
Ukuxhumana nokudonsela phansi akukakhiwa. Ngaphandle kwemikhawulo ukuzula kumboza wonke ububanzi; nika inani **Imikhawulo** (bona [Uhlu lwezingxenye, imibono echazwayo nokuhambisa kwezingxenye](topic:assembly-bom-explode)) bese ukuzula kumboza lowo mikhawulo kuphela, ngakho isibambi esivula ngama-90° sihlolwa ngama-90°.
:::

## Ucwaningo lokunyakaza

**Inhlanganisela › Ukunyakaza** iqhuba inani loxhumanisi owodwa kusukela ekuqaleni kuya ekugcineni phezu kwesikhathi onikezayo (isivinini esihlala sinjalo, esithambile, i-sine, noma phuma bese ubuyela), ibeka inhlanganisela kwisinyathelo ngasinye ngezixhumanisi zayo nemijikelezo evaliwe, futhi ibika ukuthi yiziphi izingqubuzano nokuthi izingxenye zisondela kangakanani. **Engeza iphoyinti** ilandela maphakathi ingxenye noma iphoyinti oyichofozayo kuyo, futhi ikhadi lidweba indawo yalo, isivinini nokushesha, inani loxhumanisi kanye nesikhala esisele ngesikhathi. **Londoloza i-CSV** kanye **Londoloza ishadi** ziyabhala lokho. Isivinini nokushesha kuphuma ezindaweni ezirekhodiwe, ngakho ipiki elibukhali phakathi kwezinyathelo lifundeka lincane: sebenzisa izinyathelo eziningi.

Ngemva kwe-**Qala**, ibha ye-**Hamba phakathi kokuqhutshwa** ihambisa inhlanganisela kunoma yisiphi isinyathelo (isikhathi, inani kanye nokuthi izingxenye zigqekeza yini lapho zibonakala eduze kwayo); uxhumanisi ugcina inani lawo, futhi **Buyela kumodeli** ibuyisela inhlanganisela. Izilungiselelo zocwaningo zilondolozwa efayeleni lenhlanganisela; imiphumela yazo yenziwa kabusha yi-**Qala**.

## Ucwaningo lokuxhumana

**Inhlanganisela › Ukuxhumana** ikhombisa ukuthi inhlanganisela ithwala kanjani imithwalo lapho izingxenye zicindezelana. Ingxenye ngayinye iletha izisekelo, imithwalo nempahla yayo esuka kocwaningo lwayo lwe-**Simulation** (ubuso oluqinile kwengxenye eyodwa, amandla kwenye); lapha ukhetha amapheya. **Engeza ipheya**, bese **Khetha uhlangothi A** bese uchofoza ubuso bengxenye eyodwa, **Khetha uhlangothi B** bese uchofoza ubuso bengxenye ehlukile (ukuchofoza ubuso okukhethiwe kususa; {{key:Esc}} noma **Kwenziwe** iphetha ukukhetha). Ipheya lingu-**Ngaphandle kokungqubuzana** (izingxenye ziyacindezelana kodwa ziyashelela) noma **Libotshiwe** (zihlala zindawonye); inani lokuqina libeka ukuthi ukuxhumana kumelana kanjani nokuhlezi (u-20 ukuqala okuhle, u-1000 kwesitaki sensimbi eqinile).

**Xazulula** iqhuba umthwalo ngezinyathelo futhi ibonise amandla okuxhumana, ukuhlezi okujulile kakhulu, ukunyakaza okukhulu kakhulu nokucindezeleka okukhulu kakhulu. Ingxenye engenaphayi ishiywa ngaphandle futhi ikhadi liyasho lokho. Ukuxhumana okungenakungqubuzana akuphathi ingxenye ngasohlangothini: nika yonke ingxenye isisekelo kocwaningo lwayo lwe-Simulation, noma yibophe, kungenjalo izinyathelo zingazinzi. Amapheya nezilungiselelo ziyalondolozwa efayeleni lenhlanganisela.

**Thumela i-CalculiX deck** ibhala imodeli efanayo njengefayela lokufaka le-CalculiX (.inp). I-CalculiX iwuhlelo olwehlukile olufakwa lona bese uluqalisa wena ngokwakho; **Funda imiphumela ye-CalculiX (.frd)** bese ifunda ukunyakaza ku-mesh yale nhlanganisela futhi yenqaba ifayela elenziwe ye-mesh enye. Isixazululi lapha singasebenzisa ukuguqulwa okungabuyi, kodwa hhayi i-explicit dynamics, futhi lapha akukho ukuhlaziywa okuqinisekisiwe: umhlahlandlela ohlola noma yini ebalulekile.
