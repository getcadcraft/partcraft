I-PartCraft ayigcini lutho phakathi kwezindwendwe. Ingxenye yakho ihlala ethebhini yesiphequluli kuze kube yilapho uyilondoloza njengefayela, futhi ibuyela uma uvula leyo fayela.

## Londoloza ingxenye

1. Naka igama lengxenye: chofoza indawo engenalutho ekubukeni ukuze kungakhethwa lutho, bese uthayipha **Igama** esigabeni sakwesokudla bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Isihloko somlando siyashintsha sifane nalo.
2. Cindezela {{key:Mod+S}} noma khetha {{ui:Save}}.
3. Isiphequluli silanda `<name>.partcraft.json`. Ibha yesimo ithi "Ilondoloziwe Bracket.partcraft.json (umlando ogcwele; yivule lapha ukuze uqhubeke nokuhlela)."

Uma kuvulekile isikethi, ukulondoloza kuyasivala kuqala.

Leli fayela libamba zonke izikethi, ukuxhumana, izilinganiso kanye nezici, pho nezici ezivinjiwe, isikhundla se-rollback, izilungiselelo zeshidi lomdwebo nezinothi ze-GD&T, nombhalo ogcwele wanoma yisiphi isiqinile se-STEP esingenisiwe. Uhlu lwakho lokuhlehlisa aluglongwa.

## Londoloza inhlanganisela

Kwinhlanganisela, ukuthi {{ui:Save}} noma {{key:Mod+S}} ulanda `<assembly name>.partcraft-asm.json`. Yonke ingxenye igcinwe ngaphakathi kwayo: awudingi amafayela engxenye ahlukile ukuze uyivule kabusha.

Lapho uhlela ingxenye ngaphakathi kwenhlanganisela, {{key:Mod+S}} ilondoloza leyo ngxenye yodwa njenge `.partcraft.json`. Khetha {{ui:Back}} kuqala, bese ulondoloza, ukuze ulondoloze inhlanganisela yonke.

## Vula kabusha

Noma yisiphi kwalezi:

- khetha {{ui:Open}} noma cindezela {{key:Mod+O}}, bese ukhetha ifayela;
- donsa ifayela kusuka {{pick:mac=Finder|windows=File Explorer|linux=isiphathi sakho samafayela}} liye ekubukeni kwe-3D ("Shiya ingxenye ye-PartCraft ukuze uyivule" iyavela).

I-PartCraft ihlukanisa izingxenye ezinhlanganisela ngokuthi kukhona yini ngaphakathi, ngakho zombili ziyavuleka ngendlela efanayo. Ifayela le-`.step` noma le-`.stp` livula njengesolidi esingeniswe: bheka [Vula ifayela le-STEP bese wakha kulo](topic:import-step). Ibha yesimo iyaqinisekisa: "Ivuliwe Bracket.partcraft.json: izici ezi-7." noma "Ivuliwe Hinge.partcraft-asm.json: izingxenye ezi-3, izixhumanisi ezi-2."

Uma ingxenye esesikrinini inezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga, i-PartCraft iyababuza kuqala: {{ui:Cancel}}, {{ui:Discard changes}} noma {{ui:Save first}}.

:::warning
Vala inhlanganisela ({{ui:Close}}) ngaphambi kokuvula ifayela lengxenye. Uma uvula ingxenye kuyilapho inhlanganisela isesikrinini, ingxenye ilayisha ngemuva kwenhlanganisela futhi uyibona kuphela ngemva kokuvala inhlanganisela. Ukuvula ifayela lenhlanganisela futhi kuyashintsha inhlanganisela engalondoloziwe ngaphandle kokubuza, ngakho yilondoloze kuqala.
:::

## Qala kabusha

{{ui:New}} iqala "Part1" engenalutho. Iyabuza kuqala uma kukhona izinguquko ezingalondolozwanga, futhi ivala inhlanganisela evulekile (ibuze uma ingalondoloziwe).

## Gcina izinguqulo

Ukulondoloza ngamunye kulanda kabusha, ngakho ungagcina izinguqulo ngokugcina amafayela kuphela:

- Faka inguqulo noma usuku egameni ngaphambi kokulondoloza: "Bracket rev B".
- Isiphequluli sakho singangeza " (1)", " (2)" uma ifayela elinegama elifanayo sele likhona kufolda yokulanda. Okusha kakhulu yileyo enenombolo ephezulu kakhulu, noma hlola usuku kusuka {{pick:mac=Finder|windows=File Explorer|linux=isiphathi sakho samafayela}}.
- Hambisa amafayela aqediwe asuke kufolda yokulanda aye efolda yephrojekthi yakho.

Bheka [Lapho amafayela akho ayakhona](common:your-files) ukuthola lapho okulandwa khona ngesistimu ngayinye.

## Ungalahlekelwa umsebenzi

- Londoloza ngaphambi kokuvala ithebhu, ukulayisha kabusha, noma ukuvumela ikhompyutha ilale isikhathi eside. Isiphequluli sibuza "Shiya indawo?" uma uzama ukuvala unezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga, kodwa ukuphahla noma i-laptop evaliwe akunikezi isixwayiso.
- Amawindi wangasese noma incognito ayasebenza, kodwa akukho okunye okuhlukile: ifayela libe yikopi eyodwa kuphela.
- Ukulondoloza kwe-cloud nomlando wezinguqulo akukatholakali okwamanje.
