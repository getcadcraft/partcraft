Ibha yokubuyela inquma ukuthi imlando eningi kangakanani yakhiwe. Izici ezingaphansi kwayo ziyizimnyama futhi azakhiwe; izici ezintsha zingena ebhareni.

## Hamba isici esisodwa ngesikhathi

Uma ingxenye isesikrinini (akukho sikethi noma umdwebo ovuliwe):

| Yenza | Umphumela |
|---|---|
| {{key:[}} noma inkinobho yomcibisholo ophezulu eduze kwegama lengxenye | Ibha inyuka isici esisodwa |
| {{key:]}} noma inkinobho yomcibisholo ophansi | Ibha yehla isici esisodwa |
| {{key:End}} noma inkinobho yebha iye ekupheleni | Zonke izici ziyakhiwa futhi |

I-status bar ithi ibha isiphi, isibonelo "Ibha yokubuyela ngemva kuka-Sketch1: izici ezingu-1 kwezingu-2 zakhiwe". Izinkinobho ziyacima uma ibha ingahambi ngaphezulu ngaleyo ndlela.

Ungakhetha futhi {{ui:Buyela lapha}} kunoma isici, noma uchofoze **Rollback bar · roll forward** emlandweni.

## Faka isici phakathi

1. Hambisa ibha emuva ize ihlale lapho isici esisha kufanele khona.
2. Engeza isici: sigena ebhareni.
3. Cindezela {{key:End}} ukuze wakhe lonke umlando ngaphezu kwalokho.

Kulo lonke ukuhamba kwebha kuyisinyathelo ongasihlehlisa ngo-{{key:Mod+Z}}. Indawo yebha igcinwa kufayela, ngakho ingxenye ingathunyelwa ibuyile emuva. Ithuluzi lomugqa womyalo nalo liyayihambisa: `partcraft rollback part.json` ne `partcraft forward part.json --end`.
