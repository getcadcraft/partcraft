Izinhlolovo ezimbili ezisheshayo zokubona ukuthi izinhlangothi zengxenye zibushelelezi kangakanani, kunoma yiyiphi ingxenye ku-Design workspace (i-Form workspace inayo yayo, yefomu oyihleli: bona [Izimo ezingaqondile nge-Form](topic:form-surfaces)).

## Imigqa ye-zebra

Vula {{ui:Imibono}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Imigqa ye-zebra}}. Ingxenye ibonisa ukubonakala kwesibani esinemigqa, njengoba nobuso obucwebile bukwenza:

- Lapho izinhlangothi ezimbili zihlangana **ngokuthinta** (i-fillet phezu kwendawo eyisicaba, indawo ebushelelezi ye-Gordon), imigqa iqhubeka ihamba phezu kwendawo yokuhlangana ngokugoba.
- Lapho zihlangana ku-**umgoqo** oqinile, imigqa iyaphuka igxume.
- Lapho nokugoba kuvumelana (G2), imigqa igeleza ngaphandle nje kokugoba okuncane.

Phendula umbono (ngokudonsa, noma ngobuso be-view-cube): imigqa idwetshwa kabusha isuka endleleni entsha. **Imigqa** isetha ukuthi zingaki.

## Ukugoba

Khetha {{ui:Ukugoba}} kwimenyu efanayo. Ingxenye iphenjulwa ngombala ngokugoba kwayo **kwesilinganiso**: kuluhlaza lapho ingena ngaphakathi, kukhanyayo lapho ilingana, kubomvu lapho iphuma ngaphandle. Ikhadi lithi ukugoba kuphi lapho imibala ifinyelela amandla aphelele, ngo-1/mm nangokuyisibonakaliso sexhoku. Imiphetho ebukhali ibalwa njengegoqekile kakhulu, ngakho ibonisa ngamandla; bheka izinhlangothi eziphakathi kwayo.

## Ikhadi

Womabili avula ikhadi lokuhlaziya eliphansi kwesobunxele sombono, elibamba nokuhlaziya kohlaka ([ukuhlaziya kohlaka](topic:draft-analysis)). Shintsha phakathi kwabo nge-**Bonisa**. Ukukhetha okuvuliwe futhi ku-{{ui:Imibono}}, ku-{{ui:Vala}} noma ku-{{key:Esc}} kuyicisha. Imibala ilandela ingxenye njengoba uyishintsha; akukho okulondolozwa engxenyeni.
