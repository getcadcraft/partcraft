Izinsiza zobunjiniyela (abakhiqizi, umtapo wolwazi wobunjiniyela, izihlolo namafayela edatha) **azidingi amalungelo omlawuli** futhi **azidingi i-installer**. Zibhala kuphela okudweliwe lapha, akukho okunye.

| Yini | Lapho igcinwa khona |
|---|---|
| Izingxenye zakho, nemizimba yazo eyakhiwe, amashithi edatha nezihlolo | Ifayela lengxenye oyilondolozayo (`.partcraft.json`), lapho uyilondoloza khona. |
| Abakhiqizi obavule kumafayela | Ku-storage yohlelo lwayo: i-local storage yesiphequluli ekhasini lewebhu, nefolda `data` eseduze nohlelo kwi-Studio ephathekayo. Okungafika ku-20. |
| Izilungiselelo (usayizi wama-voxel owusethile, amathrendi esixazululi, isakhiwo) | Ku-storage efanayo yohlelo. |
| Amashithi edatha, izindawo, imeshi | Kuphela lapho uzigcina khona: ukulanda kuya lapho ukhetha khona. |

Lezi zinsiza **azenzi zicelo zenethiwekhi zazo**: abakhiqizi abahambisiwe ngamafayela asesiqondisweni sohlelo, umtapo wolwazi nezihlolo zisebenza kukhompyutha yakho kwi-worker engafinyeleli inethiwekhi, nedatha yobunjiniyela (izakhiwo zamanzi nomoya, izimpahla) itholakala ngaphakathi kohlelo. Umkhiqizi ovulwe kufayela ufundwa kukhompyutha yakho futhi awuyi noma kuphi.

## I-zip ephathekayo ye-Windows nele-Linux

I-Studio ephathekayo ye-Windows igcina izilungiselelo, ama-cache nezengezo zayo kufolda ethi `data` eseduze nohlelo. I-Linux AppImage iyigcina iseduze nayo uma kukhona ifayela elibizwa nge-`portable` eseduze kwayo; ngaphandle kwalelo fayela isebenzisa ifolda yakho yasekhaya. Azikho ifomu ephathekayo ye-Mac: isistimu igcina uhlelo olulandiwe lungabhali eduze kwalo. Amanothi ephathekayo angokwesistimu (usizo lwe-`Studio`) adweba okubhalwa uhlelo.

## Ukufinyelela komsingathi

Akukho lapha okucela ukufinyelela komsingathi. Amaprinta, imishini ye-serial namadivayisi e-USB ayahlukile: ayacela lapho umsebenzi udinga wona, kanye ngedivayisi ngayinye, futhi akukho okuthinta abakhiqizi noma izihlolo.
