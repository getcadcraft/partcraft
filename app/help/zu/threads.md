{{ui:Ucingo}} ibeka icingo ezindaweni ezijikelezayo: ngaphandle kwensimbi (icingo langaphandle) noma ngaphakathi komgodi (icingo langaphakathi). I-PartCraft iyazi ukuthi yiyiphi kusuka ebusweni.

Icingo liqala **ngokubukeka kuphela**: ubuso buhlala bungashintshiwe, umbono udwetsha icingo njengamasekhele amabili asephuka, nebhodi lombumbo liyaliqopha, njengalokhu "M10×1.5 - 6g, thread 20 long". Thikha **Emodeliwe** ukusika icingo langempela elihelical ebhodini, ukuze kuphrintwe nge-3D, noma imodeli kudingeka ibukeke kahle.

## Faka icingo ensimbini noma emgodini

1. Yakha insimbi noma umgodi ngosayizi wecingo: insimbi enesidikiba esingu-Ø10 ye-bolt engu-M10, noma umgodi ongu-Ø8.5 (umgodi wokuqala wokusika) we-nut engu-M10.
2. Chofoza ubuso obujikelezayo, bese ukhetha {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} › {{ui:Ucingo}}. Uma ungakakhethi buso obujikelezayo, i-PartCraft iyakucela ukuthi uchofoze izinhlangothi, bese {{ui:Kuqediwe}}.
3. Ephaneli esekhohlo, hlola **Usayizi**. **Lingana nobuso** ikhetha usayizi ngokwayo: ensimbini, usayizi onobubanzi obukhulu obulingana nobensimbi; emgodini, usayizi onobubanzi obuncane obulingana nomgodi.

Icingo liqala ekugcineni okuvulekile kobuso, lapho ingxenye ingaqhubeki ngomugqa ophakathi: ekugcineni okukhululekile kwensimbi, emlonyeni womgodi. **Qala kolunye uhlangothi** iyaguqula lokho.

## Izilungiselelo

| Isilungiselelo | Okukhethwayo | Okuzenzakalelayo |
|---|---|---|
| **Ifomu** | ISO metric (60°), M1.6 kuya ku-M64 coarse; trapezoidal (Tr, 30°), Tr8×1.5 kuya ku-Tr60×9 | ISO metric |
| **Usayizi** | Lingana nobuso, noma usayizi oyodwa kuhlu | Lingana nobuso |
| **Ubuso bonke** | Ukucisha kukuvumela ukunikeza **Ubude (mm)**, ekalwa kusuka ekuqaleni | Kuthikhiwe |
| **Qala kolunye uhlangothi** | Icingo lihamba kusuka kolunye uhlangothi lobuso | Kuvaliwe |
| **Isandla** | Isandla sokunene noma sokhohlo | Isandla sokunene |
| **Emodeliwe** | Sika icingo langempela elihelical | Kuvaliwe |
| **Clearance (mm)** | Iyabonakala lapho kuthikhiwe i-Emodeliwe: icingo lensimbi lincane kangakanani, nelomgodi likhulu kangakanani, ngazo zonke izinhlangothi | 0.1 |

Usayizi ongalingani nobuso uyakhiqizwa noma kunjalo, uthi ulungiswe ukuze ufanele ubuso, futhi iphaneli iyakushilo, njengokuthi "Insimbi ye-Ø12 mm ayisiyo i-Ø10 mm ye-M10". Izinhlangothi eziningi zingabelana ngesici esisodwa se-{{ui:Ucingo}}; ngo-**Lingana nobuso**, ngayinye ithola usayizi wayo.

## Amacingo amodeliwe okuphrinta nge-3D

Icingo elimodeliwe lilandela iphrofayli eyisisekelo ejwayelekile: i-ISO 68-1 kumacingo e-metric (izinkanyezi ezinqamile nezingxenye ezingaphansi, izinhlangothi ezingu-60°), i-ISO 2901 kumacingo e-trapezoidal (izinhlangothi ezingu-30°). Ushintsho lwe-**Clearance** lunyusa iphrofayli yonke ngaphakathi (insimbi) noma ngaphandle (umgodi), ukuze izingxenye ezi-printed zihlangane ngamacingo:

- **0.1 kuya ku-0.2 mm** kulungele izinkuni eziningi ze-printer. Phrinta i-bolt nenut yokuhlola kuqala.
- **0** iyiphrofayli ejwayelekile, yezingxenye ezenziwe ngomshini noma zokuhlola ukulingana.

I-bolt ne-nut enesilinganiso esifanayo azikaze zithinte ngohlangothi: igebe phakathi kwazo liphindwe kabili isilinganiso, zonke izinhlangothi.

:::tip
Amapitshi amancane aphrinta kabi: M3 nangaphansi, noma ipitshi elingaphansi kuka-0.5 mm, lisondele kakhulu kokungazwa kwe-nozzle engu-0.4 mm. Modela amacingo amakhulu, usebenzise i-heat-set insert noma umgodi osikiwe kwawancane.
:::

Icingo elimodeliwe liyingxenye ye-solid: ukuthumela kwe-STL, 3MF ne-STEP kuyawuthwala, futhi ithuluzi lokulinganisa liyalilinganisa. Lenza ingxenye ibe nzima ukuyakha kabusha: icingo le-M10 elinobude obungu-20 ligcina cishe amathrayangela angu-150,000.

## Imigodi esikiwe

Umgodi **Tapped (ISO metric)** unesiwitshi **Emodeliwe** nokuthi **Clearance** efanayo. Bona [Bhola imigodi nezimbobo ngamaphoyinti eskethi](topic:holes).

## Lapho icingo lingakwazi ukwakheka

- "Icingo lihamba ebusweni obujikelezayo (isilinda): insimbi noma umgodi." Ubuso okhethiwe buyisicaba noma bugoqeke ngenye indlela.
- "Akukho usayizi ojwayelekile ofanele ubuso obujikelezayo obungu-Ø0.6 mm: khetha usayizi." {{ui:Lingana nobuso}} ayitholanga lutho ngaphakathi kuka-12 % wobuso. Khetha **Usayizi**, noma shintsha ubuso.
- "Ubuso lo icingo labuphezu kwalo sekunyamalele (…). Khetha futhi." Ushintsho lwangaphambilini lususe ubuso. Chofoza {{ui:Khetha izinhlangothi}} bese uchofoza ubuso futhi.

:::note
Amacingo amaduma (UNC, UNF), amacingo epayipi, amacingo athambile, ukuphela kwecingo nokuqala kwecingo elibhevelwe akakakhiwa okwamanje. Ikhenli engempela ibonisa icingo elimodeliwe njengelibukeka kuphela.
:::
