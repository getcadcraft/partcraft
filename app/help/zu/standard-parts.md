I-PartCraft ivula izingxenye zomshini ezivamile zilungele: izilinganiso zivela ezithebhulini zezinga, futhi wonke usayizi kuthebhu ungukucushwa ongashintshela kuwo.

## Vula eyodwa

1. Chofoza {{ui:Ingxenye ejwayelekile}} eqenjini elithi {{ui:Ifayela}}.
2. Ku-{{ui:Ingxenye}}, khetha uhlobo, isibonelo {{ui:Iskrubu enekhanda le-hex (ISO 4017)}}.
3. Ku-{{ui:Usayizi}}, khetha usayizi, isibonelo `M10 × 40`.
4. Chofoza {{ui:Vula}}.

Ingxenye ivula njengengxenye entsha, engakalondolozwa. Yilondoloze ukuze uyigcine ({{key:Mod+S}}).

| Ingxenye | Izinga | {{ui:Imingcele}} |
|---|---|---|
| Iskrubu enekhanda le-hex, ibhawu le-hex | ISO 4017, ISO 4014, ASME B18.2.1 (inch) | `d` ubukhulu be-thread, `s` ububanzi bamaflethi, `k` ukuphakama kwekhanda, `l` ubude |
| Ukhuni ohexagonal | ISO 4032, ASME B18.2.2 (inch) | `d`, `s`, `height` |
| Iwashela elivamile | ISO 7089 | `d1` ngaphakathi, `d2` ngaphandle, `h` ukuqina |
| Ipini elilinganayo | ISO 2338 | `d`, `l` |
| Ibheringi lebhola lomsele ojulile | ISO 15 | `d` umgodi wangaphakathi, `D` ngaphandle, `B` ububanzi |
| Ukhiye ohambisanayo | DIN 6885-1, uhlobo A | `b` ububanzi, `h` ukuphakama, `l` ubude |

## Shintsha usayizi

Wonke usayizi ungukucushwa (bona [Ukucushwa nethebula zokwakha](topic:design-tables)): vula {{ui:Imingcele}} bese ukhetha elinye, noma shintsha ipharamitha, njengo `l` weskrubu ende, bese uyigcina njengokucushwa kwakho.

## Okumodeliwe

- Iskrubu namabhawu: ikhanda le-hexagon nomzimba olula. Okhuni: i-hexagon nembobo yobukhulu obuyisisekelo bocingo. Ucingo akumodelwanga; engeza nge-{{ui:Ucingo}} uma udinga (bona [Ucingo](topic:threads)).
- Amapini: isilinda esilula; ama-chamfer wamaphethelo ashiywe ngaphandle.
- Amabheringi: impahla yangaphandle kuphela (umgodi wangaphakathi, ububanzi bangaphandle nobubanzi), okudingekayo ukuze uwabeke.
- Okhiye: ukhiye onomkhawulo ojikelezile.

Impahla yengxenye iyinsimbi, ngakho ipanele lesisindo likhombisa isisindo sayo. Amagiya awikho okwamanje: i-PartCraft ayinayo isici samazinyo, nediski akuyona igiya. I-CadCraft idweba amagiya ngokwesivumelwano sokudweba, futhi umtapo unawo njengemidwebo.
