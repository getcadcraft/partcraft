{{ui:Check this frame}} uthatha izingxenye zensimbi ezilula zomsebenzi wokuwelda, zakhe uhlaka kuzo, luxazulule ngaphansi kwesisindo sensimbi nemishini oyibeka kuyo, bese luhlola yonke ingxenye nawo wonke amawelda lapho izingxenye zihlangana khona. Ukuhamba kukodwa, ithebhu elilodwa.

:::warning
Isisiza **kuze kube yilapho injiniyela iyibukile**. Akuyona isitembu, futhi ukudlula akuyona idizayini. Isebenzisa imigqa emaphakathi yezingxenye njengoba zidwetshwe, ithatha wonke amajoyinti njengewelda ezungeze isigaba sonke, ithatha ubukhulu bohlangothi lwe-fillet kudonga lwesigaba (kuze kube ngu-8 mm) umaungasethanga, futhi ayihloli izikulufo, amapuleti, amapuleti esisekelo, ama-anchor, ukuxhasa, i-block shear, ukukhathala noma ukuphakamisa.
:::

## Yisebenzise

1. Dweba izingxenye ngo-{{ui:Structural member}} (izigaba zivela ethebhleni lezigaba).
2. Vula {{ui:Views}} › {{ui:Check this frame}}. Tshela lapho uhlaka lumi khona (**isisekelo** esinyaweni ngasinye: esibotshiwe, esiqinile noma esigingqikayo; {{ui:Pinned supports at the feet}} ibeka eyodwa kuyo yonke ingxenye ephansi kakhulu) nokuthi yini esekelwe kuyo (**imishini**: isisindo, indawo yokuhlehla, izindawo lapho ibotshelwe khona phansi, nokuphushwa okuthe xaxa okukhethwayo).
3. Khetha ikhodi: **ASCE 7 + AISC 360-16** noma **EN 1990 + EN 1993**. Setha ibanga lensimbi kanye, kwe-AISC, amandla ensimbi yokuwelda (482.6 MPa yi-E70).
4. Cindezela {{ui:Check}}. {{ui:Save the report as CSV}} ibhala zonke izithebhu, umugqa wokuthi akukabukwa kuqala.

Uhlaka lufundwa ngamamilimitha nomugqa oqondile wengxenye oshayela phezulu. Izindawo zokusekela nezindawo zemishini zibhalwa njenge-x y z ngohlelo olufanayo, nge-z kuyinkomba yokuphakama.

Umphetho wengxenye ogcina phakathi kwenye ingxenye uyihlukanisa lapho, njengonyawo noma indawo yokubotshelwa phansi esigqeni sengxenye. Isisindo sabelwa phakathi kwezindawo zokubotshelwa njengomzimba oqinile; isisindo sephakathi nendawo ngaphandle kwezinyawo, noma amandla okuphakamisa enyaweni, kuyashiwo, akusoze kufihlwe.

## Okutholayo

- Into esetshenziswa kakhulu nokuthi kukhona okwehlulekayo yini. Izinto ukuhlola okungakwazi ukuzahlulela (isigaba esingekho ethebhleni, insimbi engenayo ifektha ekhodini) ziyabalwa futhi ziyaqanjwa, azisoze zibalwe njengezidlulile.
- Umugqa owodwa ngengxenye ngayinye nangeqembu ngalinye lamawelda: kusetshenziswa kangakanani, ukuhlola okubusayo nesigatshana sawo, nokuhlanganiswa komthwalo okubusayo.
- Ama-tebhu afanayo ombikweni.

## Okuhlolwayo

Izigaba ezingenalutho (RHS, SHS, CHS, ipayipi) nezigaba ze-I ngokuqina, ukucindezela, ukugoba, ukusika nokusebenzisana kwazo; amawelda e-fillet namawelda agcwele ngendlela yevekthala elastic. Izindonga ezinqabile zigoba izigaba ezingenalutho kuphela; izindonga ezincane zishiwo njengezingahlolwanga. Ezinye izinhlala zabhalwa kumasethi wezinga kodwa azikaqhathaniswa nekhophi eshicilelwe; umbiko udwebe umthombo ngamunye.

Bona [Uhlaka olwenziwe ngezigaba zensimbi](topic:structural-members) ukuze udwebe izingxenye nohlu lwazo lokusika.
