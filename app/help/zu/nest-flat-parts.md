**Ukuhlela** kubeka izingxenye eziningi ephepheni ukuze kushiyeke impahla encane kakhulu. I-PartCraft ibeka izingxenye ngayinye ngayinye, ngayinye lapho igcina khona ubude obufishane kakhulu bebhodi, izama ukuhlelwa okuningana futhi iqede emasekhondini ambalwa, bese igcina okuhle kakhulu. Izingxenye zingahlala emgodini wezinye nasemgodini we-L noma i-U.

## Ukuvula

- {{ui:Thumela}} › {{ui:Hlela izingxenye ezibhethiwe ezishidini…}} ivula ngephathini eliyisicaba lengxenye, noma — kumhlangano — ngamaphathini aliyisicaba wazo zonke izingxenye zeshidi lensimbi, njengamakhophi amaningi njengoba kukhona.
- Ku-ingxenye yeshidi lensimbi, {{ui:Linganisa}} bese {{ui:Hlela ezishidini…}} ngaphansi kwephathini eliyisicaba.
- {{ui:Engeza izinkomba ezivela ku-DXF…}} yengeza zonke izinkomba ezivaliwe ku-DXF njengezingxenye: umdwebo ovela ku-CadCraft olondolozwe njenge-DXF, noma ifayela elivela kwisofthiwe yomshini wakho wokusika. Inkomba ngaphakathi kwenye iba yimbobo kuyo. Imigqa nemisongo ehlanganiswa ngokuphela ngokuphela ibalwa njengenkomba eyodwa. Amafayela ngamayintshi, amasentimitha noma amamitha ashintshelwa ku-mm.

:::note
Ukubeka ishidi lensimbi libheke phansi kudinga i-geometry kernel eqondile (bona [Ishidi lensimbi](topic:sheet-metal)). Izinkomba ezivela ku-DXF zihlala zisebenza.
:::

## Setha

Ku-ingxenye ngayinye nikeza **ukuthi udinga zingaki** kanye **nomqondo** wayo: {{ui:Noma yiluphi uhlangothi}}, {{ui:Njengoba kudwetshiwe ishidi}} lelo shidi elishiwe noma elizungezwe okufanele lihambe ngohlangothi olulodwa, noma {{ui:Ngokuphambene nesishidi}}.

Ngaphansi kwe-**Amashidi**:

- **Impahla etholakalayo**: {{ui:Amapuleti}} abude nobubanzi obunikeziwe (nenani olinalo; 0 = njengoba kudingeka), noma {{ui:Irola}} lobubanzi obunikeziwe, lude njengoba kudingeka.
- **Umkhawulo wemaphethelo** ugcinwa uvalekile emaphethelweni ayo onke eshidini (ngamaklampu noma iphethelo elingalingene).
- **Ibanga phakathi kwezingxenye** yibanga elincane kakhulu phakathi kwemigqa emibili yokusika: okungenani ububanzi bokusika komshini wakho, kanye nalokho impahla edingayo.
- **Ukujikeleza**: izinkombandlela ingxenye engajikelezwa kuzo, zonke ngama-90° ngokuzenzakalela. Ukujikeleza okumbalwa kusebenza ngokushesha; okuningi kungasebenzisa ishidi ngcono.
- **Izingxenye zingaguqulwa** kuvumela ingxenye ibekwe iphenduke. Yishiye kungasetshenziswa ishidi lensimbi: iphathini eliyisicaba elibekwe liguquliwe ligoba ngakolunye uhlangothi.

Cindezela {{ui:Hlela}}. Ukubuka kwangaphambili kukhombisa wonke amashidi; okuqukethwe kutshela ukuthi zingaki izipuleti (noma yingakanani irola elisetshenzisiwe) nokuthi izingxenye zithatha impahla engakanani. Okungafakeki kuyabhalwa ngegama.

## Londoloza

{{ui:Londoloza i-DXF}} ibhala ukuhlelwa njenge-DXF (R12, mm), ishidi ngalinye ngaphansi kwelilandelayo: imigqa yamashidi ku-ungqimba SHEET, izingxenye ku-PARTS (imisongo igcinwa njengemisongo), futhi igama lengxenye ngayinye ku-LABELS.

:::tip
Ukuxuba izingxenye eziningi kuvame ukuhlela ngcono kunamakhophi amaningi engxenye eyodwa: izingxenye ezincane zigcwalisa izikhala phakathi kwezinkulu.
:::

## Sika amashidi (G-code)

**Thumela › Amashidi ahlelwe ku-G-code** ifunda i-DXF ehlelwe (leyo, noma amashidi we-add-on ye-Cabinets ku-CadCraft) futhi yenze uhlelo olulodwa ngeshidi ngalinye ngokusebenzisa i-post oyikhethayo: izimbobo nezikhala kuqala, bese izinkomba, ngayinye isikwe ngeradiyasi yethuluzi ngaphandle kwengxenye, ngaphuthelwa ishidi kanye ne-1 mm ngaphezulu, nemigqa yokubambelela uma uyicela. Uhlu lwama-post lufaka ama-post akhelwe ngaphakathi kanye nawesifundo sakho se-Manufacture. Hlola ngayinye imikhondo kusimulator ngaphambi kokuyisebenzisa: ukubhola namacavity akwenziwa.

## Okufanele uhlole

I-PartCraft ihlola lonke uhlelo futhi kumabanga angempela (hhayi igridi eyasetshenziswa ukusesha): akukho zingxenye ezimbili eziseduze kakhulu kunebanga, nalutho emikhawulweni. Ayihleli ukusika: ukungena, ukuhleleka kokusika, ukusika imiphetho eyabelwanwe kanye nokugcina ishidi elisalayo kungumsebenzi wesofthiwe yomshini wakho wokusika. Hlola i-DXF khona ngaphambi kokusika.
