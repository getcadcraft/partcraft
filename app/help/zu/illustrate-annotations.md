Khetha ithuluzi esigabeni se-{{ui:Annotate}} ephaneli, bese uchofoza:

- **Ilebula** nenkomba: chofoza iphoyinti engxenyeni. Umbhalo uhlala eceleni kwalo nomugqa wokuxhuma eliya kuleliphoyinti; hudula umbhalo ukuze uwubeke. Umbhalo wawo uhlelwa ephaneli futhi ungasebenzisa izinkambu: `{number}`, `{name}`, `{material}`, `{description}`, `{qty}` kanye `{item}` (inombolo yebhamuni) ziyagcwaliswa kusuka engxenyeni.
- **Imicibisholo**: chofoza ubuso; umcibisholo uqala lapho bese ukhomba ngaphandle kobuso. Umugqa, wegobile noma **umcibisholo wokuphenduka** ozungeze i-normal yobuso ("turn here"). Ubude bawo, isitayela nendlela yawo kuphaneli.
- **Izilinganiso**: ibanga (amaphoyinti amabili), engeli (amaphoyinti amathathu, ikhona yesibili) noma irediyasi (amaphoyinti amathathu emphethweni ogobile). Inani lithathwa lapho izingxenye zihlanganiswe khona, ngakho ukuhlukanisa akushintshi.
- **Izimo nezithombe**: unxantathu, i-ellipse, umugqa noma umcibisholo ohlanganiswe nesithombe (ukuchofoza kabili), noma isithombe se-PNG noma JPEG.

Izichasiso engxenyeni zihamba nayo uma ihlukaniswa noma ihanjiswa embonweni, futhi aziboniswa uma ifihliwe. Ngombono ngamunye ubona izichasiso ohlwini lwawo: inkinobho yeso ephaneli iboniswa noma ifihle eyodwa kulo mbono; ibhini liyisusa emhlahlandlelweni.
