Uma wenza izingxenye ku-SolidWorks, iningi lemibono ye-PartCraft liyakujwayela: izikethi ezisezindizeni engaphambili, engaphezulu nengakwesokudla, ukuxhumana kanye nezilinganiso ezihlakaniphile, umlando wezakhi onebha yokubuyela emuva, nezinhlanganisela ezibekwe ngokuxhuma. Indlela yokufinyelela kuzo ihluke ezindaweni ezithile, futhi lokhu kwakha kokuqala kwenza kancane. Leli khasi lihlanganisa izinkambiso zakho nezinto ezikumangaza.

## Umehluko oyibhidla abantu

**Isikethi siphelele yiphrofayili.** I-Extrude ne-Revolve zisebenzisa yonke ilophu evaliwe esikethini. Awukwazi ukukhetha isifunda esisodwa noma ikhonkco elilodwa lesikethi ukuze uthole isakhi. Sebenzisa isikethi esisodwa ngesakhi ngasinye.

**Isikethi esithathwa yisakhi.** Isakhi sisebenzisa isikethi osivale ngokugcina noma esikhethwe emlandweni. Uma ungakhethi lutho sithatha isikethi esisha kakhulu esingasetshenziswa yisakhi. Uma uthola esingafanele, khetha isikethi esifanele bese ukhetha isakhi futhi.

**Izinguquko zisebenza njengoba uthayipha.** Alikho idayalogi yesakhi enokuthi OK noCancel. Yonke inombolo yakha kabusha ngokushesha, futhi i-{{key:Mod+Z}} yindlela yokubuyela. Uhlu lokuHlehlisa luhlanganisa izinguquko zesikethi, izakhi nezilungiselelo ngokufanayo.

**Ukweqile kokuchazwa akuvunyelwa ukuhlala kungaqondakali.** Alikho izilinganiso ezishayelwayo (ezisezinkomba). Isilinganiso esilungiswe yizinye izixhumanisi sibikwa njengesiweqile ("sase sinomkhawulo. Sisuse."). Isilinganiso esiphikisanayo sishiya i-geometry ingashintshi futhi sibe bomvu. Susa esisodwa, noma hlehlisa.

**Ama-sweep nama-loft akakakhiwa; ama-fillet, ama-chamfer, ama-shell nama-draft anemikhawulo.** Le nguqulo ye-PartCraft yakha zonke izingxenye nge-mesh kernel; ibha yesimo ithi "Mesh kernel" njalo. Ama-fillet ne-chamfer asebenza emaphethelweni aqondile phakathi kwezindawo ezithe xaxa, futhi lapho ama-round ehlangana endaweni eyodwa, abe mitred, angaxubani. Ama-shell ayavuleka, nama-draft athambeka, ubuso obuthe kuphela. Izakhi ezidinga i-exact kernel zisasebenza, kodwa zithi "akakakhiwa" lapho zikhethwa. Bona [I-mesh kernel](topic:solid-kernels) kanye [Okungakakhiwa](topic:not-built-yet).

**Amabili ka-Fillet.** Esikethini, {{ui:Fillet}} ({{key:F}}) igoqa ikhona yesikethi. Engxenyeni, i-{{ui:Fillet}} eqenjini lezakhi igoqa amaphethelo aqinile (amaphethelo aqondile phakathi kwezindawo ezithe xaxa kule nguqulo). Alikho i-sweep noma i-loft kule nguqulo: bona [Okungakakhiwa](topic:not-built-yet).

**Izimbobo zidinga amaphoyinti akhululekile.** {{ui:Imbobo}} ibhola ngamaphoyinti adalwe ngethuluzi le-Point. Izikhungo zendilinga nokuphela komugqa akubalwa.

**I-Through all ingeyokusika kuphela.** I-boss idinga ukujula. Alikho imiphetho ye-"up to vertex / surface / next" okwamanje.

**Isiqinile sinamaphethelo amancane.** Imesh kernel yakha ubuso obugobile ngezinhlangothi ezithe xaxa, ngakho umbono, izivolumu kanye nokuthumela kwe-STL ne-3MF kwakhiwe ngazo: **Amajika** ephaneli yengxenye uyawenza ahlukane kangcono. Ukuthumela kwe-STEP okunembile akukhona: kubhala ubuso obugobile ngomumo wabo wangempela.

**Amamillimitha kuphela.** Alikho isilungiselelo se-inch okwamanje. Phindaphinda usayizi we-inch nge-25.4 uma uwubhala.

**Y iphezulu.** Indiza ye-Front ibheke wena futhi i-Top ibheke phansi ngengxenye, njengoba ulindele, kodwa i-axis yomhlaba ephezulu yi-Y. Ukuthumela kwe-STL, 3MF kanye ne-STEP enembile kuguqulwa kuye ku-Z ephezulu; i-STEP enamaphethelo igcina i-Y iphezulu.

**Izixhumanisi ziwubuso, hhayi amapheya e-geometry.** Uxhumanisi uqala njalo kubuso obuthe ababili obubekwe ubuso nobuso. Ngephini esimgodini, khetha ubuso obuthe obuzungeze umgodi nesigxobo sephini, bese usetha **Isikhungo** ku-axis yomgodi nakuhlo lwe-axis ye-revolve yephini. Izingxenye ezingafinyelelwa yizixhumanisi zihlala lapho zafakwa khona; azikwazi ukudonswa. Izixhumanisi emjikelezweni azixazululwa.

**Ukulondoloza kuyidawuniloda.** Alikho ifayela oligcina lishintshwa njalo. Ukulondoloza ngakunye kuyifayela elisha kwifolda yakho yokudawuniloda. Qamba ingxenye kuqala, bese ugcina amafayela akho kwifolda yephrojekthi.

**Alikho imenyu yokuchofoza ngesokudla, alikho ithebhu yomphathi wemiyalo.** Konke kusethuluzi nasephaneli yesokudla, futhi ithuluzi liyashintsha ngokuthi usenzani: amathuluzi esikethi avela esikethini kuphela, namathuluzi enhlanganisela enhlanganiselweni kuphela.
