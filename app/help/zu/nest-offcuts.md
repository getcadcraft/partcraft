Uma uhlela izingxenye ezipuleteni, okusele okusetshenziswayo kwepuleti ngalinye (hhayi izingcezu ezincane kakhulu) kungagcinwa njenge-**insalela**: inkomba enombolo (R-0001, R-0002 …) enempahla, ubukhulu, usayizi walo kanye nomsebenzi owalenza. Uhlu lulondolozwa kanye nefayela futhi lungalondolozwa njenge-CSV.

## Sebenzisa futhi

Faka **impahla** kanye **nobukhulu**, ushiye {{ui:Sebenzisa izinsalela kuqala}} livuliwe, bese uhlela. Izinsalela zempahla efanayo nobukhulu obufanayo zizanywa okuncane kuqala; izingxenye ezilingene zifakwa kuzo, ngaphakathi kwenkomba yensalela ngayo nasemaphethelweni ayo; okungafakeki kufakwa kumapuleti amasha. Umbiko womsebenzi usho ukuthi yiziphi izinsalela ezisetshenziswe nokuthi yini esele. Uma {{ui:Londoloza okusele njengezinsalela}} livuliwe, {{ui:Engeza lo msebenzi empahleni}} bese ilibhala ohlwini lwempahla: insalela esetshenziswe imakwa **Kusetshenziwe** (nomsebenzi) futhi ayisanikezwa futhi; okusele kuyo kuba insalela entsha enokhumbula umzali wayo.

## Yini ebalwa njengensalela

Okusele kulinganiswa ngebanga osisethe (akukho ngxenye esondela kunalelo kusika). Imiphetho emincane kuno-1/20 wepuleti nezingcezu ezincane kuno-2 % yalo ziyimfucuza; shintsha womabili la makhawulo uma indawo yakho igcina izingcezu ezincane. Izimbobo ngaphakathi kwezingxenye ziyezingxenye ngokwazo futhi azigcinwa. Irola alinazinsalela.

## Emdwebweni

I-DXF yokuhlela idweba umkhawulo wensalela ngayinye ku-ungqimba REMNANTS nenombolo yayo maphakathi, ukuze izingcezu zimakwe lapho zisikwa.

Akukakwakhiwa: uhlaka lwensalela, ishidi lebhakhodi noma lelebula, izinsalela zamarola, impahla eyabiwe phakathi kwamaphrojekthi.
