Ikhyubhu lombono lihlala ekhoneni elingakwesokudla eliphezulu lombono ka-3D (hhayi lapho usaketsha noma ensheshini yokudweba). Liyaguquka nombono, ngakho likhombisa njalo ukuthi ubheka kangakanani: {{ui:Phezulu}} yisiqephu se-+Y yengxenye (indiza ephezulu), {{ui:Phambili}} isiqephu se-+Z (indiza ephambili ibheke wena lapho ubheka kuyo), {{ui:Ngakwesokudla}} isiqephu se-+X. Lihlala libonakala kancane kuze kube yilapho ulikhomba.

## Bheka ngaseceleni, emphethweni noma ekhoneni

1. Khomba ikhyubhu. Ingxenye ngaphansi kwesikhombi iyakhanya: phakathi kohlangothi, umphetho (ukhanya ezinhlangothini zombili ezihlanganyelwa yiwo) noma ikhona (ezinhlangothini zombili ezintathu).
2. Chofoza kuyo. Umbono uphendukela ukuze ubuke kusuka lapho, ugcine iphakathi nokusondeza okufanayo.

Kukhona izindawo ezingu-26 ongachofoza kuzo: izinhlangothi ezi-6 ({{ui:Phezulu}}, {{ui:Phansi}}, {{ui:Phambili}}, {{ui:Emuva}}, {{ui:Ngakwesobunxele}}, {{ui:Ngakwesokudla}}), imiphetho eyi-12 namakhona ayi-8. Ikhona linika umbono ojwayelekile wamakota amathathu; umphetho ubonakala uphakathi kwezinhlangothi ezimbili.

Ukuchofoza {{ui:Phambili}}, {{ui:Phezulu}} noma {{ui:Ngakwesokudla}} kunikeza imibono efanayo nakwe-{{ui:Imibono}} ku-ibha yamathuluzi, ngaphandle kokusondeza ukuze kugcwale.

## Zungeza ngokudonsa ikhyubhu

Donsa ikhyubhu ukuze uphendule umbono, njengokuzungeza ngaphakathi kombono ngokwawo. Ukudonsa akusoze kuthathwe njengokuchofoza, ngakho ungambamba ikhyubhu noma kuphi.

## Indilinga yekhampasi

Indilinga ngaphansi kwekhyubhu iqukethe N, E, S no-W.

- **Donsa indilinga** ukuze uphendule imodeli ezungeze i-axis ethe xaxa, njengoba imi etafuleni elijikelezayo. Indilinga ilandela isikhombi.
- **Chofoza uhlamvu** ukuze ubuke kusuka lolo hlangothi, ugcine ukuthi ubheka phansi kangakanani. Embonweni osuka phezulu ngqo, uhlamvu ochofoza kuwo luya ezansi kwesikrini.

## Imibono ebheke ngqo: izimpini

Uma ubheka ngqo esiqeshini, izimpini ezincane zivela zizungeze ikhyubhu:

- Izinxantathu ezine **zisa phambili** isiqephu sohlangothi lolo: unxantathu ongaphezulu kwekhyubhu ukhombisa isiqephu esasiphezulu, njalo njalo.
- Izimpini ezimbili **ezigobile** ezingakwesokudla eliphezulu ziphendula isithombe ngekota yokujikeleza ngakwesokudla noma ngakwesobunxele, zingashintshi isiqephu obheka kuso. Zisebenzise ukuphendula ipulani ukuze udonga olude luhambe ngokuqondile esikrinini.

## Ekhaya

Inkinobho yendlu ekhoneni elingakwesobunxele eliphezulu lekhyubhu ibuyela embonweni wamakota amathathu futhi ifaka ingxenye ngaphakathi. {{key:Home}} yenza okufanayo lapho ikhyubhu inokugxila kwekhibhodi.

## Ngekhibhodi

{{key:Tab}} uye kukhyubhu, bese:

| Ukhiye | Yenza |
|---|---|
| {{key:left}} / {{key:right}} | khetha uhlangothi, umphetho noma ikhona ezungeze ikhyubhu, ngezinyathelweni ezingu-1/8 sokujikeleza (abafundi besikrini batshela ukuthi yiliphi) |
| {{key:up}} / {{key:down}} | khetha eliphakeme noma elisezansi, kuya ku-{{ui:Phezulu}} noma phansi ku-{{ui:Phansi}} |
| {{key:Enter}} noma {{key:Space}} | bheka kusuka kolukhethiwe |
| {{key:Shift+left}} / {{key:Shift+right}} | phendula umbono ngekota yokujikeleza ezungeze i-axis ethe xaxa |
| {{key:Home}} | umbono wasekhaya |
| {{key:Esc}} | phuma ekhyubhini |

Izinkinobho ezicindezelwa ekhyubhini zihlala ekhyubhini: azivuli amathuluzi e-PartCraft.

## Isithonjana se-axis noma lutho esikhundleni

Vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} ({{key:Mod+,}}), ithebhu ye-{{ui:PartCraft}}, bese usetha **Isikhombi sekhona** sibe {{ui:Ikhyubhu lombono}} (okuzenzakalelayo), {{ui:Isithonjana sezingatha}} (uphawu oluncane lwe-X, Y, Z ngaphansi kwesobunxele: X obomvu, Y oluhlaza, Z oluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka) noma {{ui:Lutho}}. Lesi sisheshi sikhumbula ukukhetha. Bona [Izilungiselelo ze-PartCraft](topic:settings#corner-indicator).

:::note
Ikhyubhu libonisa ingxenye njengoba i-PartCraft iyakha: Y ephezulu, ngakho i-{{ui:Phezulu}} yekhyubhu iyindiza ephezulu. Izinhlamvu zekhampasi zokujikeleza kuphela; ingxenye ayinayo inyakatho.
:::
