Isikrini sinezingxenye ezinhlanu: ibha yamathuluzi phezulu, umlando ngakwesobunxele, i-view ye-3D phakathi, ipaneli yezakhiwo ngakwesokudla kanye nebha yesimo ngaphansi. Phezu kwakho konke kuhlezi i-app bar ye-CadCraft.

## Ibha ye-app

Ibha encane eseqongeni ingeyewebhusayithi ye-CadCraft, hhayi ye-PartCraft uqobo. Ibonisa igama le-app nalapho isebenza, imenyu yenguqulo, **Mayelana ne-PartCraft**, inkinobho yemodi yesikrini kanye noshintsho lwetimu Iphepha / Imodeli.

- **Iphepha** wumugqa omnyama esikhwameni elikhanyayo. **Imodeli** wumugqa okhanyayo kusikhangiso esimnyama. Imibala yesikethi ilandela itimu.
- {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} (igiya, noma {{key:Mod+,}}) inethebhu elithi {{ui:PartCraft}}: iyiphi inkinobho yemouse ejikeleza i-view, ivili lemouse noma i-trackpad, nezihlobanisi lapho udweba. Bheka [Izilungiselelo ze-PartCraft](topic:settings).
- Inkinobho yemodi yesikrini, noma {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}}, ijikeleza phakathi kwe-normal, full screen, kanye ne-full screen nebha ezifihliwe. Nebha ezifihliwe, hambisa isikhombi ngaphezulu komphetho (noma thinta lapho) ukuze ziphinde zivele. {{key:Esc}} ibuyisela ku-normal. Lesi sinqamuleli yi-{{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} nakuMac, hhayi ⌘.

## Umlando (ngakwesobunxele)

Ipaneli ngakwesobunxele wumlando wezici zengxenye, uhlu i-PartCraft eyakha kusuka phezulu kuya phansi.

- Ikhanda liwigama lengxenye ("Part1" kuze kube usuku ululungise igama).
- **Indiza ephambili**, **Indiza ephezulu** kanye **Indiza yesokudla** zivela kuqala. Chofoza enye ukuze uyikhethe; iyakhanyisa ku-view.
- Ngaphansi kwazo kukhona izici zakho ngokuhlelwa kwazo. Isikethi esisetshenziswa yisici sibhalwe ngaphansi kwalesi sici.
- Iflegi ekugcineni komugqa ikutshela isimo sawo:

| Iflegi | Okusho |
|---|---|
| (akukho) | Kwakhiwe, nesikethi sichazwe ngokuphelele |
| − | Isikethi esingakachazwa ngokuphelele (singasanyakaza) |
| + | Isikethi esichazwe ngokweqile |
| ⚠ | Kwakhiwe, nesixwayiso (hlola ukuze ufunde) |
| ⚠ error | Isici sehlulekile. Hlola ukuze ufunde ukuthi kungani, noma chofoza |
| ⚠ not built | Isici sidinga esinye esehlulekile noma esivaliwe |
| off | Kuvinjiwe |

- Ngakwesokudla kwegama lesici kubonakala inombolo yaso enkulu, njengo "25 mm" we-Extrude noma "Ø6.6 thru" wembobo. Chofoza kabili inombolo, noma cindezela {{key:F2}} emugqeni, ukuze uyishintshe lapho. Bheka [Shintsha usayizi](topic:change-sizes).
- Chofoza isici ukuze usihlele ngakwesokunene. Chofoza kabili isikethi ukuze usivule.
- Uma ubuyela emuva, umugqa othi **Ibha yokubuyela emuva · qhubeka phambili** uvela ohlwini. Izici ngaphansi kwawo zihlwenywe ngegrey futhi azakhiwe.
- Uma umugqa ugxilile, {{key:Up}} ne-{{key:Down}} zihambisa phakathi kwemigqa, ne-{{key:Alt+Up}} / {{key:Alt+Down}} zihambisa isici ngokwaso, lapho umlando uvuma.

Kwinhlanganisela leli phaneli libonisa **Izingxenye** (eyokuqala imakwe ngokuthi "ibekwe phansi") no**Amajoyinti** esikhundleni salokho.

### Izici zengxenye

Ngaphansi komlando, ibhokisi le-**Ingxenye** lidweba **Ivolumu**, **Indawo yobuso**, **Usayizi (X × Y × Z)**, **Imigodi edlule**, **Izidumbu**, **Izinxantathu**, inani **Lezici** nokuthi zingaki **Ezihlulekile**. Ivolumu neendawo zesolidi elinobuso elakhiwe yi-mesh kernel, zincane kancane kunenani langempela lezimo ezijikelezayo (bheka [Izici nezinketho zazo](topic:features#curves-resolution)).

Kwinhlanganisela ibhokisi linekhanda elithi **Inhlanganisela**: Izingxenye, Izibonelo, Amajoyinti, Akuxhunyiwe kanye Nokungenelela.

## I-view ye-3D (phakathi)

I-view ibonisa isolidi, amapuleni asisekelo nanoma yisiphi isikethi esingakasetshenziswa yisici.

Amapuleni asisekelo adwetshwa ngokugcwele uma ingxenye ingenalutho. Kuthi ngemva kokuba nesolidi azihambe emuva: adwetshwe ngokukhanya, ukuze angamboza ingxenye, nokuchofoza kuwelele engxenyeni. Ayabuya ngokugcwele uma ukhetha lapho udweba khona ({{ui:Isikethi}} ngaphandle kokukhetha) nalapho ukhetha enye emlandweni. {{ui:Imibono}} › {{ui:Izindiza eziyisisekelo}} iyazifihla ngokuphelele; zisabonakala uma ukhetha lapho udweba khona. I-PartCraft iyakhumbula leso sinqumo.

- Dudula ukuze uphendule ingxenye, dudula ngesokudla noma ngaphakathi ukuze uhambe, ukhulise ngevili endaweni yesikhombi. Imininingwane ku-[Izinkinobho nemouse](topic:keyboard).
- Hambisa phezu kobuso, umphetho noma indiza ukubona ukukhanya. Chofoza ukuze ukhethe. Ubuso buyakhanya bonke, nemiphetho idwetshwa ngokuphelele futhi ikhethwa ngokuphelele. {{key:Shift}}+chofoza ukuqoqa izinto eziningi. Zihlala zimakiwe.
- Ikhubo le-view ngakwesokudla ngaphezulu liguqula ingxenye: chofoza ubuso, umphetho noma ikhona ukubuka kusuka khona, dudula ukuze uphendule; inkinobho yendlu ibuyela kusimo sokuqala. Bheka [Ikhubo le-view](topic:view-cube).
- Uma ukhetha i-Extrude ebonisa umcibisholo oweorenji ohlangothini lwayo, dudula ukuze ushintshe ukujula noma i-engeli, noma bhala ebhokisini eliseduze nawo. Bheka [Shintsha usayizi](topic:change-sizes#drag-it-in-the-view).
- Uma ingxenye iyize, ibhokisi elithi **Qala ingxenye** lidweba izinyathelo zokuqala.
- Wisa ifayela le-`.partcraft.json` noma le-`.partcraft-asm.json` endaweni ye-view ukuze uyivule. Ifayela le-`.step`, `.stp`, `.stl`, `.obj`, `.3mf` noma `.3dm` elishiywe lapho livula njengesidumbu esiqinile.

### Ngenkathi ukhetha imiphetho noma izindawo

Ngemva kwe-{{ui:Fillet}}, {{ui:Chamfer}} noma {{ui:Shell}} ngaphandle kokukhetha lutho (noma {{ui:Khetha imiphetho}} kwisici esinjalo), ingxenye ibonakala njengangaphambi kwalelo sici. Chofoza imiphetho noma izindawo ukuze wengeze noma ukhiphe; izimpawu zazo zikhombisa okukhethiwe. {{ui:Kuqediwe}} noma {{key:Esc}} iyaqeda. Bheka [Jikeleza, gqumuza nokhande ngaphakathi](topic:fillet-chamfer-shell).

### Esheethini lomdwebo

{{ui:Umdwebo}} ithathela indawo i-view ye-3D ngeshidi lomdwebo le-A3. Ivili lemouse lisondeza, dudula ngesokunxele noma ngaphakathi uyashelelisa, futhi {{key:F}} iyalingana. Bheka [Yenza umdwebo](topic:drawing-pdf).

### Ngenkathi isikethi sivuliwe

I-view iphendukela ukubheka ngqo indiza yesikethi, nesendlalelo esingu-2D sivela phezulu. Ingxenye ibonisa kuphela izici ezenziwe ngaphambi kwalesi sikethi. Ibha encane ngakwesobunxele esiphezulu ibonisa ichip yesimo sesikethi ("Ichazwe ngokuphelele", "Ichazwe ngokungaphelele · amadigri angu-3 enkululeko" njalo njalo) negama lalo, njengo "Sketch2 on a face of Extrude1".

## Ipaneli yezakhiwo (ngakwesokudla)

Ipaneli ngakwesokudla ibonisa lokho okukhethiwe, nekhanda layo libiza:

- **Ingxenye** (akukhethiwe lutho): igama layo **Igama** nesilungiselelo se-**Amajika**.
- Indiza: inkinobho ethi {{ui:Sketch kuyo}}.
- **Ubuso**: uhlobo lwalo (ubuso obuthe, isilinda nerediyasi nomugqa wayo omaphakathi, ukhoni, ibhola noma ithoyisi), indawo yalo, isici esilenzile, nakubuso obuthe {{ui:Sketch kulobu buso}}, {{ui:Bheka ngqo}}, {{ui:Khipha ubuso njenge-DXF}}, {{ui:Extrude kusuka kulo}}, {{ui:Sika kulo}} kanye {{ui:Umgodi lapha}} (bheka [Dudula noma ukhande ubuso bonke](topic:extrude-and-cut#pull-or-push-a-whole-face)).
- **Umphetho**: uhlobo lwawo (oqondile, isongo nesisekelo nesilinganiso sesongo, noma ijika), ubude bawo, nezinhlangothi ezimbili okuphakathi kwazo (njengo "Extrude1 end face and Extrude1 side"), nezinkinobho {{ui:Fillet}} ne-{{ui:Chamfer}}.
- Imiphetho noma izindawo eziningi ezikhethwe nge-{{key:Shift}}: inani lazo, ne-{{ui:Fillet}} ne-{{ui:Chamfer}} zemiphetho, kanye ne-{{ui:Sula}}.
- Isici: izinombolo nezinketho zaso, izilinganiso zesikethi saso, bese {{ui:Vimbela}}, {{ui:Buyela lapha}}, {{ui:Hambisa phezulu}}, {{ui:Hambisa phansi}}, {{ui:Susa}} negama laso **Igama**.
- Isikethi esivuliwe: isimo sasho, izihlobanisi ongazengeza ekukhethweni, uhlu lwezihlobanisi nezilinganiso, **Engeza ngamakhoordinethi**, kanye {{ui:Phuma kusiketshi}}, {{ui:Lingana}} no-{{ui:Khipha njenge-DXF}}.
- Esheethini lomdwebo: izilungiselelo zesigaba nezemininingwane, noma uphawu olukhethiwe lwe-GD&T.
- Kwinhlanganisela: inhlanganisela, ingxenye ekhethiwe, noma ijoyinti elikhethiwe.

Yonke ibhokisi lenombolo lisebenza uma ucindezela {{key:Enter}} noma {{key:Tab}}, noma uchofoze endaweni. Ingxenye iyakhiwa kabusha ngokushesha; akukho inkinobho ethi KULUNGILE. Sebenzisa {{key:Mod+Z}} ukubuyela emuva.

## Ibha yesimo (ngaphansi)

- Ngakwesobunxele, izixhumanisi zesikhombi esikethini (kuphela lapho kuvulwe isikethi).
- Phakathi, imilayezo: okufanele wenze ngokulandelayo, nokuthi kungani kwenqatshelwe into. Yifunde uma kungabonakali ukuthi kwenzeka okuthile.
- Ngakwesokudla eduze, ichip ye-kernel: njalo ithi "Kernel yemeshi" kulolu nguqulo. Hlola ukuze ufunde ukuthi kusho ukuthini. Bheka [I-mesh kernel](topic:solid-kernels).
- Ngakwesokudla, ichip: "Iyakhiwa kabusha…" ngenkathi ingxenye iyakhiwa kabusha, bese "Yakhiwa kabusha ngo-… ms" uma konke kuhamba kahle, noma "1 isici sihlulekile". Kwinhlanganisela ithi "Ibekwe ku-… ms" noma ibala izinkinga zokuxhuma.

Bheka futhi [Qala umanuwali ku-Illustrate](topic:illustrate): indawo yokusebenza eyenza imanuwali yenhlanganisela ngezingxenye nezinhlanganisela zakho.

## Esikrinini esincane

Kuya ku-760 pixels obubanzi, njengefoni, i-view igcina isikrini: ibha yamathuluzi iwumugqa owodwa oskrolelwa ngokuqondile, namaphaneli avulela njengamashidi kusuka kubha engaphansi. {{ui:Amathuluzi}} ifihla ibha yamathuluzi futhi {{ui:Hambisa}} iyibeka ngaphansi. Bheka [Trackpad, ukuthinta nezikrini ezincane](topic:trackpad-and-touch).
