Lonke usayizi kwingxenye ye-PartCraft yinombolo ongayishintsha kamuva: izilinganiso ezisezikethini zayo nezilungiselelo zezakhi zayo. Akudingeki uvule isikethi ukuze ushintshe izilinganiso zaso.

## Shintsha isilinganiso sesikethi kusuka ephanelini

1. Chofoza isakhi emlandweni, isibonelo "Extrude1".
2. Ngaphansi kwezilungiselelo ze-extrude yayo, **Izilinganiso zesikethi (Sketch1)** zidwebela yonke isilinganiso sesikethi sayo: "Ibanga", "Idayamitha", "Ikona (°)" njalo njalo.
3. Bhala inani elisha kubhokisi elilodwa bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} noma {{key:Tab}}.

Isikethi sixazulula ngenani elisha, bese ingxenye yonke iyakhiwa kabusha. Ukukhetha isikethi ngokwaso kubonisa uhlu olufanayo njenge-**Izilinganiso zokushayela**.

Umsakazo noma idayamitha kumele kube ngaphezu kuka-zero, nama-engeli abe phakathi kuka-0 no-180 degrees; ibha yesimo iyasho uma kungenjalo.

:::tip
Ibhokisi ngalinye yiliphi? Amabhokisi adwetshwa ngokulandelana kwezilinganiso ezenziwe ngakho. Uma ungaqinisekile, vula isikethi (chofoza kabili kuso), chofoza kabili isilinganiso endaweni yokudweba, bese ushintsha lapho.
:::

## Faka inombolo enkulu ngokushesha

Khetha isakhi emlandweni (noma uengeze kuphela) bese uthayipha inombolo: iya ebhokisini elikhulu lesakhi, elishintsha kakhulu (**Ukujula** kwe-Extrude, **Ikona** ye-Revolve, **Idayamitha** yomgodi, **Inani** lomumo, **Umsakazo** we-Fillet, udonga lwe-Shell). Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze uyisete, noma {{key:Esc}} ukuze ugcine eyakudala. Ukungeza i-Extrude kutshelwa ibha yesimo: "Extrude1 yengeziwe. Shintsha izinombolo zayo ngakwesokudla, noma thayipha inombolo manje ngokujula kwayo."

## Shintsha inombolo emlandweni

Umugqa ngamunye wesakhi emlandweni ubonisa inombolo yawo eyinhloko eceleni kwegama lawo: "Extrude1 25 mm", "Hole1 Ø6.6 thru", "Fillet1 R2", "LPattern1 4 × 20 mm". Chofoza kabili inombolo (noma khetha umugqa bese ucindezela {{key:F2}}) bese kuvulwa ibhokisi lapho. Thayipha inani elisha bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}; {{key:Esc}} igcina eyakudala. Lisebenza njengebhokisi lesakhi ngakwesokudla: imikhawulo efanayo, `=` ukuze uthayiphe isibalo njengalesi `=width/2`, nesinyathelo esisodwa sokuHlehlisa.

Ku-Extrude, Hole noma kumumo, ukuchofoza kabili noma kuphi emugqeni kuvula ibhokisi. Ku-Fillet, Chamfer, Shell, Uhlaka noma Thread ukuchofoza kabili egameni kusakhetha amaphethelo noma ubuso bawo, ngakho chofoza kabili inombolo ngokwayo. Izikethi azinayo inombolo emugqeni: ukuchofoza kabili kuvula isikethi.

## Donsa embonweni

Khetha i-Extrude emlandweni (noma uyichofoze embonweni, bese umugqa wayo) kuthi umcibisholo we-orenji umi ebusweni bawo bokugcina, nokujula kubhokisi eliseceleni kwawo. Donsa umcibisholo: ingxenye iyalandela ngesikhathi udonsa, futhi uma uyeqa, kusetha ukujula okusha njengesinyathelo esisodwa ({{key:Mod+Z}} iyambuyisela). Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ngesikhathi udonsa ukuze ubuyele lapho uqale khona. Ukujula kunyakaza ngezinyathelo zengxenye eyodwa kwelishumi lobukhulu bayo: i-1 mm ku-Extrude engu-25 mm, i-0.1 mm kwengu-3 mm.

I-Revolve ithola umcibisholo ogobile ekugcineni kokujikeka kwayo: wudonse uzungeze i-axis ukuze uguqule ikona, ngamadigri aphelele kusukela ku-1° kuya ku-360°.

Noma chofoza ebhokisini eliseceleni komcibisholo, uthayiphe inani (noma `=` nesibalo) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Akukho mcibisholo ku-Extrude edlula **Dlula konke** noma ifika ebusweni, noma kunani elilawulwa yisibalo: shintsha lezo ngakwesokudla.

## Izinombolo ezishayela ingxenye

| Lapho | Okungashintshwa |
|---|---|
| Isikethi | Zonke izilinganiso: ubude, amabanga, imisakazo, idayamitha, ama-engeli |
| Extrude ne-Sika | Ukujula, isimo sokuphela, indlela, i-boss noma ukusika |
| Revolve kanye nokusika okujikelezayo | Ikona, i-axis, i-boss noma ukusika |
| Umgodi | Idayamitha, ukujula, dlula konke, idayamitha nokujula kwe-counterbore |
| Fillet, Chamfer, Shell | Umsakazo, ibanga, ukuqina kodonga nendlela, namaphethelo noma ubuso |
| Olayini kanye Ngokujikeleza | Inani, ukuhlukaniswa noma ikona, indlela noma i-axis |
| Ingxenye | Amajika (ukuthi imibumbo ejikelezayo ihlukaniswa kahle kangakanani) |
| Umdwebo | Lapho isigaba sisika khona, usayizi nesikali sesiqephu, amanani e-GD&T |

## Hlela ingxenye ukuze izinombolo ezifanele zishayele

Izinombolo onikeza zona yizona ongazishintsha. Izinkambiso ezimbalwa zenza ushintsho lwamuva luziphathe kahle:

- **Linganisa okubalulekile emsebenzini.** Uma umgodi kumele uhlale ama-15 mm ukusuka emaphethelweni, uwulinganise ukusuka kuleyo mphetheloe, hhayi emsukeni.
- **Chaza izikethi ngokuphelele.** Ngakho ushintsho luhambisa okufanele kuphela (bona [Chaza isikethi ngokuphelele](topic:relations-dimensions)).
- **Sebenzisa ukuxhumana ngenjongo.** Ukulingana kumigodi emibili kusho ukuthi idayamitha eyodwa ishayela bobabili. Izimo ezilinganayo: beka i-origin emugqeni wesikhungo, noma sebenzisa Midpoint.
- **Dweba izikethi ezisebusweni obenziwe yizakhi.** Isikethi esisebusweni bokugcina be-extrude siyanyakaza uma ujula ushintsha.
- **Sebenzisa umumo esikhundleni sokukopisha.** Umgodi owodwa owenziwe umumo kayisishiyagalombili unedayamitha eyodwa yokushintsha.

## Ngaphezu kwezinombolo ezingodwa

- **Inombolo eyodwa eshayela okuningi:** sebenzisa amapharamitha nezibalo: [Shayela ingxenye ngamapharamitha](topic:parameters).
- **Izinhlobo eziningi zengxenye eyodwa:** londoloza ngayinye njenge [ukucushwa](topic:design-tables); zigcinwa efayeleni lengxenye.
- **Izibalo ebhokisini lenombolo:** thayipha `=40/2` noma `=width / 2`. Lokho kwenza isibalo; bona [Izibalo ezindleleni zombili](topic:equations-two-way).
- **Amayunithi e-inch:** ingxenye ibonisa ama-millimeter. Ebhokisini lenombolo elinesimpawu `=`, noma kupharamitha, i-`2in` iguqulwa ibe ngu-50.8.
