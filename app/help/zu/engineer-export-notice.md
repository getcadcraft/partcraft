:::note
**Izilawuli zokuthumela phandle.** Injiniyela nezikhiqizi zayo ngamathuluzi wokuklama ajwayelekile akhiwe ngezibalo ezishicilelwe zobunjiniyela bokufundisa. Asisho ukuthi noma yisiphi isikhiqizi, uklama, ishidi ledatha noma ifayela elithunyelwe liyakhululeka ezilawulweni zokuthumela phandle noma ezijezisweni, noma ukuthi lingathunyelwa ngokusemthethweni, lidluliselwe noma lisetshenziswe kunoma yiliphi izwe noma yimuphi umuntu. Wena ubhekele ukuhlukanisa okudizayinile nokulandela imithetho yokuthumela phandle nezijeziso ezikusebenzela. Lesi yisaziso esisisisekelo: sihlela ukushintsha lezi zilawuli ukuze zihambisane nemithetho yokuthumela phandle yezwe lomsuka.
:::

Lamagama afanayo ashiwo ngaphansi kwawo wonke amashidi edatha (kwiphaneli, ku-PDF nakufayela le-JSON) nasecaleni kwanoma yisiphi isikhiqizi esiphethe isaziso, njengenozulu.

Injiniyela ayisiyo uhlelo lwayo: iyingxenye ehlanganyelwe yazo zonke izinhlelo ze-CadCraft ezayifakayo, futhi isaziso sifana kuzo zonke. Bheka [Izingxenye ezivela kwifiziksi: izikhiqizi](topic:engineer-generators).
