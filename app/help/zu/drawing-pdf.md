I-PartCraft yenza ishidi lomdwebo kusuka engxenyeni: imibono ephambili, ephezulu nesokudla ngeprojekshini yekona yesithathu, umbono we-isometric, usayizi jikelele nebhokisi lesihloko eshidini elisuka ku-A4 kuya ku-A0 noma ku-ANSI A kuya ku-E. Ngaphezu kwalokho ungangeza isigaba, umbono wemininingwane kanye nezimpawu ze-GD&T. Ishidi liyavuselelwa lapho ingxenye ishintsha, nezilungiselelo zalo zigcinwa kufayela lengxenye.

## Vula ishidi lomdwebo

1. Yakha ingxenye. (Uma kungakakhiwa lutho: "Yakha isolid kuqala, bese wenza umdwebo wayo.")
2. Khetha {{ui:Umdwebo}} ebhalweni lamathuluzi.

Imodeli ye-3D ithathelwa indawo yishidi. Ibha yamathuluzi ibonisa {{ui:Idatamu}}, {{ui:Ifreyimu}}, {{ui:Ukuqeda}}, {{ui:Imininingwane}}, {{ui:PDF}} no {{ui:Emuva}}, nephaneli esokudla inesihloko **Umdwebo**. Ibha yesimo ithi indlela imibono eyenziwe ngayo: "Umdwebo (amajika adwetshwa njengama-facet). Idatamu, Ifreyimu kanye Nokuqeda kubeka i-GD&T embonweni; iphaneli isetha isigaba nombono wemininingwane." Imibono idwetshwa kusuka kuma-facet e-mesh kernel, ngakho imiphetho eyindilinga iba yimigqa emifushane eqondile; ama-arc eqondile adinga i-kernel eqondile, into lolu hlobo lolu alunayo.

Zulazula eshidini: skrola ukuze usondeze, donsa ngenkinobho yesokudla noma ephakathi ukuze ushelelise, cindezela {{key:F}} ukuze ulinganise ishidi. Lapho ishidi ligxile, {{key:Plus}} no {{key:Minus}} zisondeza, futhi {{key:Shift}} kanye nezinkinobho zomcibisholo zishelelisa.

Khetha {{ui:Emuva}}, {{ui:Buyela kumodeli}} noma cindezela {{key:Esc}} (lapho kungekho okukhethiwe futhi nethuluzi lingasebenzi) ukuze ubuyele kumodeli ye-3D.

## Okusemshidini

- **Ishidi oliyikhethayo**, lelandiscape (i-A3 uma ungakhethi elinye), linomngcele nebhokisi lesihloko: isihloko, inkampani, inombolo yomdwebo, ukuguqulwa, impahla yokwakha, kudwetshwe / kuhlolwe / kugunyazwe ngu-, usuku, isikali, usayizi weshidi, "Izinyunithi: amamilimitha · Ukuprojekta kwekona yesithathu", nokuthi imibono ichanekile noma inama-facet.
- **Ibhulokhi lokuguqulwa** esikhonkwaneni esingenhla kwesokudla uma usuwengezile ukuguqulwa.
- **Imibono yangaphambili, yangaphezulu nesokudla**: umbono wangaphezulu ngaphezulu kombono wangaphambili, nowesokudla ngakwesokudla kwawo. Imigqa efihliwe idashiwe.
- **Umbono we-isometric** lapho kukhona indawo.
- **Usayizi jikelele**: ububanzi nobude embonweni wangaphambili, ukujula embonweni wangaphezulu.
- **Isikali esisodwa** semibono eyinhloko esuka kuthotho lwe-ISO (1:1, 1:2, 1:5 … 2:1, 5:1, 10:1), okukhethwa ukuze zilingane.

## Khetha usayizi weshidi

Eshidini lomdwebo, ngaphandle kokukhethwa kwanoma yini, khetha {{ui:Ishidi}} ephaneli esokudla: **A4, A3, A2, A1, A0** (ISO 216) noma **ANSI A, B, C, D, E** (11 × 8.5 kuya ku-44 × 34 in). Imibono iyahlelwa kabusha ngesikali se-ISO esikhulu kakhulu esilingana, ngakho ishidi elikhulu lingabonisa ingxenye ikhulu.

## Gcwalisa ibhokisi lesihloko

Izinkambu ze-**Ibhokisi lesihloko** ephaneli esokudla ziya ngqo eshidini: {{ui:Isihloko}}, {{ui:Inombolo yomdwebo}}, {{ui:Ukuguqulwa}}, {{ui:Inkampani}}, {{ui:Idwetshwe ngu-}}, {{ui:Ihlolwe ngu-}}, {{ui:Igunyazwe ngu-}}, {{ui:Usuku}} (unyaka-inyanga-usuku), {{ui:Ukubekezelela okujwayelekile}} kanye {{ui:Ukuqeda}} (lezi ezimbili zishicilelwa ngaphezulu kwebhokisi lesihloko). Bhala ebhokisini bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} noma uchofoze kwenye indawo. Ibhokisi elingenalutho lishiya iseli lalo lingenalutho, ngaphandle kwalezi:

- **Isihloko** sibonisa igama lengxenye kuze kube yilapho ubhala elinye.
- **Usuku** lubonisa usuku lwanamuhla kuze kube yilapho ubhala elinye.
- **Ukuguqulwa** kubonisa ukuguqulwa okusha kakhulu kuhlu kuze kube yilapho ubhala elinye.

**Impahla yokwakha** ivela engxenyeni: chofoza esikhaleni esingenalutho kumodeli, bese ukhetha {{ui:Impahla yokwakha}} ephaneli yengxenye. Impahla efanayo inika izici zobunzima ze-Measure ukuminyana kwayo.

## Engeza ukuguqulwa

Ngaphansi kwe-**Ukuguqulwa**, chofoza {{ui:Engeza ukuguqulwa}}. Umugqa ngamunye omusha uthatha inhlamvu elandelayo (A, B, C …; ngemva kwe-Z kuza i-AA) nosuku lwanamuhla. Bhala okushintshile nowenze ushintsho. {{ui:Susa}} isusa umugqa. Ibhulokhi lokuguqulwa esikhonkwaneni esingenhla kwesokudla leshidi liwudrowa ohlwini, owadalwa kuqala, kuze kube ngu-20.

Usayizi weshidi, ibhokisi lesihloko, ukuguqulwa nempahla kugcinwa kufayela lengxenye, futhi noshintsho ngalunye lungesinyathelo esisodwa se-{{ui:Hlehlisa}}.

## Engeza umbono wesigaba

Ingxenye inqunywa indiza futhi ukusikeka kugedadiwe. Njengemibono yonke, isigaba sidwetshwe kusuka kuma-facet, ngakho imiphetho eyindilinga iba yimigqa emifushane eqondile.

1. Ephaneli esokudla, setha **Isigaba A-A** sibe **Embonweni wangaphambili**, **Embonweni wangaphezulu** noma **Embonweni wesokudla**.
2. Isigaba sivela eshidini, sigedadiwe lapho ukusika kudlula ezintweni, nomugqa wokusika kanye nemicibisholo "A" embonweni okuthathwe kuwo.
3. Setha **Sika ku (mm)** ukuze uhambise indiza yokusika. Iqala iphakathi kwengxenye.

Setha **Isigaba A-A** sibe **Akukho** ukuze uyisuse.

## Engeza umbono wemininingwane

1. Setha **Imininingwane B** embonweni okuzokhuliswa (noma khetha {{ui:Imininingwane}} ebhalweni lamathuluzi).
2. Chofoza phakathi kwendawo okuzokhuliswa, embonweni lowo. Isiyingi esimakwe "B" siyachaza, futhi **Imininingwane B** elikhuliwe livela eshidini ngesikali sayo, isibonelo "Detail B (2:1)".
3. Setha **Detail radius (mm)** ngosayizi wesiyingi kanye **Detail scale** ngokukhula: 2, 4, 5 noma 10 × ishidi.

Imininingwane ingathathwa emibonweni yangaphambili, yangaphezulu, yesokudla noma yesigaba, hhayi kumbono we-isometric. Uma kungekho ndawo yazo zonke imibono, iphaneli ithi "No room on the sheet for: detail. Make the detail smaller or drop a view."

## Engeza ithebula lemigodi

Ithebula lemigodi liluhlu lwemigodi yengxenye nezindawo zayo, ukuze kubholwe noma kwenziwe i-CNC: khetha **Ithebula lemigodi** › **Embonweni wangaphezulu** (noma wangaphambili, noma wesokudla) ephaneli.

- Yonke imigodi ebonakala ngokuqondile kulowo mbono ithola ithegi eseceleni kwayo: **A1**, **A2** … ngosayizi wokuqala, **B1** … ngolandelayo.
- Ithebula lidwetsha ithegi ngalinye, **X** no **Y** ngamamilimitha, nosayizi wayo: "Ø14 THRU", "Ø6.6 × 10 DEEP", i-counterbore noma i-countersink, noma incazelo yezinga lomgodi othrediwe.
- X no-Y zilinganiswa kusukela ekhoneni elingezansi kwesobunxele lomphambili wombono, okumakwe **X0 Y0** eshidini.
- Imigodi eyenziwe ngamaphethini ifakiwe. Imigodi engakahlali ngokuqondile kumbono ibalwa ephaneli futhi ayifakwa ethebhulini.

Ithebula liyabekwa endaweni engenalutho eshidini nakuPDF.

## Beka izimpawu ze-GD&T nezokuqeda ubuso

1. Khetha {{ui:Idatamu}}, {{ui:Ifreyimu}} (ifreyimu yokulawula isici) noma {{ui:Ukuqeda}} (uphawu lokuqeda ubuso).
2. Chofoza iphuzu embonweni lapho kufanele lihlanganiswe. Liyakhomba eyona mugqa edwetshiwe eseduze ngaphakathi kwe-2.5 mm.
3. Uphawu luvela linomugqa, likhethiwe, nephaneli esokudla ibonisa izilungiselelo zalo.

Hudula uphawu ukuze uhambise; umugqa uhlala endaweni yephoyinti. Ngophawu olukhethiwe, izinkinobho zomcibisholo zilihambisa ngo-1 mm. {{key:Delete}} noma {{key:Backspace}} (noma {{ui:Susa}} ephaneli) iyasusa; {{ui:Kuqediwe}} noma {{key:Esc}} iyayikhipha ekukhethweni. Konke kushintsha kuhlu lokuhlehlisa.

### Idatamu

**Inhlamvu**: u-A kuya ku-Z, ngaphandle kwe-I, O no-Q (zifundeka njengo-1, 0 no-O). Idatamu ngalinye lithatha inhlamvu elandelayo engasetshenziswanga.

### Ifreyimu yokulawula isici

| Izilungiselelo | Okukhethwayo |
|---|---|
| **Tolerance** | Straightness, Flatness, Circularity, Cylindricity, Profile of a line, Profile of a surface, Perpendicularity, Angularity, Parallelism, Position, Concentricity and Symmetry (kokubili kususiwe ku-Y14.5-2018, kumakwe njalo), Circular runout, Total runout |
| **Zone (mm)** | Inani lokubekezelela, okuzenzakalelayo 0.1 |
| **Diameter zone (⌀)** | Ezici ezinendawo esilindrikhalini; ivulekile ngokuzenzakalela ku-Position |
| **Material** | RFS (akukho), MMC (Ⓜ), LMC (Ⓛ), lapho isici sivumela |
| **Primary / Secondary / Tertiary datum** | Kufika kuma-datum amathathu ayinhlamvu, ngayinye nge-**… modifier** (MMB, LMB) lapho livunyelwe |

I-PartCraft ihlola ifreyimu ngemithetho ye-ASME Y14.5 futhi itshela okungalungile phezulu kwephaneli, isibonelo "Flatness is a form tolerance: it takes no datum references." noma "Position needs at least one datum reference." Umugqa othi **Ifundwa** uchaza ifreyimu ngamagama.

### Ukuqeda ubuso

| Izilungiselelo | Okukhethwayo | Okuzenzakalelayo |
|---|---|---|
| **Ra (µm)** | Ukuqina okuphakathi | 1.6 |
| **Process** | noma yiyiphi inqubo, ukucutshungulwa, akukho impahla esusiwe | ukucutshungulwa |
| **Note** | Kuya kumabhoksi angu-40, njengolayini noma inqubo | |

Ngaphandle kokukhethwa, iphaneli ibala **Izinothi eshidini**, nganye no **Khetha**.

## Thumela i-PDF

Khetha {{ui:PDF}} ebhalweni lamathuluzi, {{ui:Thumela i-PDF}} ephaneli, noma {{ui:Thumela}}, bese {{ui:Umdwebo (PDF): imibono, isigaba, imininingwane, GD&T}} kumodeli. I-PDF iyalandelwa njenge-`<part name> - drawing.pdf`, nebha yesimo ibala okuqukethwe kuyo.

Phrinta ngo-"Usayizi wangempela" ephepheni losayizi weshidi ukuze isikali sihlale siqondile.

## Vumela ishidi lenze lokho

- **Ishidi › Okuzenzakalelayo** likhetha iphepha ngawe: isikali esikhulu esijwayelekile esiphansi noma esilingana no-1:1, ishidi elincane kakhulu le-A elibamba imibono kulesi sikali (uhlu lukhombisa okukhethiwe).
- **Linganisa imigodi** lilinganisa imigodi embonweni obona kakhulu kuyo ngokuqondile: uphawu lwesikhungo kwelinye ngalinye, isiyingi esisodwa ngosayizi ("2× Ø6 THRU" nomugqa wokukhomba), nezindawo zazo ezilinganiswa kusukela ekhoneni elingezansi kwesobunxele lombono. Ububanzi, ubude nokujula jikelele kuhlala kukhona.
