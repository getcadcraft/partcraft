Imiyalo ye-PartCraft ifinyelelwa ngendlela efanayo noma yiyiphi. **Isitayela semenyu** sinquma kuphela lapho zihlala khona esikrinini, lapho zibizwa khona nokuthi yiziphi okhiye ezizisebenzisayo. Khetha esinye ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} ({{key:Mod+,}}) › {{ui:PartCraft}} › {{ui:Imenyu, okhiye nemouse}}; ushintsho luyasebenza ngokushesha, futhi lesi siphequluli siyakukhumbula.

## Izitayela ezimbili

| Isitayela | Okutholayo |
|---|---|
| {{ui:PartCraft}} | I-toolbar yePartCraft: i-workspace switcher, File, Sketch, Features, Drawing, Assembly, Edit ne-View. Okhiye {{key:S}} sketch, {{key:F}} lingana, {{key:M}} linganisa, {{key:X}} ukubuka kwesigaba. Qalisa. |
| {{ui:Isihlahla sezakhi}} | I-ribbon elungiswe njengoba izinhlelo ze-mechanical CAD ezisekelwe ezici zilungisa zazo: imenyu ye-{{ui:Ifayela}} (nazo zonke izinto ezithunyelwayo), izinkinobho ezisheshayo, namathebhu {{ui:Izici}}, {{ui:Isikethi}}, {{ui:Ubuso}}, {{ui:Hlola}}, {{ui:Umdwebo}}, {{ui:Inhlanganisela}}, {{ui:Izifundo}} (izindawo zokusebenza nezithuluzi zazo), {{ui:Illustrate}} (amathuluzi amanual esikhaleni se-Illustrate; amathuluzi aso ashintsha kuqala abe yi-Illustrate) kanye ne-{{ui:Ukubuka}}. {{ui:Izici}} iqala ngo-{{ui:Isikethi}}, ngoba ingxenye iqala ngesketch. Okhiye abengeziwe: {{key:E}} extrude, {{key:H}} hole, kanye {{key:V}} bese inhlamvu yombono: {{key:V}} {{key:F}} front, {{key:V}} {{key:B}} back, {{key:V}} {{key:T}} top, {{key:V}} {{key:O}} bottom, {{key:V}} {{key:R}} right, {{key:V}} {{key:L}} left, {{key:V}} {{key:I}} isometric, {{key:V}} {{key:N}} normal to. Ku-3D view inkinobho ephakathi iyazungeza, {{key:Shift}} nenkinobho ephakathi ishelelisa, inkinobho yesokudla ishelelisa kanti inkinobho yokunxele ikhetha kuphela. |

Zonke izitayela zigcina {{key:Mod+Z}}, {{key:Mod+Y}}, {{key:Mod+S}}, {{key:Mod+O}}, {{key:Mod+C}}, {{key:Mod+X}}, {{key:Mod+V}}, {{key:Mod+D}} ne-{{key:Mod+A}}.

Ithuluzi lenye indawo yokusebenza liyashintsha liye kuyo kuqala; ithuluzi le-Design elikhethwe kwenye indawo yokusebenza libuyela ku-Design kuqala. Amathuluzi e-sketch alinda i-sketch, amathuluzi okudweba alinda ishidi lokudweba, amathuluzi kahlanganiso alinda uhlanganiso: ibha yesimo ikutshela okufanele uqale ngakho.

:::tip
Ngaphakathi kwe-sketch, esihlokweni sokudweba, kuhlanganiso nakwezinye izindawo zokusebenza, izinhlamvu zigcina incazelo yazo lapho (i-{{key:L}} umugqa, i-{{key:R}} irektangele, i-{{key:J}} ukuhlangana …) kuzo zonke izitayela. I-{{key:Delete}}, i-{{key:Esc}}, i-{{key:Enter}}, i-{{key:Tab}}, i-{{key:Space}} nezimpini zigcina okwazo njalo. Okhiye bolungiselelo lwePartCraft isitayela sakho esabela komunye umyalo abalungeli ukusebenza, kanti ibha yesimo ikhombisa lapho umyalo wabo uye khona.
:::

## Yenza kube ngokwakho

1. Khetha isitayela esiseduze kakhulu nalokho ofuna, bese uchofoza {{ui:Lungisa…}}.
2. Ku-{{ui:Okhiye nemenyu}}, thola umyalo (ibhokisi lokusesha liyasiza), {{ui:Engeza ukhiye}} bese ucindezela ukhiye, noma okhiye ababili ngokulandelana. Chofoza ukhiye ukuze ukususe.
3. Ngombala we-ribbon, {{ui:Lapho ihlala khona}} ihambisa umyalo kwelinye ithebhu nepaneli, kumenyu ye-{{ui:Ifayela}}, noma ku-{{ui:Okhiye kuphela}}.
4. Ku-{{ui:Imouse}}, setha ivili, izinkinobho ezishelelisa i-sketch, nokuthi yini inkinobho ngayinye ku-3D view.
5. Yinike igama bese uchofoza {{ui:Londoloza}}.

Ukulondoloza isitayela esakhelwe kuqala kwenza ikopi yakho ("My Feature tree"); esakhelwe kuqala asishintshi nanini. Ukhiye onikezwe imiyalo emibili, noma olandela ukhiye wamagama amabili angakayithayiphwa, ubonakala ngokubomvu futhi kumele ulungiswe ngaphambi kokuthi ulondoloze.

## Imouse

- **Ivili lemouse**: {{ui:Ukuskrola phezulu kusondeza ngaphakathi}} (isiqalo) noma {{ui:Ukuskrola phezulu kusondeza ngaphandle}}, nokuthi kusheshe kangakanani. I-pinch yetrackpad isondeza njalo ngendlela eminwe yakho enyakaza ngayo.
- **Shelelisa i-sketch nge**: inkinobho ephakathi, inkinobho yesokudla, noma zombili. Ukuchofoza ngakwesokudla ku-sketch kusaqeda isinyathelo sethuluzi.
- **Donsa ku-3D view**: {{ui:Eyomibuko ngamunye}} igcina isethingi ye-PartCraft ({{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:PartCraft}}: kwesokunxele kuzungeza, noma inkinobho ephakathi izungeza). Noma setha inkinobho ngayinye ukuthi izungeze, ishelelise, isondeze noma ingenzi lutho; {{key:Shift}} nenkinobho yokuzungeza iyashelelisa. Ukuchofoza okungadonsi kukhethe kusaqhubeka.

## Thatha nawe

{{ui:Thumela}} igcina isitayela esisetshenziswayo njengefayela elincane `.ccinput.json`; {{ui:Ngenisa…}} iyengeza, ivela kolunye ikhompyutha noma kumsebenzisi ohlangene naye. Ifayela elingenisiwe lingakhomba kuphela imiyalo yePartCraft: okunye okukuwo kuyasuswa futhi kubalwe ebhalweni yesimo. {{ui:Susa lesi sitayela}} isusa esinye sakho.

:::note
Lezi zilungiselelo zihlala kulesi siphequluli. {{ui:Sebenzisa leli sitayela kuzo zonke izinhlelo}} ngaphansi kwamakhadi wezitayela yenza ukuthi zonke izinhlelo zivule ngesitayela esihambisanayo (i-toolbar yazo, noma i-ribbon), nezinguquko ozenzile kwenye ngayinye. Ukukhetha esinye isitayela kuhlelo olulodwa kushintsha lolo hlelo kuphela.
:::
