## Okuzokwenzayo

Dweba umnsalo esikethini ngendlela efana nalokho okwaziyo: amaphoyinti amathathu kuwo, isikhungo sawo, iradiyasi yawo, ikona eliwuhambisayo noma indlela eyishiya ngayo. Amanani angachofozwa noma athayipheke.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula isikethi (bheka [Izisekelo zokudweba](topic:sketch-basics)).
2. Kwibha yamathuluzi yesikethi, vula uhlu oluseduze kwe-{{ui:Arc}} bese ukhetha indlela, isibonelo {{ui:Arc: Start, End, Radius}}. Izindlela yilezi: Indilinga: Amaphoyinti angu-3; Indilinga: Ukuqala, Isikhungo, Ukuphela; Indilinga: Ukuqala, Isikhungo, Ikona; Indilinga: Ukuqala, Isikhungo, Ubude; Indilinga: Ukuqala, Ukuphela, Ikona; Indilinga: Ukuqala, Ukuphela, Indlela; {{ui:Arc: Start, End, Radius}}; Indilinga: Isikhungo, Ukuqala, Ukuphela; Indilinga: Isikhungo, Ukuqala, Ikona; Indilinga: Isikhungo, Ukuqala, Ubude; kanye ne-{{ui:Arc: Continue}}.
3. Landela ibha yesimo, ekhomba indlela futhi ichaze okudingekayo okulandelayo: chofoza iphoyinti lokuqala, isikhungo, iphoyinti lesibili emnsalweni noma iphoyinti lokuphela.
4. Lapho umyalo uthi "or type", thayipha inombolo bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: ikona ephelele ngamadigri (inombolo engalungile = ngokuphambene nowashi), ubude be-chord, isiqondiso sokuthinta sokuqala ngamadigri, noma irediyasi (inombolo engalungile = indlela ende).
5. Umnsalo udwetshiwe. {{ui:Arc: Continue}} iqala umnsalo olandelayo ekugcineni komugqa wokugcina noma umnsalo.

## Izinqaku

- Imimnsalo igcina isikhungo sayo, ukuqala nokuphela, ngakho izixhumanisi nezilinganiso zisebenza kuyo njengakuqala.
- Ibha yesimo yenqaba okufakwayo okungenzeki futhi ithi kungani, isibonelo amaphoyinti amathathu emugqeni owodwa, noma irediyasi encane kakhulu ukuthi ifike phakathi kwamaphoyinti amabili okuphela.
- {{ui:Arc: Continue}} lapho kungadwetshiwe lutho ithi "Akukho okuqhubekayo kuso: dweba umugqa noma umnsalo kuqala."
