I-PartCraft yenziwe ngemouse enevili nenkinobho ephakathi, kodwa i-trackpad isebenza kahle uma usazi izinkomba, nesikrini sokuthinta siyasebenza ukubuka izingxenye nokudweba okulula kweskethi.

## Trackpad

:::mac
| Ukuthi | Yenza lokhu |
|---|---|
| Jikeleza ingxenye (3D) | Chofoza uhudule ngomunwe owodwa (noma hudula ngeminwe emithathu, uma uvule ukuhudula ngeminwe emithathu) |
| Shelelisa (3D) | Bamba {{key:Shift}} (noma ⌘) bese uchofoza uhudule, noma chofoza uhudule ngeminwe emibili icindezelwe (ukuhudula kwe-secondary-click) |
| Jikeleza ingxenye, uswayiphe | Swayipha ngeminwe emibili; nge-{{key:Shift}}, ukuswayipha kushelelisa. Kudinga **Scrolling** esethwe ku "Work it out" (okokuqala) noma "Trackpad" |
| Sondeza (3D nokudweba) | Pinch. Ukusondeza kuya ngasesikhombisini. Ngo-**Scrolling** esethwe ku "Mouse wheel", ukuskrola ngeminwe emibili nakho kuyasondeza |
| Chofoza ngesokudla | Chofoza ngeminwe emibili, noma {{key:Ctrl}}+chofoza |
| Shelelisa eskethini | Swayipha ngeminwe emibili ("Work it out" noma "Trackpad"), noma chofoza uhudule ngeminwe emibili icindezelwe. {{key:Shift}}+hudula akushelelisi eskethini (kudweba iwindi lokukhetha) |
| Engeza ekukhetheni eskethini | {{key:Shift}}+chofoza noma ⌘+chofoza |

Vula secondary click ku-Izilungiselelo zesistimu, Trackpad, Point & Click uma ukuchofoza ngeminwe emibili kungenzi lutho.
:::

:::windows linux
| Ukuthi | Yenza lokhu |
|---|---|
| Jikeleza ingxenye (3D) | Cindezela i-touchpad (noma thinta ubambe) bese uhudula |
| Shelelisa (3D) | Bamba {{key:Shift}} noma {{key:Ctrl}} bese uhudula, noma hudula ngesifunda senkinobho yesokudla ecindezelwe |
| Jikeleza ingxenye, uswayiphe | Swayipha ngeminwe emibili; nge-{{key:Shift}}, ukuswayipha kushelelisa. Kudinga **Scrolling** esethwe ku "Work it out" (okokuqala) noma "Trackpad" |
| Sondeza (3D nokudweba) | Pinch lapho i-touchpad isekela khona. Ukusondeza kuya ngasesikhombisini. Ngo-**Scrolling** esethwe ku "Mouse wheel", ukuskrola ngeminwe emibili kuyasondeza |
| Chofoza ngesokudla | Thinta ngeminwe emibili, noma ikhona ekhoneni eliphansi kwesokudla letouchpad |
| Shelelisa eskethini | Swayipha ngeminwe emibili ("Work it out" noma "Trackpad"), noma hudula ngesifunda senkinobho yesokudla ecindezelwe. {{key:Shift}}+hudula akushelelisi eskethini |
| Engeza ekukhetheni eskethini | {{key:Shift}}+chofoza noma {{key:Ctrl}}+chofoza |
:::

**Scrolling** itholakala ku-[Izilungiselelo ze-PartCraft](topic:settings): "Work it out" (okokuqala) ithatha ukuskrola okungaphansi noma okusanhlamvu njengetrackpad, "Trackpad" ihlala yenza njalo, "Mouse wheel" ayikaze yenze njalo. Pinch iyasondeza kuzo zombili izimo.

Izinkinobho zomcibisholo zijikeleza ibha ye-3D, futhi {{key:Shift}} nezinkinobho zomcibisholo ushelelisa, uma usuchofozile embonweni: kuyasiza ku-trackpad. {{key:Plus}} ne-{{key:Minus}} zisondeza.

## Izikrini zokuthinta

I-PartCraft ifunda ukuthinta, ngakho amathebulethi, amalaptop athintwayo nama-foni ayasebenza, nemikhawulo.

:::touch
**Okhiye bokuthinta**

Uma usuthinte isikrini, kuvela ibha encane ngakwesokudla esingezansi kombono nokhiye abasweli umunwe. Iyanyamalala uma usebenzisa imouse.

- {{ui:Esc}}: ukhiye we-Esc. Uyakhansela okuqalile noma ubuyele emuva, njengalokho okwenza ukhiye.
- {{ui:Engeza}} (eskethini, nge-Select): uma ivuliwe, ngayinye ukuthinta kwengeza into ekukhetheni noma kuyisuse, njenge-{{key:Shift}}+chofoza. Yithinte futhi ukuze ubuyele ekukhetheni into eyodwa ngesikhathi.
- {{ui:Kuqediwe}} (uma udweba uchungechunge lomugqa, i-spline noma i-polygon): iyiqedele, njengokuchofoza ngesokudla.

Ukubamba umunwe ungashintshi cishe ingxenyana yesibili kwenza okufanayo ngokuthinta okukodwa: ngethuluzi lokudweba iqedela okudwetshwayo, nge-Select ingeza into engaphansi komunwe ekukhetheni noma iyisuse. Amaphoyinti nemigqa kulula ukuwathinta ngomunwe: i-PartCraft ibheka cishe kabili ukuthinta ngokuzungeza kunokuchofoza.

**Embonweni we-3D**

- Umunwe owodwa: jikeleza ingxenye.
- Iminwe emibili: pinch ukusondeza, hambisa ndawonye ukushelelisa.
- Thinta: khetha ubuso, umphetho noma indiza. Akukho ukukhanyisa kwe-hover ngaphambi kokuthinta.

**Eskethini**

- Thinta ngethuluzi lokudweba ukubeka iphoyinti ngalinye: thinta ukuqala, bese uthinta ukuphela. Ukuhlanganisa kwenzeka lapho ukhipha umunwe.
- Iminwe emibili pinch futhi ishelelise. Ukubeka umunwe wesibili kuyayeka ukudonsa okuqale ngomunwe wokuqala.
- Ukuqeda uchungechunge lomugqa, thinta {{ui:Kuqediwe}}, bamba umunwe ungashintshi, noma thinta {{ui:Umugqa}} futhi (noma elinye ithuluzi) ku-toolbar.
- Nge-Select, thinta ukukhetha; hudula iphoyinti noma umugqa ukuwahambisa; hudula endaweni engenalutho ukuze uthole iwindi. Ukuthinta kwesibili kushintsha ukukhetha, ngaphandle uma {{ui:Engeza}} ivuliwe (noma ubambe umunwe ungashintshi kwenye into yesibili).
- Ukushintsha isilinganiso, sebenzisa **Edit** eduze kwaso ephaneli yesokudla.

**Ishidi lombumbo**

Thinta ukubeka nokukhetha izimpawu, bese uhudula impawu ukuyinyakazisa. Pinch ukusondeza, hambisa iminwe emibili ukushelelisa, noma hudula umunwe owodwa phezu kwephepha elingenalutho. Umunwe wesibili uyayima impawu oyihudulayo, futhi ibuyele emuva.

**Okudinga ikhibhodi**

- Ukubhala izinombolo: ikhibhodi yesikrini iyavela emabhokisini wezinombolo nasezindaweni zezilinganiso.
- Izinkinobho zamathuluzi: sebenzisa i-toolbar.
:::

## Izikrini ezincane namafoni

- Ku-1280 pixels noma ngaphezulu, i-toolbar ilingana emugqeni owodwa.
- Phakathi kuka-760 no-1100 pixels, amaphaneli ohlangothini aba mancane.
- Ngaphansi kuka-760 pixels (ifoni, noma iwindi elincane), isikrini siyagxilwa: i-toolbar (swayipha ngokuhlangene ukuthola izinkinobho eziningi), umbono we-3D, ibha yesimo, bese amaphaneli wezakhiwo nomlando. Skrola ikhasi ukuze ufinyelele amaphaneli.
- Imenyu ({{ui:Thumela}}, {{ui:Imibono}}) ivula kuwo wonke ububanzi besikrini.

:::note
Ku-iPhone akukho imodi yesikrini esigcwele ku-browser; {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} nenkinobho yemodi yesikrini zishintsha kuphela phakathi kwejwayelekile nokufihla amabha.
:::

Ifoni iyanele ukuvula ingxenye, ukuyijikeleza nokuhlola izinto zayo. Ukumodela okwangempela kudinga isikrini esikhulu nemouse noma trackpad.
