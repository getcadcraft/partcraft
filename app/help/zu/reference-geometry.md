Ijometri yesinkomba ingumsebenzi wokwakha izici ozakhela phezu kwawo: **ipuleni** lokuhlela phezu kwalo lapho kungekho uhlangothi, **i-axis** yokujikeleza noma yokuphindaphinda ngayo, **iphoyinti** lokumisa i-extrude. Ayinampahla yayo. Amapuleni abonakala embonweni njengezikwele ezimhlophe ezikhanyayo, ama-axis njengemigqa enamachashaza, namaphoyinti njengamachashaza amancane; kuzo zonke lezi zintathu kuyajoyina umlando futhi kulandela okwenziwe ngakho uma usayizi wangaphambili eshintsha.

Zisendaweni ye-menu ye-**Okwengeziwe** yeqembu lezici: {{ui:Indiza}}, {{ui:I-axis}}, {{ui:Iphoyinti}}.

## Yenza ipuleni

1. Chofoza uhlangothi olusalele, noma ipuleni lesisekelo emlandweni, ukuze uqale kulo (uyakhetha).
2. Khetha {{ui:Okwengeziwe}}, bese {{ui:Indiza}}. Iqala njengepuleni eliphambanisiwe ngamaphesenti angu-20 osayizi wengxenye kusuka kokukhethile (noma kusuka kwipuleni eliphezulu).
3. Ephaneli yesokudla khetha **Uhlobo** nezinkomba zalo:

| Uhlobo | Izinkomba | Izinombolo |
|---|---|---|
| Ukuphambanisa kusuka epuleni noma kuhlangothi | **Kusuka kwipuleni** | **Ibanga (mm)**, elingelinani uma kusohlangothini olunye |
| Ngengeli, idlula komphetho noma kwi-axis | **Kusuka kwipuleni**, **Jikeleza ngokuzungeza** (i-axis yomhlaba, isici se-axis, noma umphetho oqondile okhethiwe) | **I-engeli (°)** |
| Idlula kumaphoyinti amathathu | **Iphoyinti 1**, **Iphoyinti 2**, **Iphoyinti 3** | — |
| Ipuleni eliphakathi phakathi kwamabili | **Ipuleni lokuqala**, **Ipuleni lesibili** (uma zilinganayo: phakathi kwazo; uma zihlangana: ipuleni elihlukanisa ingeli ngamabili) | — |
| Ithinta isilinda | **Isilinda** (uhlangothi lwesilinda), **Iphoyinti eliseduze** (uhlangothi oluphi) | — |
| Ilingana komphetho, endaweni yephoyinti | **Umphetho**, **Endaweni yephoyinti** (ipuleni lidlula kuphoyinti lomphetho eliseduze nalo, nakuphoyinti nje) | — |
| Idlula komphetho nephoyinti | **Umphetho oqondile** (noma i-axis), **Idlula kuphoyinti** | — |
| Ilingana komphetho esiphakathini | **Umphetho** (ngaphakathi ubude baw, ulingana nawo lapho), noma uma ungekho umphetho **Iphoyinti 1** kanye **Iphoyinti 2** (ipuleni phakathi kwawo) | — |

Yonke inkomba inenkinobho ye-**Khetha**: yicindezele, bese uchofoza embonweni. Isiqeshana sepuleni samukela ipuleni lesisekelo, ipuleni lesinkomba noma uhlangothi oluqinile; isiqeshana samaphoyinti samukela ukuphela komphetho (chofoza eduze nalowo mkhawulo), isici sephoyinti, noma indawo ohlangothini; isiqeshana somphetho samukela umphetho. {{key:Esc}} ikhansela ukukhetha. **Guqula i-normal** iphendula ipuleni (ukuthi isikethi esihlezi phezu kwalo sibheke ngaphi).

Kule nguqulo, isiqeshana esidinga umphetho noma uhlangothi lwesilinda (Ithinta isilinda, nezi-Edge) siphendula ukuthi "akakakhiwa okwamanje": sebenzisa amaphoyinti noma i-axis kunalokho.

## Hlela phezu kwepuleni

Khetha ipuleni (emlandweni noma ngokuchofoza isikwele saso embonweni) bese ukhetha {{ui:Isikethi}}, noma **Hlela phezu kwalo** ephaneli yalo. Isikethi silandela ipuleni: uma ushintsha ibanga lepuleni, nesikethi, nazo zonke izici ezisesikethini, ziyanyakaziswa.

## Ama-axis namaphoyinti

- {{ui:I-axis}}: idlula kumaphoyinti amabili, i-axis yesilinda noma ye-cone, lapho amapuleni amabili ehlangana, ngomphetho oqondile (noma i-axis yomphetho wesekhanda), noma ilingana nohlangothi ngephoyinti.
- {{ui:Iphoyinti}}: ngokuhlangabezana, endaweni yekhona noma sikhethe kwesikethi, noma ngaphakathi komzimba womphetho wesekhanda, plus amaphoyinti "phakathi kwezinto":

| Uhlobo lwephoyinti | Izinkomba |
|---|---|
| Phakathi komphetho (noma phakathi kwamaphoyinti amabili) | **Umphetho** (ngaphakathi ubude baw, nakuma-arc namajika), noma uma ungekho umphetho **Iphoyinti 1** kanye **Iphoyinti 2** |
| Lapho umphetho noma i-axis ihlangana nohlangothi noma nepuleni | **Umphetho** (oqondile noma ogobile) noma i-axis, kanye **Ihlangana nepuleni** (ipuleni noma uhlangothi oluqinile) noma **Noma uhlangothi** (uhlangothi oluqinile noma isilinda). Isiyingi sihlangana nepuleni kabili, nomugqa udlula kwisilinda kabili: **Okunye ukuhlangana** kuthatha okwesibili |
| Lapho amapuleni amathathu ehlangana | **Ipuleni lokuqala**, **Ipuleni lesibili** kanye **Ipuleni lesithathu** (amapuleni noma uhlangothi oluqinile) |

Ngamunye wawo uhluleka ngomlayezo olula uma izinkomba zawo zingalingani: umugqa ophambene nepuleni, umugqa ongakhahlanga isilinda, umphetho ongafiki epuleni, amapuleni amathathu angahlanganisi endaweni eyodwa, noma iphoyinti emgqeni okufanele ulungise ipuleni kuwo.

Zisebenzise kwezinye izici:

| Lapho | Indlela |
|---|---|
| Revolve | **I-axis**: khetha isici se-axis kuhlu (kumele silale kwipuleni lesikethi) |
| Iphethini ejikelezayo | **I-axis**: isici se-axis |
| Iphethini eqondile | **Indlela**: isici se-axis, noma ipuleni (i-normal yalo) |
| Mirror | **Ipuleni le-mirror**: ipuleni lesinkomba |
| Extrude | **Ukuphela**: Kuze kufike euhlangothini noma epuleni, Kuze kufike ku-vertex (isici sephoyinti). Akakakhiwa kule nguqulo (idinga i-kernel eqinile) |
| Draft | **Ipuleni elingathathi hlangothi** |

## Uma inkomba isinyamalele

Ipuleni elenziwe kusuka ohlangothini olungasekho futhi lehluleka ngomlayezo obiza lona, njengo "Uhlangothi lapho lihlezi kulo selusuka ku-Extrude1." Alisoze lisuswe lingene kolunye uhlangothi. Izikethi nezici ezisephezu kwalo zimakwa "zingakakhiwa" kuze ukhethe inkomba entsha; ingxenye eseleyo ihlala njengoba yayinjalo.

:::note
Le nguqulo yakha yonke ingxenye nge-mesh kernel, edala amapuleni, ama-axis namaphoyinti kusuka kumapuleni wesisekelo, ohlangothini oluqinile olwenziwe nge-Extrude noma i-Revolve, amaphoyinti esikethi, izixhumanisi nezinye izici zesinkomba. Noma yini edinga umphetho, isiphetho somphetho noma uhlangothi oluthambile idinga i-kernel eqinile: akakakhiwa kule nguqulo ("Isebenzisa uhlangothi noma umphetho wesolidi, okungatholakala yi-kernel eqinile kuphela: akakakhiwa kule nguqulo.").
:::
