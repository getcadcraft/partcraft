**Akukakwakhiwa kule nguqulo.** Ifini idinga i-kernel yejiyomethri echanekile, engekho kule nguqulo ye-PartCraft: yakha zonke izingxenye nge-mesh kernel. [Okungakakwakhiwa](topic:not-built-yet) kuchaza okufanele wenze esikhundleni salokho. Ingxenye eseleyo yalelikhasi ichaza kanjalo lapho isebenza nge-kernel echanekile.
:::

I-**fini** (noma i-gusset) iwudonga olucekeceke oqinisa ingxenye, njengonxantathu ngaphakathi kwekhona yebhulaketi ye-L. Udweba umphetho wayo kuphela: i-PartCraft igcwalisa isikhala phakathi kwalowo mugqa nezindonga. Idinga **i-kernel echanekile** (bheka [I-mesh kernel](topic:solid-kernels)).

## Engeza ifini

1. Yenza indiza phakathi nendawo lapho ifini izofika khona (ibhulaketi ye-L ejulile engu-40, indiza engu-20 phambi kwesikethi sayo: bheka [Izindiza zesisekelo, ama-axis namaphoyinti](topic:reference-geometry)).
2. Dweba endizeni **umugqa owodwa** usuka odongeni uye kodonga — noma imigqa embalwa exhunyiwe ukuze ibe umphetho ogobile wefini. Umugqa ungamisa ngaphambi kwezindonga: amaphethelo awo aqhubeka kuze kuhlangane nazo.
3. Khetha isikethi bese ukhetha {{ui:Okwengeziwe}} › {{ui:Ifini}}.

Ifini igcwaliswa kusuka emugqeni iye ezindongeni ngakolunye uhlangothi oluya emaphakathini wengxenye, ngakho i-gusset ihlala ekhoneni langaphakathi. Uma lolo hlangothi livulekile kanti olunye livaliwe, i-PartCraft iyiphendulela wena futhi iyatshela.

## Izilungiselelo

- **Ubukhulu (mm)** buwubukhulu befini ngaphesheya kwendiza yesikethi. Bungalawulwa yiparamitha.
- **Ubukhulu** (ukukhetha okungaphansi kwalo): **Kumaphakathi endizeni yesikethi**, **Phambi kwendiza** (ngokuhamba i-normal yayo) noma **Ngemuva kwendiza**.
- **Phendula** igcwalisa olunye uhlangothi lomugqa.

## Imilayezo

- **Ngalolu hlangothi umphetho wefini awuhlangani nomzimba** — akukho okuvala isikhala kulolo hlangothi: khetha noma susa **Phendula**, noma hambisa umugqa ukuze izindonga ziwuvale.
- **Ifini idinga ukhetho lomugqa oluvulekile oyodwa esikethini** — isikethi sinesimo esivaliwe, i-arc noma i-spline, noma imigqa engahlanganisi ngokuphela kuya kokuphela.
- **Umphetho wefini uhamba ngaphakathi komzimba lapho** — umugqa ulele ngaphakathi kwe-solid; wudwebe esikhaleni esivulekile phakathi kwezindonga.
