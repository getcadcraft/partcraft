**Illustrate** yindawo yokusebenza yamadokhumenti adingwa ngaphandle kwedizayini: imibono ehlukaniswe namabhaluni nohlu lwezingxenye, imidwebo yomugqa yezincwadi, imiyalelo ngesinyathelo ngesinyathelo, izinkinobho zokunyakaza nefayela le-3D umthengi angalivula ngaphandle kwe-CAD. Khetha {{ui:Illustrate}} kwisishintshi se-{{ui:Indawo yokusebenza}} ekuqaleni kwethuluzi.

**Incwadi** iyifayela lalo siqu (`<name>.partcraft-manual.json`). Igcina ikhophi yesimo sengxenye ngayinye, isihlahla somkhiqizo nezimfanelo zezingxenye, nexhumanisa kulapho zavela khona, ukuze usebenze ku-incwadi kanti idizayini iqhubeka. Uma idizayini ishintsha, [Buyekeza kusuka kwidizayini](topic:illustrate-update) iletha izinguquko.

## Qala enye

Chofoza {{ui:Okusha}} eqenjini {{ui:Umhlahlandlela}}, bese:

- {{ui:Kusuka engxenyeni evulekile}}: ingxenye evulekile ku-Design.
- {{ui:Kusuka kwinhlanganisela evulekile}}: iboniswa esikhundleni salo uma kuvulekile inhlanganisela; izingxenye zayo, izindawo zazo, izinombolo zengxenye, izinto nezinkinobho zokunyakaza kuyeza nazo.
- {{ui:Kusuka kwifayela lenhlanganisela…}}: ifayela le-`.partcraft-asm.json` elilondoloziwe; i-PartCraft ibumba kuqala izingxenye zalo.
- {{ui:Kusuka kwenye ifayela…}}: i-glTF (.glb, .gltf), OBJ, STL noma 3MF. Isihlahla nemigqa yegama lefayela siyagcinwa, futhi amakhophi engxenye eyodwa (isimo esifanayo esibekwe kabili) aba yingxenye eyodwa enani. Amafayela e-STL nama-OBJ awatshengisi iyunithi yawo, ngakho uyabuzwa. Amafayela e-STL afundwa ngo-Z phezulu; i-glTF ne-3MF zitshela eyazo.

Lapho incwadi ivuliwe, i-Illustrate ibonisa ikhophi yayo yezingxenye; ingxenye noma inhlanganisela evulekile ku-Design iyafihlwa kuze kubuyele ku-Design.

## Londoloza nokuvula

{{ui:Londoloza}} eqenjini {{ui:Umhlahlandlela}} (noma {{key:Mod+S}}) ilondoloza incwadi; {{ui:Vula}} iyivula. Konke okushintshwa ku-Illustrate kuyisinyathelo esisodwa emlandweni wencwadi yayo: {{ui:Hlehlisa}} no {{ui:Phinda wenze}} asebenza ku-incwadi uma i-Illustrate ivuliwe.

## Akakakhiwa okwamanje

- Amafayela e-STEP awavuleki ku-{{ui:Kusuka kwenye ifayela…}} okwamanje. Vula i-STEP ku-Design esikhundleni salokho (ifika njengomzimba oqinile: bona [Vula ifayela le-STEP nakha kulo](topic:import-step)) bese uqala incwadi ngokuthi {{ui:Kusuka engxenyeni evulekile}}, noma uyilondoloze njenge-glTF, 3MF, OBJ noma STL.
- I-ModelCraft ne-BimCraft azikwazi okwamanje ukudlulisa imodeli ngqo ku-Illustrate: yilondoloze njenge-glTF (.glb) bese usebenzisa {{ui:Kusuka kwenye ifayela…}}.
