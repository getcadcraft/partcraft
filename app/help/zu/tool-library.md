Ithebhu ethi **Amathuluzi** ye-Manufacture ilanda amathuluzi engxenye. Ngaphansi kwe-**Umtapo wamathuluzi** ungagcina amathuluzi akho ngaphezu kwengxenye eyodwa:

- {{ui:Londoloza leli thuluzi}} yengeza ithuluzi elikhethiwe ku-**Amathuluzi ami alondoloziwe**; {{ui:Londoloza wonke amathuluzi alengxenye}} yengeza wonke. Amathuluzi ami alondoloziwe agcinwa kulesi siphequluli futhi atholakala kuyo yonke ingxenye. Ithuluzi osele uliloondoloze (uhlobo, usayizi, izigxobo nokuguqula okuthayiphile) alikaphindwa kabili.
- {{ui:Yengeza kule ngxenye}} ufaka ithuluzi elilondoloziwe elikhethiwe engxenyeni. Uma inombolo yalo ye-T isuvele isetshenziswa ithuluzi elinye engxenyeni, luthola inombolo elandelayo etholakalayo, futhi iphaneli iyakusho lokho. {{ui:Susa emtapweni wami}} iyasisusa ohlwini oluloondoloziwe (hhayi ezinxenyeni ezisevele zinalo).
- {{ui:Thumela amathuluzi…}} ibhala amathuluzi engxenye njengefayela le-`.json`; {{ui:Ngenisa amathuluzi…}} yengeza amathuluzi alelo fayela engxenyeni, ngemithetho efanayo (ithuluzi elisevele likhona liyashiywa, inombolo ye-T esetshenzisiwe iyashintsha). Ifayela libamba usayizi, izigxobo, izinto, nokuguqula kwesivinini nokudla okuthayiphile; alinazo izitembu (ids). Lisebenziseka ukudlulisa amathuluzi esitolo kukhompyutha esilandelayo, noma ukuwanikeza umngane wakho.

Uma isiphequluli singagcini umtapo (isitoreji siyavalwa noma sigcwele), ukulondoloza kuyakusho lokho, futhi **Thumela amathuluzi** iyindlela yokugcina ifayela. Ifayela kumele libe umtapo wamathuluzi we-PartCraft walesi sivivinyo noma owendala, unamathuluzi angaphansi kuka-500 anosayizi ovumelekile; okunye konke kuyenqatshwa ngesizathu.

Izinga lokusika nama-chip load ahambisana nethuluzi ngalinye ngamanani okuqala; bona [Yenza ama-toolpath kanye ne-G-code](topic:manufacture).
