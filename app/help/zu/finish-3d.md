Imisebenzi emibili kwi-{{ui:Manufacture}} isika umphezulu wemodeli yonke ngethuluzi elibekwe lime (ama-eksisi amathathu). Azidingi ukukhetha: zisebenza kukho konke okwemodeli okubonakala sekusuka phezulu. Qala ngokuhlanza ingxenye kuqala (adaptive noma pocket); ukudlula kokuqeda phezu kwesitoko esingakasikwa kuxwayisa ukuthi kuzosika kujule.

## Nxande

Imigqa eqondile ikhathazeke emodelini ngo-**engeli** osethayo, ngo-**stepover** ngayinye (ingxenyana yobubanzi bethuluzi). Umugqa ngamunye ulandela umphezulu: ingcino yethuluzi iphakama kangangoba kufanele ukuze akukho ngxenye yesikhangiswa esehla ngaphansi kwemodeli, futhi umugqa usikwa kahle kakhulu lapho umphezulu ugoba khona. Uma i-**Zig-zag** ivuliwe, ithuluzi lihlala lingene phakathi kwemigqa lapho lingakwenza ngokuphepha (lihlolwa ngakho konke ukuhamba); ngaphandle kwalokho liyaphakama.

- **Ukubekezelela**: ukuthi ingxenye eqondile yomugqa ingaba kude kangakanani nokuphakama kwecino langempela (uhhafu walo usetshenziswa njengomkhawulo maphakathi nengxenye ngayinye). Okuzenzakalelayo ngu-0.01 mm.
- **Kuphela lapho umphezulu usuka ku-… kuya ku-… (°)**: qeda izindawo ezincane kuphela noma ezinamathambeka kuphela. Lapho umphezulu ungaphandle kwalobo bubanzi, umugqa uyashiywa futhi ithuluzi liyaphakama.
- **Isitoko esizoshiya**: imodeli ehlanganiswe ngalobo bubanzi (ithuluzi lithanda ukukhula ngalo: liyalungela amabhola nama-bull-nose; ithuluzi elingalingene lishiya okungenani lowo mthamo).
- **Ezansi**: akukho okusikwa ngaphansi kwalolu z.

## Scallop

Amapasi agcina ibanga elingashintshi phezu komphezulu kusuka komunye, ukuze igquma (i-scallop) eshiywe phakathi kwamapasi amabili amakhelwane libe **ukuphakama** oyicelayo. Ibanga lilandela ukuphakama kanye nomsakazo webhola: s = 2·√(2·R·h − h²), isibonelo cishe 0.69 mm phakathi kwamapasi ngebhola Ø6 negquma elingu-0.02 mm. I-bull-nose isebenzisa umsakazo wekhoni layo. Isiphetho esiyisicaba siyenqatshelwa: asinagquma lokulinganisa.

Amapasi aqala **emphethelweni wemodeli, ngena ngaphakathi** noma **ngaphezulu kwemodeli, ehla phansi**. Ayizinxanxathela zebanga phezu komphezulu (hhayi zokuphakama), ngakho ethambekile aseduze ngepulani, nasesihlahleni abanda. Ibanga linqunywa ku-grid phezu komphezulu futhi linemiphumela ecishe ibe ngu-1 %; ukuphakama kwegquma kuqondile lapho umphezulu uyisicaba noma ugoba kancane, kuseduze lapho ugoba ngokushesha. **Climb** igcina uhlangothi oluphezulu lomphezulu ngakwesokudla sethuluzi.

## Ukuthi izindlela zinhle kangakanani

Okuhlolwayo yilokho okuhlolwa ngezibalo ezivaliwe noma ngokusesha okuzimele kwesikhala sethuluzi:

- Ukuphakama kwecino ngaphansi kwethuluzi kuqondile kwebhola, kwe-bull-nose nakwesiyisicaba ezindaweni, emaphethelweni nasemakhoneni e-mesh, futhi akusoze kube phezulu kunokuphakama kwangempela; kwemigqa enxande indlela ihlala ngaphakathi kokubekezelela kwayo, ingekho ukugawuka okujule kunalokho.
- Ubude bezindlela ebhodweni elithambekile buvumelana nesibalo esivaliwe; amapasi e-scallop esicaba ahlukaniswe ngebanga elicelwe, negquma phakathi kwawo liphuma ngokuphakama okucelwe.
- Ukulingiswa kwesitoko akubonisi ukugawuka ngemva kwedlulo futhi kubonisa amagquma asele.

Lezi zindlela akuzona: ziyizindlela zokuqeda ezilula, hhayi konke okunikezwa uhlelo lokukhiqiza.

- Akukakhiwa: ukuqeda ngomugqa wokuphakama okungashintshi (constant z), ukuhlanza ngepensela nasemakhoneni, amapasi aphuma emagqumeni, ukuprojekta ivini phezu komphezulu, ukuthola izindawo ezicishe zibe nesicaba, nokuhlola ukushaywa kwesibambi nesiqu. Ukuhlanza nokusala okusele kuyimisebenzi yabo [Ukuhlanza nge-3D nokusala okusele](topic:rough-3d); ithuluzi elithambekayo lingu-[Ukuqedwa kwe-5-eksisi](topic:multi-axis#5-axis-finishing).
- Isikhungo sethuluzi sihlala phezu kwemodeli: izindlela ziyama emaphethelweni emodeli esikhundleni sokuqhubeka zingaphandle kwayo, ngakho udonga oluma ngqo ngasemphethelweni alugcini kuze kufike ezansi kwalo.
- Ukugoba okungaphansi akubonwa (umphezulu uwukuthi ithuluzi lihlangana nawo lisuka phezulu).
- Imodeli iyi-mesh evela ku-kernel, ngakho izindlela zilandela i-mesh (ukubekezelela kwayo kwe-chord cishe 0.0002 wedayagonali yengxenye), hhayi umphezulu oqondile.
- Ukuqeda imodeli enkulu ngokubekezelela okuncane kwenza uhlelo olude olunemigqa emifushane eminingi. Hlola okuphumayo kusimulator somshini wakho ngaphambi kokusika.
