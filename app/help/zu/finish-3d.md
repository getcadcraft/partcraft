Imisebenzi emibili ku-Manufacture isika **ubuso bemodeli yonke** ngethuluzi elibekwe lima ngqo (amaeksisi amathathu). Abakhethi lutho: basebenza kuyo yonke imodeli ebonakala kusuka phezulu. Qala ngokugaya ingxenye kuqala (i-adaptive noma i-pocket); ukudlula kokuqeda phezu kwempahla engasikiwe kuxwayisa ukuthi kungasika ngokujulile.

## Okuhambisanayo

Imigqa eqondile phezu komodeli ku-**i-engeli** oyisethayo, **i-stepover** ehlukaniswe (ingxenye yobubanzi bethuluzi). Umugqa ngamunye ulandela ubuso: isiphetho sethuluzi siphakama njengoba kumele ukuze akukho ingxenye yesisiki engena ngaphansi kwemodeli, futhi umugqa usikwa ncane lapho ubuso bugoba khona. Uma i-**Zig-zag** ivuliwe, ithuluzi lihlala lisezansi phakathi kwemigqa lapho lingenza kahle ngokuphephile (kuhlolwa ngomugqa wokuhamba); kungenjalo liyaphakama.

- **Ukubekezelela**: umugqa oqondile ungaba kangakanani kude nokuphakama kweqiniso kwesiphetho (uhhafu wakho usetshenziswa njengomkhawulo ephakathi kwesiqephu ngasinye). Okuzenzakalelayo 0.01 mm.
- **Kuphela lapho ubuso bungaphansi kwe-… futhi bungaphezulu kwe-… (°)**: qeda izindawo ezinkulu kuphela noma ezinamathambeka kuphela. Lapho ubuso bungaphandle kohlu, umugqa uyasuswa futhi ithuluzi liyaphakama.
- **Impahla yokushiya**: imodeli ehlanganiswe ngalobo bude (isisiki sikhuliswa ngakho: siqondile kumathuluzi esisiki nebull-nose; ithuluzi eliphansi lishiya okungenani lokho).
- **Ingaphansi**: akukho okusikiwe ngaphansi kwalolu z.

## Scallop

Izindlela ezigcina ibanga elifanayo phezu kobuso kusukela kwelinye, ngakho umsila (i-scallop) osele phakathi kwezindlela ezimbili eziseduze unokuphakama **okucelwayo**. Ibanga lilandela ukuphakama nomsakazo webhola: s = 2·√(2·R·h − h²), isibonelo cishe 0.69 mm phakathi kwezindlela zebhola engu-Ø6 nomsila ongu-0.02 mm. Ithuluzi le-bull-nose lisebenzisa umsakazo wekhona layo. I-flat end mill iyenqatshwa: ayinawo umsila wokulinganisa.

Izindlela ziqala **kumphetho wemodeli, zingena ngaphakathi** noma **kusukela phezulu kwayo, zehla**. Yizinkomba zebanga phezu kobuso (hhayi zokuphakama), ngakho ethambekeni ziseduze endizeni futhi emsedlalweni zibe kude kakhulu. Ibanga lilinganiswa kugridi phezu kobuso futhi lilungile cishe ngo-1 %; ukuphakama komsila kuqondile lapho ubuso bungaqondile noma bugoba kancane, bucishe lapho bugoba ngokushesha. **Climb** igcina uhlangothi oluphezulu lobuso ngakwesokudla sesisiki.

## Ukuthi izindlela zinhle kangakanani

Okuhlolwa yimivivinyo, ngamafomula avaliwe noma ngokusesha okungaxhomekeki kwendawo yesisiki:

- Ukuphakama kwesiphetho ngaphansi kwethuluzi kuqondile kuthuluzi lebhola, le-bull-nose nelikaphansi ezindaweni, emaphethelweni nasemakhoneni emesh, futhi akusoze kube phezulu kokuphakama kweqiniso; kwemigqa ehambisanayo indlela ihlala ngaphakathi kokubekezelela kwayo, ingekho ukugaywa okudlula lokho.
- Ubude bezindlela endizeni ethambekile buvumelana nefomula evaliwe; izindlela ze-scallop endizeni ziba ibanga elicelwe phakathi kwazo, nomsila phakathi kwazo uphuma ngokuphakama okucelwe.
- Ukulingisa kwempahla akubonisi ukugaywa ngemva kwedlule futhi kubonisa imisila esele.

Lezi zindlela akuzona: zizindlela zokuqeda eziyisisekelo, hhayi konke okunikezwa uhlelo lokukhiqiza.

- Akukakhiwa: i-waterline (z eqinile), ukuhlanza kwe-pencil nasemakhoneni, izindlela ezijikelezayo nezisonge, ukuphrojekta ijika phezu kobuso, ukutholwa kwezindawo ezilinganayo, ukugaya kokuphumula, ukuhlolwa kokushaywa kwebhulaki nomshank, nokuthambeka kwanoma yiliphi ithuluzi.
- Isisiki sethuluzi sihlala phezu kwemodeli: izindlela zimisa emaphethelweni emodeli kunokuba zidlule kuwo, ngakho udonga oluqondile eduze nomphetho aluqedwa kuze kube sesiseyisiseko salo.
- Ama-undercut awabonakali (ubuso yilokho ithuluzi elihlangana nalo kusuka phezulu).
- Imodeli iyimesh evela ku-kernel, ngakho izindlela zilandela imesh (ukubekezelela kwayo kwe-chord cishe 0.0002 yesiqondiso sengxenye), hhayi ubuso oqondile.
- Ukuqeda imodeli enkulu ngokubekezelela okuhle kwenza uhlelo olude olunezinhlaka eziningi ezimfushane. Hlola okukhiphayo kusimuleyitha somshini wakho ngaphambi kokusika.
