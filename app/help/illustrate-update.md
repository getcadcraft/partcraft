title: Update a manual from the design
summary: When the design changes, Update brings in the new shapes and places and keeps views, annotations, balloon numbers and animation keys. You see what moved, what is new and what was removed before anything changes; it is one undo step.
section: tasks
order: 168
keywords: illustrate, update, design change, revision, sync, changed assembly, report
---

Click {{ui:Update from the design…}} under the manual's name, then choose the open part or assembly, or a file (the same kind the manual was made from).

A report lists what will change:

- **Moved** parts, and how far.
- **Changed shape**.
- **New** parts: shown in every view, with no balloon number yet.
- **Removed** parts, with the annotations and animation keys that pointed at them: they go too, and are named here so nothing is dropped silently.
- **Renamed** parts.

Click {{ui:Update}} to apply it. Parts are matched by their own id in the design, so views, annotations, balloon numbers and keys stay with them. Update is one step: {{ui:Undo}} puts the manual back as it was.
