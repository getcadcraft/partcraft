Open the {{ui:Animation}} section:

- {{ui:Key everything}}, {{ui:Key the selected parts}} and {{ui:Key the camera}} store how things are now at the current time: each part's place, whether it is shown, its opacity and colour, and the camera. Change the scene, move the {{ui:Time}} slider, key again: the animation goes from one key to the next.
- {{ui:Record the exploded view}} adds one key per step of the scene's exploded view, a second a step, after what is there.
- {{ui:Views as camera stops}} plays the views in order: the camera moves to each, holds it, and the parts take each view's looks.

{{ui:Play}}, {{ui:Pause}}, {{ui:Stop}} and {{ui:Loop}} play it in the view; the keys of each part and the camera are listed (click a key to go to its time). {{ui:Save video}} writes WebM or MP4 (where the browser can encode it) or the frames as PNG pictures in a ZIP.

The animation is kept in the same format ModelCraft's Animate workspace uses. Its curve editor and clips are ModelCraft's for now.
