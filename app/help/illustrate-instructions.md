Open the {{ui:Instructions}} section and click {{ui:Start instructions from the views}}: each view becomes a step, in the views' order.

For each step choose the {{ui:View}}, a {{ui:Title}}, the {{ui:Picture}} (as the view looks, line art, flat colours or shaded), whether to {{ui:List the parts this step shows}}, and type {{ui:Notes}}, {{ui:Warnings}} and {{ui:Tools}} (one a line). Steps move up and down; {{ui:Add step}} adds one from any view.

{{ui:A cover page}} and {{ui:A parts-list page at the end}} are on by default. {{ui:Save as PDF}} writes the pages; {{ui:Save as HTML}} writes one page that reads on a screen and prints one step a page.

## Templates

The {{ui:Template}} sets the paper, landscape or portrait, a logo (PNG or JPEG), the heading colour and a footer line. {{ui:Save template}} writes it as a `.partcraft-template.json` file to use in other manuals with {{ui:Use a template…}}.
